# Competitor Analysis: Country Landing Pages

> **Analyzed Site:** Medical Tours India - Bangladesh Landing Page
> **URL:** https://www.medicaltoursindia.com/countries/medical-tourism-india-for-bangladeshis
> **Date:** 2026-08-30
> **Purpose:** Extract best practices and identify opportunities for Khan Meditour

---

## Page Structure Analysis

### ✅ What They Do Well

#### 1. **Clear Value Proposition (Hero Section)**
- **Country-specific headline:** "Medical Tourism in India for Bangladeshi Patients"
- **Two immediate questions answered:**
  - "Is India a good option for Bangladeshi patients?"
  - "How does TechdrHealth help families from Bangladesh?"
- **Specific treatments mentioned:** Cancer care, cardiac surgery, transplants, orthopedics
- **Key benefits upfront:** Bengali/English support, visa help, competitive pricing

**Lesson for Khan Meditour:** Lead with patient questions, not company messaging

---

#### 2. **Local Currency Integration (BDT)**
- **Cost table shows:**
  - USD pricing (international standard)
  - BDT conversion (~110 BDT per USD)
  - USA comparison pricing
  - Percentage savings (89-93%)
- **Treatments with BDT pricing:**
  - Cancer Treatment: ৳330,000 – ৳2,750,000 BDT
  - Heart Surgery: ৳495,000 – ৳1,320,000 BDT
  - Liver Transplant: ৳2,750,000 – ৳4,400,000 BDT
  - Eye Surgery: ৳33,000 – ৳275,000 BDT

**Lesson for Khan Meditour:** Add local currency conversion for all countries (BDT, NGN, AED, KES, etc.)

---

#### 3. **City-Specific Guidance**
- **Section:** "Popular treatment cities for Bangladeshi patients"
- **Cities listed:** Kolkata, Hyderabad, Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai
- **Context:** "Dhaka and Chittagong have frequent air links to Kolkata, Delhi, and other Indian metros"
- **Practical advice:** Distance matters, but specialty volume matters more

**Lesson for Khan Meditour:** Add city recommendations based on:
- Flight connectivity from patient's country
- Hospital specialization (not just proximity)
- Example: "For Bangladeshi patients, Kolkata (3-hour flight) offers cardiac and oncology centers, while Bangalore (4 hours) specializes in transplants and neurosurgery"

---

#### 4. **Step-by-Step Visa Process**
- **4-step visual process:**
  1. Share medical reports
  2. Receive invitation letter
  3. Apply at Indian mission / e-visa
  4. Travel & admission
- **Country-specific details:** "Requirements can vary by mission"
- **Practical language:** "Attendant visas can be filed for family members"

**Lesson for Khan Meditour:** Create visual step-by-step visa guides per country

---

#### 5. **Cost Comparison Table**
- **Columns:**
  - Treatment name
  - India (USD)
  - Approx. local currency (BDT)
  - Typical USA cost
  - Est. savings percentage
- **Specific treatments:** Cancer, Heart Surgery, Liver Transplant, Eye Surgery
- **Disclaimer:** "Savings vs USA are estimates from mid-range package figures. Exchange rates change — request a written quote for your case."

**Lesson for Khan Meditour:** Build dynamic cost comparison tables with:
- India pricing
- Local currency conversion
- Alternative destination pricing (USA, UK, Singapore, Thailand, South Africa)
- Percentage savings
- Disclaimer about estimates

---

#### 6. **Patient Story with Country Context**
- **Quote:** "Chose liver transplant in India for Bangladeshi patients after evaluating wait times and cost versus Dhaka referrals"
- **Attribution:** Rahim H., Bangladesh · Liver Transplant
- **Link:** "More Bangladesh patient stories →"

**Lesson for Khan Meditour:** Tag testimonials with:
- Country of origin
- Treatment received
- Specific comparison (vs local hospitals or other destinations)

#### 7. **Country-Specific FAQs**

  - "Do Bangladeshi patients need a medical visa?"

### ❌ What They Could Improve (Opportunities for Khan Meditour)

#### 1. **Missing Visual Hierarchy**
- **Issue:** Text-heavy page with minimal visual breaks
- **Opportunity:** Add:
  - Hero image with Bangladeshi patient or flag
  - Icons for benefits (cost, visa, language, travel)
  - Infographics for cost comparison
  - Doctor/hospital photos
  - Video testimonials

---

#### 2. **No Doctor Profiles**
- **Issue:** No mention of specific doctors who treat Bangladeshi patients
- **Opportunity:** Add section:
  - "Doctors experienced with Bangladeshi patients"
  - Show 3-4 doctor cards with:
    - Name, specialty, experience
    - "Treated X+ Bangladeshi patients"
    - Languages spoken (Bengali, English)
    - WhatsApp consultation link

---

#### 3. **No Hospital Showcase**
- **Issue:** Cities mentioned but no specific hospital details
- **Opportunity:** Add section:
  - "Top hospitals for Bangladeshi patients"
  - Show 3-4 hospital cards with:
    - Name, location, accreditations
    - Specialties relevant to Bangladeshi patients
    - "X+ Bangladeshi patients treated here"
    - Distance from Dhaka (flight time)

---

#### 4. **Generic Patient Story**
- **Issue:** Only one testimonial, minimal detail
- **Opportunity:** Add:
  - 3-5 detailed patient stories
  - Video testimonials (higher trust)
  - Before/after photos (where appropriate)
  - Specific cost savings: "Saved ৳15 lakh vs Dhaka private hospital"

---

#### 5. **No Travel Logistics Section**
- **Issue:** Mentions "airport pickup" but no details
- **Opportunity:** Add section:
  - "Your journey from Bangladesh to India"
  - Flight options (airlines, frequency, duration)
  - Airport pickup details
  - Accommodation near hospitals
  - Local transport
  - SIM card / internet
  - Food options (Bengali cuisine, halal)

---

#### 6. **No Cultural Considerations**
- **Issue:** Doesn't address Muslim-majority country needs
- **Opportunity:** Add:
  - Halal food availability
  - Prayer facilities at hospitals
  - Ramadan considerations
  - Family accommodation (important in South Asian culture)

---

#### 7. **Weak CTA Placement**
- **Issue:** CTAs only at bottom of page
- **Opportunity:** Add CTAs:
  - In hero section (primary CTA)
  - After cost comparison table
  - After patient stories
  - Floating WhatsApp button (they have this)
  - Exit-intent popup (optional)

---

#### 8. **No Treatment-Specific Landing Pages**
- **Issue:** Generic country page, not treatment-specific
- **Opportunity:** Create:
  - `/countries/bangladesh/heart-surgery`
  - `/countries/bangladesh/cancer-treatment`
  - `/countries/bangladesh/ivf-treatment`
  - Each with country + treatment-specific content

---

#### 9. **Limited SEO Optimization**
- **Issue:** Title tag is generic, missing long-tail keywords
- **Opportunity:** Optimize:
  - **Current:** "Medical Tourism in India for Bangladeshi Patients"
  - **Better:** "Medical Treatment in India for Bangladeshi Patients | Cost, Visa, Hospitals | Khan Meditour"
  - Add more long-tail keywords in H2/H3 tags
  - Add structured data (FAQPage, MedicalWebPage)

---

#### 10. **No Multi-Language Support**
- **Issue:** English only (for Bangladeshi page)
- **Opportunity:** Add Bengali version:
  - `/bn/countries/bangladesh`
  - Full Bengali translation
  - Bengali testimonials
  - Bengali-language blog posts

## Country Metadata Additions (Based on Competitor Analysis)

```typescript
export interface CountryMetadata {
  // ... existing fields ...
  
  // NEW: Currency information
  currency: {
    code: string;           // 'BDT', 'NGN', 'AED', etc.
    symbol: string;         // '৳', '₦', 'د.إ', etc.
    exchangeRate: number;   // USD to local currency (e.g., 110 for BDT)
  };
  
  // NEW: City recommendations
  recommendedCities: Array<{
    name: string;           // 'Kolkata', 'Bangalore', etc.
    flightTime: string;     // '3 hours from Dhaka'
    directFlights: boolean;
    airlines: string[];     // ['Air India', 'IndiGo', etc.]
    specialties: string[];  // ['Cardiac', 'Oncology', 'Transplant']
    hospitalCount: number;  // Number of partner hospitals in this city
  }>;
  
  // NEW: Treatment cost comparisons
  treatmentCosts: Record<string, {
    indiaUSD: string;       // '$8,000-12,000'
    localCurrency: string;  // '৳880,000-1,320,000'
    usaCost: string;        // '$70,000-150,000'
    savingsPercent: string; // '93%'
    alternativeDestinations: Record<string, string>; // { 'Singapore': '$35,000', 'Thailand': '$15,000' }
  }>;
  
  // NEW: Cultural/religious considerations
  religiousMajority?: 'muslim' | 'christian' | 'hindu' | 'mixed';
  culturalNeeds: string[]; // ['Halal food', 'Prayer facilities', 'Family accommodation']
  
  // NEW: Travel logistics
  majorAirports: string[]; // ['Dhaka (DAC)', 'Chittagong (CGP)']
  visaOnArrival: boolean;
  eVisaAvailable: boolean;
  embassyVisaRequired: boolean;
}
```

## SEO Improvements (Based on Competitor Analysis)

### Title Tag Formula
```
[Treatment Category] in India for [Nationality] Patients | Cost, Visa, Hospitals | Khan Meditour
```

**Examples:**
- "Medical Treatment in India for Bangladeshi Patients | Cost, Visa, Hospitals | Khan Meditour"
- "Healthcare in India for Nigerian Patients | Save 50-70% | Khan Meditour"
- "Cardiac Surgery in India for UAE Patients | JCI Hospitals | Khan Meditour"

### Meta Description Formula
```
Affordable [treatment category] in India for [nationality] patients. Save [X%] with [key benefit 1], [key benefit 2], and [key benefit 3]. Free consultation.
```

**Examples:**
- "Affordable medical care in India for Bangladeshi patients. Save 60-80% with visa support, Bengali staff, and JCI hospitals. Free consultation."
- "Advanced healthcare in India for Nigerian patients. Save 50-70% with English support, visa assistance, and accredited hospitals. Get free quote."

### H2 Heading Formula (Use Long-Tail Keywords)
- "Medical treatment in India for [nationality] patients"
- "Treatment costs in India for [nationality] patients"
- "Medical visa process for [nationality] patients"
- "Popular treatment cities for [nationality] patients"
- "FAQs for [nationality] patients travelling to India"

## Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Insights

### High-Converting Elements (from competitor)
1. **Cost comparison table** - Shows immediate value
2. **Local currency conversion** - Makes pricing tangible
3. **Percentage savings** - Emotional trigger (89-93% savings)
4. **Step-by-step visa guide** - Reduces friction
5. **Patient testimonial** - Social proof
6. **WhatsApp CTA** - Low-friction contact method

1. **Video testimonials** - 2-3x higher conversion than text
2. **Live chat** - Immediate answers to questions
3. **Cost calculator** - Interactive tool for personalized estimates
4. **Urgency messaging** - "Limited slots available this month"
5. **Trust badges** - JCI, NABH, ISO certifications
6. **Doctor availability** - "Dr. [Name] available for consultation this week"
