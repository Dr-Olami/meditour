## Overview

Manipal Hospital, Old Airport Road is the flagship facility of Manipal Health Enterprises — one of India's largest and most respected healthcare networks. Established in 1991, this 600-bed quaternary care hospital has been a pioneer in super-speciality healthcare in South India for over three decades. Located on HAL Old Airport Road in the heart of Bangalore, the hospital is easily accessible from Kempegowda International Airport and major city centres.

The hospital holds the distinction of being the first **JCI-accredited** hospital in Karnataka, along with **NABH** and **NABL** accreditations. These certifications reflect the hospital's unwavering commitment to international standards in patient safety, clinical excellence, diagnostic accuracy and continuous quality improvement.

With over 1,700 doctors across 60+ specialities, Manipal Old Airport Road handles more than 3 million patients annually, making it one of the busiest and most trusted tertiary care centres in India.

## Centres of Excellence

### Cardiac Sciences

The cardiac sciences department offers comprehensive heart care including interventional cardiology, adult and paediatric cardiothoracic surgery, electrophysiology and cardiac rehabilitation. The hospital is equipped with advanced catheterisation laboratories, modular cardiac operating theatres and a dedicated **cardiac care unit (CCU)**. The team has performed thousands of successful angioplasties, bypass surgeries and valve replacements.

### Urology & Nephrology

Manipal Old Airport Road is renowned for its urology and nephrology programmes, offering kidney transplant services, dialysis, stone disease management, uro-oncology and robotic urological surgery. The hospital's kidney transplant programme is one of the most established in Karnataka with excellent outcomes.

### Gastroenterology & Hepatology

The gastroenterology department provides diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy, liver transplant services, hepatobiliary surgery, treatment for inflammatory bowel disease and complex GI conditions. The liver transplant programme has achieved outstanding success rates.

### Oncology

The cancer care unit offers medical oncology, surgical oncology, radiation oncology and haemato-oncology with bone marrow transplant services. A multidisciplinary tumour board ensures personalised treatment plans, supported by advanced imaging including PET-CT and linear accelerator (LINAC) for radiation therapy.

### Neurology & Neurosurgery

The neurosciences team specialises in stroke management, brain tumour surgery, spinal surgery, epilepsy treatment, movement disorders and interventional neurology. Advanced neuro-imaging and intra-operative monitoring technologies support precise surgical interventions.

### Orthopaedics & Joint Replacement

The orthopaedics department is known for joint replacement procedures (hip, knee and shoulder), sports medicine, arthroscopy, spine surgery and trauma care. Minimally invasive surgical techniques ensure faster recovery and better outcomes.

### Organ Transplant

Manipal Old Airport Road runs comprehensive kidney and liver transplant programmes. The transplant unit includes dedicated ICUs, HEPA-filtered operating theatres and a specialised transplant coordination team managing the entire patient journey from evaluation to lifelong post-transplant follow-up.
