## Overview

Manipal Hospital, Old Airport Road is the 600-bed flagship facility of **Manipal Health Enterprises** — one of India's largest and most respected healthcare networks, with over 12,600 beds across 49 hospitals. Established in 1991, it has been a pioneer of super-speciality healthcare in South India for over three decades and remains the **first JCI-accredited hospital in Karnataka** (accredited since 2006, before any other hospital in the state).

What makes Manipal Old Airport Road clinically distinctive is its **robotic surgery leadership**: it was the first hospital in Karnataka to introduce robotic cancer surgery in 2009 (now 6,000+ cases) and the first in South India to launch the Mazor X robotic spine surgery system. It was also the first in Bangalore to offer HIPEC and PIPAC for peritoneal surface cancers. Combined with one of India's largest multi-organ transplant programmes (heart, lung, kidney, liver, pancreas, bone marrow), this makes Manipal the strongest choice on Khan Meditour's panel for patients needing robotic oncology, robotic spine or complex transplant care.

The hospital holds the distinction of being the first **JCI-accredited** hospital in Karnataka (since 2006), along with **NABH** and **NABL** accreditations. It also holds **AAHRPP** accreditation for human research protection and was the first Indian multi-superspeciality hospital to attain **ISO 9001:2000** certification (since upgraded to **ISO 9001:2015**). These certifications reflect the hospital's unwavering commitment to international standards in patient safety, clinical excellence, diagnostic accuracy and continuous quality improvement.

With over 1,700 doctors across 60+ specialities and more than 3 million patients annually across the Manipal Hospitals network (as of 2024), Manipal Old Airport Road is one of the busiest and most trusted tertiary care centres in India.
