## Overview

Fortis Hospital, Bannerghatta Road is a multi-speciality tertiary care hospital established in 2006 as part of Fortis Healthcare Limited, one of India's leading integrated healthcare providers. Located opposite IIM Bangalore, the hospital has built a strong reputation for clinical excellence, patient safety and medical innovation over nearly two decades of service.

The hospital is accredited by **Joint Commission International (JCI)** — making it the first JCI-accredited hospital in Karnataka — as well as **NABH** (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers) and **NABL** (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories). It is the only Indian hospital to feature among the world's top five destinations for medical tourism by the **Medical Travel Quality Alliance (MTQUA)**, serving patients from over 175 countries.

With over 150 senior doctors and 800 para-medical staff across approximately 40 specialities, Fortis Bannerghatta Road delivers comprehensive super-speciality care backed by evidence-based protocols and robust clinical processes.

## Centres of Excellence

### Cardiac Sciences

The cardiac sciences department offers interventional cardiology, adult and paediatric cardiothoracic surgery, electrophysiology and cardiac rehabilitation. The hospital is equipped with advanced catheterisation laboratories, modular cardiac operating theatres and a dedicated **cardiac care unit (CCU)** with 24/7 monitoring. The dedicated cardiac rehab unit supports patients through structured recovery programmes after surgery or cardiac events.

### Oncology

Fortis Bannerghatta Road provides comprehensive cancer care including medical oncology, surgical oncology, radiation oncology and haemato-oncology with bone marrow transplant (BMT) services. The hospital's multidisciplinary tumour board ensures personalised treatment plans for each patient, supported by advanced imaging and pathology services.

### Neurology & Neurosurgery

The neurosciences team specialises in stroke management, brain tumour surgery, spinal surgery, epilepsy treatment and movement disorders. Advanced neuro-imaging and intra-operative monitoring technologies support precise surgical interventions.

### Gastroenterology & Hepatobiliary Sciences

The gastroenterology department offers diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy, liver transplant services, hepatobiliary surgery and treatment for complex GI conditions. The liver transplant programme is one of the most established in South India.

### Orthopaedics & Joint Replacement

The orthopaedics department is known for joint replacement procedures (hip, knee and shoulder), sports medicine, arthroscopy and trauma surgery. The team employs minimally invasive surgical techniques for faster recovery and better outcomes.

### Organ Transplant

Fortis Bannerghatta Road runs comprehensive transplant programmes for kidney, liver, heart, pancreas and small bowel. The transplant unit includes dedicated ICUs, HEPA-filtered operating theatres and a specialised transplant coordination team managing the entire patient journey from evaluation to post-transplant care.
