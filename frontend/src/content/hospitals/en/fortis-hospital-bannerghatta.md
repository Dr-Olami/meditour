## Overview

Fortis Hospital, Bannerghatta Road is a 284-bed multi-speciality tertiary care hospital established in 2006 and part of **Fortis Healthcare Limited**, one of India's leading integrated healthcare providers affiliated with IHH Healthcare Berhad. Located opposite IIM Bangalore, the hospital has built a strong reputation for clinical excellence, patient safety and medical innovation over nearly two decades of service.

Fortis Bannerghatta Road's defining distinction is its **unmatched accreditation record**: it holds **six consecutive JCI accreditations** since February 2008 — the most of any hospital in Bangalore — and is the **only Indian hospital to feature among the world's top five destinations for medical tourism** by the Medical Travel Quality Alliance (MTQUA, October 2014). This makes it the most internationally validated hospital on Khan Meditour's panel. Clinically, the hospital is a **robotic surgery leader**, with over 500 robotic-assisted urology procedures completed and pioneering work in robotic breast reconstruction using natural tissue — a procedure first performed in South India at this campus.

The hospital is accredited by **Joint Commission International (JCI)** since February 2008 — with six consecutive accreditations — as well as **NABH** (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers, four times renewed) and **NABL** (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories). It is the only Indian hospital to feature among the world's top five destinations for medical tourism by the **Medical Travel Quality Alliance (MTQUA)**, certified in October 2014 as the first hospital in India to receive MTQUA medical tourism certification.

With over 150 senior doctors and 800 para-medical staff across approximately 40 specialities, Fortis Bannerghatta Road delivers comprehensive super-speciality care backed by evidence-based protocols and robust clinical processes.
