## Overview

Narayana Institute of Cardiac Sciences (NICS) is a 606-bed cardiac super-speciality hospital located at NH Health City, Bommasandra, Bangalore. Commissioned in 2000 by **Dr. Devi Shetty** — one of the world's most renowned cardiac surgeons and the founder of Narayana Health — NICS is widely regarded as **one of the world's largest and highest-volume dedicated cardiac hospitals**, performing up to 60 heart surgeries per day and handling the highest cardiac surgical volume of any single facility in India.

What makes NICS globally unique is its **scale, paediatric depth and affordability model**. The hospital is equipped with 19 dedicated cardiac operation theatres (including 2 robotic) and 8 cath labs (including 1 hybrid), and treats children from over 78 countries for congenital heart disease — 40% of all surgeries at the facility are performed on children and newborns. In 2018, NICS performed the first artificial heart transplant in Asia. This combination of Dr Shetty's vision, world-class infrastructure and a pricing model designed to make heart surgery accessible to patients who cannot afford it elsewhere makes NICS the strongest cardiac referral option on Khan Meditour's panel.

The hospital is accredited by **Joint Commission International (JCI)** and **NABH**, ensuring adherence to the highest international standards in patient safety, clinical governance and quality improvement. With a team of 283 highly qualified doctors and over 1,500 healthcare professionals (as of 2024), and a capacity of 60 heart surgeries per day, NICS has become a trusted destination for patients seeking world-class cardiac treatment from across India and the world.

## Centres of Excellence

### Adult Cardiac Sciences

The adult cardiology department offers the full spectrum of interventional and surgical cardiac care including coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG), heart valve repair and replacement, angioplasty and stenting, and electrophysiology services. The hospital performs complex procedures including the Ross procedure, ventricular aneurysm repair and transcatheter aortic valve implantation (TAVI/TAVR).
