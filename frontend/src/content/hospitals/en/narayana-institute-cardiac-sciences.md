## Overview

Narayana Institute of Cardiac Sciences (NICS) is a 606-bed cardiac super-speciality hospital located at NH Health City, Bommasandra, Bangalore. Commissioned in 2000 by **Dr. Devi Shetty**, the founder of Narayana Health, NICS stands as one of the world's largest and most advanced dedicated cardiac hospitals. The facility has revolutionised heart care in India and established itself as a global leader in paediatric and adult cardiac surgery.

The hospital is accredited by **Joint Commission International (JCI)** and **NABH**, ensuring adherence to the highest international standards in patient safety, clinical governance and quality improvement. With a team of 283 highly qualified doctors and a capacity of 60 heart surgeries per day, NICS has become a trusted destination for patients seeking world-class cardiac treatment from across India and the world.

## Centres of Excellence

### Adult Cardiac Sciences

The adult cardiology department offers the full spectrum of interventional and surgical cardiac care including coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG), heart valve repair and replacement, angioplasty and stenting, and electrophysiology services. The hospital performs complex procedures including the Ross procedure, ventricular aneurysm repair and transcatheter aortic valve implantation (TAVI/TAVR).

### Paediatric Cardiac Sciences

NICS is globally renowned for its paediatric cardiac programme, housing the **world's largest dedicated paediatric cardiac ICU with 80 beds**. The hospital provides cardiac treatment for neonatal, paediatric and adult patients, specialising in congenital heart conditions including pulmonary atresia, tetralogy of Fallot and complex congenital anomalies.

### Heart & Lung Transplant

The transplant programme at NICS covers heart and lung transplants, supported by a dedicated transplant ICU, experienced transplant surgeons and a comprehensive post-transplant care protocol. The hospital also maintains a homograft and valve banking unit for transplant procedures.

### Electrophysiology & Arrhythmia Management

The **Electrophysiology Centre** specialises in diagnosing and treating heart rhythm disorders (arrhythmias). Services include catheter ablation, pacemaker implantation, ICD implantation and cardiac resynchronisation therapy (CRT).

## Advanced Technology & Infrastructure

- **606 beds** across cardiac specialities — one of the largest heart hospitals in the world
- **16 dedicated cardiac operation theatres** with advanced surgical technology
- **6 digital catheterisation laboratories** including one **hybrid cath lab** for combined interventional and surgical procedures
- **80-bed Paediatric Cardiac ICU** — the largest in the world
- **200 critical care beds** for post-operative cardiac care
- State-of-the-art **Cardiac Critical Care Unit (CCU)**
- **NABL-accredited laboratory** services
- **Blood bank** and transfusion services
- Advanced **cardiac imaging** including 2D Echo, CT scan, MRI, DSA lab and Holter monitoring
- **Homograft and valve banking unit** for transplant procedures
- Dedicated **cardiac rehabilitation centre**

## Specialised Clinics

NICS offers a range of specialised clinics to cater to various cardiac conditions:

- **Arrhythmia Centre** — dedicated to diagnosing and treating irregular heartbeats
- **Heart Failure Clinic** — specialised care for patients with heart failure
- **Lifestyle Modification Clinic** — programs to help patients adopt healthier habits
- **Valve Repair Clinic** — focused on heart valve repair and management
- **Adult Congenital Heart Diseases Clinic** — comprehensive care for adults with congenital heart conditions
- **Fetal Cardiac Echo** — advanced imaging for prenatal assessment of heart conditions
