## Overview

SPARSH Hospital, Hennur Road is a 300-bed quaternary care facility and the newest addition to the SPARSH Group of Hospitals, one of Karnataka's most respected healthcare networks. Inaugurated in 2025, the hospital is designed to serve every healthcare need — from everyday wellness to the most complex medical interventions — with cutting-edge technology, a highly experienced clinical team and compassion at its core.

The hospital is **NABH accredited**, ensuring adherence to national standards in patient safety, clinical governance and quality improvement. As a quaternary care centre, SPARSH Hennur offers advanced treatments across 50+ speciality departments, making it one of the most comprehensive healthcare facilities in North Bangalore.

With over 2,500 direct and indirect employment opportunities generated, the hospital also contributes to academic medicine and research in the region. SPARSH Group plans to scale to nine hospitals with over 1,700 beds by the end of 2025.

## Centres of Excellence

### Orthopaedics & Joint Replacement

Carrying forward the SPARSH legacy of excellence in orthopaedics, the Hennur campus specialises in joint replacement surgeries (hip, knee and shoulder), sports medicine, spine surgery, trauma care and arthroscopy. The team employs advanced minimally invasive techniques and robotic-assisted surgery for precision and faster recovery.

### Cardiac Sciences

The cardiac sciences department offers interventional cardiology, cardiac surgery, heart transplant services and cardiac rehabilitation. The hospital is equipped with a state-of-the-art **cath lab** for advanced cardiac care, dedicated cardiac ICU and modular cardiac operating theatres.

### Oncology

SPARSH Hennur provides comprehensive cancer care powered by advanced technology and multidisciplinary expertise. The hospital features **radiation oncology (LINAC)** and high-dose therapy units, along with medical and surgical oncology services. The **Bone Marrow Transplant (BMT) suites** are HEPA-filtered for infection control, ensuring the highest safety standards for transplant patients.

### Neurosciences

The neurosciences team covers neurology, neurosurgery, interventional neurology and stroke management. Advanced neuro-imaging and neuro-interventional radiology capabilities support precise diagnosis and minimally invasive treatment of complex brain and spinal conditions.

### Gastroenterology

The gastroenterology department offers medical and surgical gastroenterology, including bariatric endoscopy and nutrition, therapeutic endoscopy and treatment for complex GI and liver conditions.
