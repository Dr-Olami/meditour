## Overview

Apollo Hospitals, Bannerghatta Road is a 350-bed multi-speciality tertiary care facility and the Bangalore flagship of the **Apollo Hospitals Group** — India's largest and most geographically widespread healthcare chain, serving 150+ countries. Established in 2007 opposite the prestigious Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Bangalore, the hospital spans over 212,000 square feet of purpose-built clinical infrastructure and has become a destination of choice for international patients seeking advanced cardiac, oncology, transplant and robotic-surgery care.

What sets Apollo Bannerghatta apart is its **unique technology stack**: it is the only hospital in Bangalore to offer **CyberKnife** robotic radiosurgery (non-invasive tumour treatment for brain, spine, lung, liver and pancreas), **Da Vinci Xi** robotic surgery, and a comprehensive **multi-organ transplant programme** (kidney, liver, pancreas and multi-organ) under one roof. This combination — precision oncology, robotic surgery and transplantation — is unavailable elsewhere in the city and makes the hospital a single referral point for complex multi-system disease.

The hospital is accredited by the **Joint Commission International (JCI)** — the global gold standard for healthcare quality — and the **National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH)**, ensuring adherence to the highest international protocols in patient safety, clinical excellence and infection control.

With over 700 daily OPD visits and 500+ international patient consultations every month (per Apollo Hospitals official site, as of 2024), Apollo Hospitals Bannerghatta Road has earned a reputation as one of South India's most trusted healthcare institutions. The hospital has treated over 70,000 international patients (cumulative) from across the world.
