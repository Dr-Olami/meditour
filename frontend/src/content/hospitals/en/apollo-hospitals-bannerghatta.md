## Overview

Apollo Hospitals, Bannerghatta Road is a 250-bed multi-speciality tertiary care facility established in 2007 as part of the Apollo Hospitals Group, India's largest healthcare chain. Located opposite the prestigious Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Bangalore, the hospital spans over 212,000 square feet of purpose-built clinical infrastructure and serves as a flagship destination for both domestic and international patients.

The hospital is accredited by the **Joint Commission International (JCI)** — the global gold standard for healthcare quality — and the **National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH)**, ensuring adherence to the highest international protocols in patient safety, clinical excellence and infection control.

With over 700 daily OPD visits and 500+ international patient consultations every month, Apollo Hospitals Bannerghatta Road has earned a reputation as one of South India's most trusted healthcare institutions. The hospital has treated over 70,000 international patients from across the world.

## Centres of Excellence

### Cardiac Sciences

The Apollo Heart Institute at Bannerghatta Road offers comprehensive cardiac care including interventional cardiology, cardiac surgery, electrophysiology and heart transplant services. The institute is equipped with dedicated catheterisation laboratories for angioplasty and stenting, modular cardiac operating theatres, and an advanced cardiac critical care unit (CCU) for post-operative monitoring.

### Oncology

The cancer care unit provides the full spectrum of treatment modalities — medical oncology, surgical oncology and radiation oncology — under one roof. The hospital is equipped with **CyberKnife** robotic radiosurgery and **IMRT** (Intensity-Modulated Radiation Therapy) for precision tumour targeting with minimal damage to surrounding healthy tissue. A dedicated chemotherapy day care unit and onco-pathology lab support comprehensive cancer management.

### Orthopaedics & Joint Replacement

The orthopaedics department specialises in joint replacement surgeries (hip, knee and shoulder), sports medicine, spine surgery and trauma care. The team employs the **Da Vinci Robotic Surgical System** for minimally invasive procedures, enabling faster recovery, smaller incisions and reduced hospital stays.

### Organ Transplant

Apollo Hospitals Bannerghatta Road runs a comprehensive transplant programme covering kidney, liver and heart transplants. The facility includes a dedicated transplant ICU, HEPA-filtered modular operating theatres and a dedicated transplant coordination team that manages donor workup, recipient preparation and lifelong post-transplant follow-up.

### Neurosciences

The neurosciences institute brings together neurology, neurosurgery, stroke management and rehabilitation. Advanced neuro-imaging including 3 Tesla MRI and 64-slice CT angiogram supports precise diagnosis of complex brain and spinal conditions.

## International Patient Services

Apollo Hospitals Bannerghatta Road is a preferred destination for medical tourists from over 120 countries. The dedicated **International Patient Desk** provides end-to-end assistance including:

- Multilingual coordinators fluent in **Arabic, Russian, Bengali** and English
- Visa invitation letters and travel documentation support
- Airport pickup and drop services
- Accommodation arrangements for patients and families
- International cuisine and dietary customisation
- Foreign currency exchange and SIM card assistance
- Medical records transfer and post-operative teleconsultation
- Interpreter services for over 15 languages

The hospital handles 500+ international patient consultations monthly, making it one of the busiest international patient centres in South India.
