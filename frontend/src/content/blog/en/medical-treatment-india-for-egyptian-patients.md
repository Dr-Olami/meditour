Egyptian families increasingly travel to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment, drawn by JCI-accredited hospitals and costs well below private options in the region. Cairo and Alexandria connect to India through direct EgyptAir and Air India services as well as one-stop routes via Dubai, and the e-medical visa processes in under a week. This guide covers costs in Egyptian Pounds, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Cairo, Alexandria or Giza.

## Why Egyptian patients choose India

- **40–60% cost savings** compared with Turkey, Germany and Jordan
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments
- **Arabic and English speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **Direct flights from Cairo** — EgyptAir and Air India fly Cairo–Mumbai nonstop in around 6 hours; one-stop routes via Dubai reach Bangalore and Delhi
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with an EGP reference for planning

## Medical visa from Egypt: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Egypt requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-medical visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Cairo to India for treatment

- **EgyptAir direct to Mumbai** — nonstop, around 6 hours; the simplest option for cancer, cardiac and cosmetic cases centred in Mumbai
- **Air India direct to Mumbai** — nonstop, around 6 hours; convenient scheduling for families from Cairo and Alexandria
- **Emirates via Dubai** — one-stop into Bangalore or Delhi in about 6.5 hours, with comfortable connections

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Egyptian patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Egyptian families
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Egyptian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic — with the direct Cairo flight), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does heart surgery cost in India vs Egypt?**
Cardiac surgery packages in India run $4,500–12,000 (£216,000–576,000) — typically 40–60% less than comparable private care abroad, at JCI-accredited hospitals.

**Is there a direct flight from Cairo to India?**
Yes — EgyptAir and Air India fly Cairo to Mumbai nonstop in about 6 hours; Emirates and other carriers connect one-stop to Bangalore and Delhi via Dubai.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from Egypt?**
The e-medical visa typically processes in 5–7 business days once the hospital invitation letter is issued.

**Can my relative stay with me in the hospital?**
Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Egypt?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Egyptian physician on request.
