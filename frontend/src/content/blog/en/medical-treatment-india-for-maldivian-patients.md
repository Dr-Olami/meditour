Maldivian families have travelled to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and transplant treatment for years. With Male connected to Bangalore, Chennai and Kochi by direct flights in roughly 2 hours and visa-on-arrival available for Maldivian citizens, the route is one of the most accessible for patients from the Maldives. This guide covers the costs in Rufiyaa, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Male, Addu City or Fuvahmulah.

## Why Maldivian patients choose India

- **50–70% cost savings** compared with private care in Singapore, Sri Lanka or Thailand
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments experienced with Maldivian families
- **Dhivehi and English-speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **A direct flight option** — IndiGo and Air India fly Male–Bangalore and Male–Chennai nonstop in about 2 hours
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with a Rufiyaa reference for planning

## Medical visa from the Maldives: requirements and process

The medical visa India from the Maldives requirements follow the visa-on-arrival pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — recommended for smooth admission and treatment planning
3. **Travel to India (visa on arrival)** — Maldivian citizens receive visa-on-arrival for up to 30 days; carry your passport, medical documents and the invitation letter
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Male to India for treatment

- **IndiGo direct to Bangalore** — nonstop, around 2 hours; the simplest option for transplant, cardiac and orthopedic cases centred in Bangalore
- **Air India direct to Chennai** — nonstop, around 2 hours; ideal for cardiac, cancer and orthopedic treatment in Chennai
- **IndiGo direct to Kochi** — nonstop, around 1.5 hours; convenient for cardiac, general surgery and orthopedic cases

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Maldivian patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Maldivian families and their documentation
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Maldivian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (2h from Male, direct — transplant, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Chennai** (2h, direct — cardiac, cancer, orthopedics), **Kochi** (1.5h, direct — cardiac, general surgery, orthopedics).

## Top treatments Maldivian patients travel for

1. **Cardiac surgery** — bypass and valve procedures at 50–70% below regional private pricing
2. **Orthopedic treatment** — knee and hip replacements, spine surgery
3. **Cancer care** — full protocols including radiation and surgical oncology
4. **Kidney transplants** — dedicated programmes with international coordinators
5. **Fertility treatment** — IVF cycles planned around a 2–3 week stay

## How to get started

1. Gather recent medical reports, prescriptions and imaging
2. Send them for a **free opinion and written cost estimate** — you receive a hospital shortlist matched to your case
3. Confirm the hospital and receive the invitation letter
4. Travel to India on visa-on-arrival (30 days); file attendant visas for family
5. Book the direct Male–Bangalore, Male–Chennai or Male–Kochi flight
6. Fly, get admitted, and recover while the coordinator manages logistics

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does heart surgery cost in India vs the Maldives?**
Cardiac surgery packages in India run $4,500–12,000 (Rf67,500–180,000) — typically 50–70% less than comparable private care abroad, at JCI-accredited hospitals.

**Is there a direct flight from Male to India?**
Yes — IndiGo and Air India fly Male to Bangalore, Chennai and Kochi nonstop in 1.5–2 hours.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from the Maldives?**
Maldivian citizens receive visa-on-arrival for up to 30 days — no prior visa application is required, though carrying the hospital invitation letter is recommended.

**Can my relative stay with me in the hospital?**
Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to the Maldives?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Maldivian physician on request.
