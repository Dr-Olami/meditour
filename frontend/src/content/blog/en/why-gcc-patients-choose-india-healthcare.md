The Gulf has excellent hospitals — but for many elective and major procedures, out-of-pocket costs in Dubai, Riyadh or Doha push families to look elsewhere. India has quietly become the region's most practical healthcare destination: 3 to 5 hours by direct flight, internationally accredited hospitals, and package prices that are 40–70% lower than Gulf private care. This guide covers the numbers, the visa rules that differ by nationality, and what to look for in a hospital.

## Why the Gulf travels to India for treatment

- **40–60% savings vs UAE private hospitals** and 50–70% vs care in Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman and Bahrain — cardiac surgery priced at $4,500–12,000 in India often exceeds $35,000–50,000 in Singapore and far more in Gulf private facilities
- **Short, direct flights** — 3.5 hours from Dubai, 4.5 from Riyadh, 4 from both Kuwait City and Doha, 3 from Muscat, 3.5 from Manama
- **Arabic-speaking coordinators** and halal-conscious hospital services at major international patient departments
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** — India has one of the world's largest concentrations of Joint Commission International accredited facilities
- **Visa simplicity for Emiratis** — UAE nationals receive visa on arrival, making India one of the easiest medical trips in the region to plan on short notice

## Visa rules by GCC nationality

- **UAE nationals:** medical visa on arrival in India — carry your passport, medical reports and hospital appointment letter
- **Saudi, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain nationals:** e-visa, typically processed in **3–5 business days**, supported by a hospital invitation letter
- **Residents of any GCC country holding other passports:** e-visa processing times vary by nationality; we confirm the current checklist for your passport when you send reports

The process for e-visa nationals follows four steps: share medical reports, receive the hospital invitation letter, apply online with documents, and fly once approved. Attendant visas can be filed for family members.

## Choosing a hospital: what actually matters

An Arabic speaking hospital in Bangalore or Mumbai is not a marketing claim — major international patient departments employ Arabic-language coordinators, and halal hospital services for UAE patients and wider GCC families (halal meals, prayer rooms, gender-aware care where requested) are standard at partner hospitals. When shortlisting, verify:

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — non-negotiable for major surgery
2. **International patient department** with Arabic-language support
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your specific procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Package pricing in writing** — admission, procedure, hospital stay and discharge medication, itemised
5. **Post-return follow-up** — telemedicine consults after you fly home

## Recommended cities for GCC patients

- **Bangalore** — India's transplant, cardiac and orthopedics hub; 3.5 hours from Dubai, 5 from Riyadh
- **Mumbai** — strong for cancer, cardiac and cosmetic surgery; 3 hours from Dubai, 4.5 from Riyadh
