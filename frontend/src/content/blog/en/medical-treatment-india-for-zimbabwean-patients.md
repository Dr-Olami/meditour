Zimbabwean patients travel to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment — procedures that are either unavailable locally or priced well beyond reach in private regional hospitals. With connecting flights from Harare through Addis Ababa or Nairobi, and an e-medical visa processing in under a week, the route is practical for patients from Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru and across Zimbabwe. This guide covers the costs in Zimbabwean Dollars, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Harare.

- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with a Z$ reference for planning

## Medical visa from Zimbabwe: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Zimbabwe requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

Plan for the visa timeline when booking flights — applying a month before your intended travel date avoids re-booking fees. Carry physical copies of all medical reports; both the airline and the hospital international desk may ask for them.

## How to travel from Harare to India for treatment

There are no direct flights from Zimbabwe to India, but the one-stop routes are reliable:

- **Ethiopian Airlines via Addis Ababa** — daily departures from Harare; total journey around 9–10 hours to Mumbai, Delhi or Bangalore
- **Kenya Airways via Nairobi** — comfortable connection into Mumbai and Delhi
- **Emirates via Dubai** — strong schedule into Bangalore, Mumbai or Delhi

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Zimbabwean patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Zimbabwean families and their documentation
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Zimbabwean physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics).
