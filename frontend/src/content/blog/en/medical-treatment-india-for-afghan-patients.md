Afghan families have travelled to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and trauma treatment for years. With Kabul connected to Delhi by direct flights in roughly 2.5 hours and the Indian e-medical visa processing in under a week, the route is one of the most practical options for patients from Afghanistan. This guide covers the costs in Afghani, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Kabul, Herat, Kandahar or Mazar-i-Sharif.

## Why Afghan patients choose India

- **70–85% cost savings** compared with private care in Pakistan, Turkey or Iran
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments experienced with Afghan families
- **Dari, Pashto and English-speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **A direct flight option** — Kam Air flies Kabul–Delhi nonstop in about 2.5 hours; connecting routes reach Bangalore and Mumbai
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with an Afghani reference for planning

## Medical visa from Afghanistan: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Afghanistan requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-medical visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Kabul to India for treatment

- **Kam Air direct to Delhi** — nonstop, around 2.5 hours; the simplest option for cardiac, cancer and trauma cases centred in Delhi
- **Air India via Delhi** — direct service into Delhi with onward connections to Bangalore and Mumbai
- **Ariana Afghan Airlines** — scheduled flights into Delhi with connecting options to Mumbai

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Afghan patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Afghan families and their documentation
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Afghan physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Delhi** (2h from Kabul, direct — cardiac, cancer, trauma, orthopedics), **Bangalore** (4h, connecting via Delhi — transplant, cardiac, cancer), **Mumbai** (3h, direct via Kam Air — cancer, cardiac, orthopedics).

## Top treatments Afghan patients travel for

1. **Cardiac surgery** — bypass and valve procedures at 70–85% below regional private pricing
2. **Orthopedic treatment** — knee and hip replacements, spine surgery and trauma reconstruction
3. **Cancer care** — full protocols including radiation and surgical oncology
4. **Kidney transplants** — dedicated programmes with international coordinators

## How to get started

1. Gather recent medical reports, prescriptions and imaging
2. Send them for a **free opinion and written cost estimate** — you receive a hospital shortlist matched to your case
3. Confirm the hospital and receive the invitation letter
4. Apply for the e-medical visa (5–7 business days); file attendant visas for family
5. Book the direct Kabul–Delhi flight or a connecting route to Bangalore or Mumbai
6. Fly, get admitted, and recover while the coordinator manages logistics
