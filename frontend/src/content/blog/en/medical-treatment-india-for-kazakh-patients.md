With major cities like Almaty, Astana, Shymkent and Karaganda offering one-stop flight connections of 5–6 hours, and a straightforward Indian e-visa process, Kazakh families can access cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment with Russian and Kazakh language support throughout.

## Medical visa from Kazakhstan: requirements and process

The medical visa india from Kazakhstan requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Almaty to India for treatment

- **Air Astana** — one-stop via Delhi, around 5–6 hours; daily flights with strong medical assistance
- **IndiGo** — one-stop via Delhi, around 6.5 hours; competitive fares and convenient connections
- **Air Astana through Delhi to Mumbai** — one-stop via Delhi, around 6 hours; good for western India hospitals

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Kazakh patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Kazakh families
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Kazakh physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (Transplant/Cardiac/Orthopedics/Cancer), **Delhi** (Cardiac/Cancer/Orthopedics), **Mumbai** (Cancer/Cardiac/Cosmetic).
