Jordanian families increasingly travel to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment, drawn by JCI-accredited hospitals and costs well below private options in the region. Amman, Zarqa and Irbid connect to India through one-stop routes via Dubai, and the e-medical visa processes in under a week. This guide covers costs in Jordanian Dinars, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Amman and other major cities.

## Why Jordanian patients choose India

- **40–60% cost savings** compared with Turkey, Germany and Jordan
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments
- **Arabic and English speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **One-stop flights via Dubai** — connecting routes from Amman reach Delhi in about 5.5 hours, Mumbai in 6 and Bangalore in 7
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with a JOD reference for planning

## Medical visa from Jordan: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Jordan requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-medical visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Amman to India for treatment

- **Emirates via Dubai** — one-stop into Delhi in about 5.5 hours, Mumbai in 6 and Bangalore in 7; the most common route for Jordanian families
- **Flydubai via Dubai** — one-stop into Delhi and Mumbai; competitive fares from Amman
- **Qatar Airways via Doha** — comfortable connections into Delhi and Mumbai

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Jordanian patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Jordanian families
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Jordanian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does heart surgery cost in India vs Jordan?**
Cardiac surgery packages in India run $4,500–12,000 (د.ا3,195–8,520) — typically 40–60% less than comparable private care abroad, at JCI-accredited hospitals.

**Is there a direct flight from Amman to India?**
No — there are no direct flights from Amman to India, but one-stop routes via Dubai reach Delhi in about 5.5 hours, Mumbai in 6 and Bangalore in 7.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from Jordan?**
The e-medical visa typically processes in 5–7 business days once the hospital invitation letter is issued.

**Can my relative stay with me in the hospital?**
Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Jordan?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Jordanian physician on request.
