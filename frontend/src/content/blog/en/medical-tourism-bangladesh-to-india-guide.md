## Medical visa from Bangladesh: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Bangladesh requirements are straightforward when the paperwork is prepared in advance:

1. **Share your medical reports** — send scans and prescriptions via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — this letter confirms your appointment and is required by the e-visa portal
3. **Apply for the e-medical visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **3–5 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members travelling with the patient

Two practical notes: apply for an e-medical visa (not a regular tourist visa) so hospital admission and any extensions are smooth, and carry physical copies of all reports — port health desks occasionally ask for them.

## Choosing the right city and hospital

Bengali-speaking hospital support matters most in cities with established Bangladeshi patient flows:

- **Kolkata** — 1 hour from Dhaka, direct flights on Biman Bangladesh, IndiGo and Air India; strongest for cardiac, oncology and neurosurgery; culturally the easiest transition
- **Bangalore** — around 3 hours from Dhaka with direct flights; India's transplant and orthopedics hub with large international patient departments
- **Chennai** — around 3 hours via IndiGo or SriLankan; known for cardiac, oncology and orthopedics

When comparing hospitals, look for JCI or NABH accreditation, a dedicated international desk, published success rates for your specific procedure, and a coordinator who speaks Bengali.
