Kenyan families have travelled to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment for years. With Nairobi's Jomo Kenyatta International Airport connected to India — including a direct Kenya Airways service to Mumbai — and e-medical visas processing in under a week, the route is more practical than many assume. This guide covers the costs in Kenyan Shillings, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Nairobi, Mombasa or Kisumu. Neighbouring patients from Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda follow the same pathway via Nairobi.

## Why Kenyan patients choose India

- **50–70% cost savings** compared with private care in Kenya or medical travel to South Africa or the UK
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments that have served East African families for decades
- **English-speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **A direct flight option** — Kenya Airways flies Nairobi–Mumbai nonstop in about 6 hours; one-stop routes via Addis Ababa reach Bangalore and Delhi in 7
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with a Kenyan Shilling reference for planning

## Medical visa from Kenya: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Kenya requirements follow the standard e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-medical visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

Patients from Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda follow the same steps, usually connecting through Nairobi or Addis Ababa.

## How to travel from Nairobi to India for treatment

- **Kenya Airways direct to Mumbai** — nonstop, around 6 hours; the simplest option for cancer, cardiac and cosmetic cases centred in Mumbai
- **Ethiopian Airlines via Addis Ababa** — one-stop into Bangalore or Delhi in about 7 hours
- **Qatar Airways via Doha** — comfortable connections into Bangalore and Delhi

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Kenyan patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with East African families and their documentation
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Kenyan physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic — with the direct Nairobi flight), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics).

## How to get started

1. Gather recent medical reports, prescriptions and imaging
2. Send them for a **free opinion and written cost estimate** — you receive a hospital shortlist matched to your case
3. Confirm the hospital and receive the invitation letter
4. Apply for the e-medical visa (5–7 business days); file attendant visas for family
5. Book the direct Nairobi–Mumbai flight or a one-stop route via Addis Ababa
6. Fly, get admitted, and recover while the coordinator manages logistics

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does heart surgery cost in India vs Kenya?**
Cardiac surgery packages in India run $4,500–12,000 (KSh 580K–1.5M) — typically 50–70% less than comparable private care abroad, at JCI-accredited hospitals.

**Is there a direct flight from Nairobi to India?**
Yes — Kenya Airways flies Nairobi to Mumbai nonstop in about 6 hours; Ethiopian and Qatar Airways connect one-stop to Bangalore and Delhi.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from Kenya?**

Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Kenya?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Kenyan physician on request.
