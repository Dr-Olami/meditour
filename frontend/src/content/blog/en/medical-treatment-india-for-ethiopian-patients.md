Ethiopian patients travel to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment — procedures that are either unavailable locally or carry long waiting lists at home. A major advantage for Ethiopian families is the direct Ethiopian Airlines service from Addis Ababa to Mumbai, Delhi and Bangalore, cutting travel time to around 6 hours. With an e-medical visa processing in under a week, the route is practical for patients from Dire Dawa, Gondar, Mekelle and across Ethiopia. This guide covers the costs in Ethiopian Birr, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Addis Ababa.

## Why Ethiopian patients choose India

- **50–70% cost savings** compared with South Africa, Thailand or Turkey
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments
- **Amharic and English speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **Direct flights from Addis Ababa** — Ethiopian Airlines flies nonstop to Mumbai, Delhi and Bangalore in about 6 hours
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with a Br reference for planning

## Medical visa from Ethiopia: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Ethiopia requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

Ethiopian cuisine options and fasting accommodation are available at major international patient departments — mention dietary requirements in advance so the coordinator can arrange them.

## How to travel from Addis Ababa to India for treatment

Ethiopia is one of the few African countries with direct flights to India:

- **Ethiopian Airlines direct to Mumbai** — nonstop, around 6 hours; the simplest option for cancer, cardiac and cosmetic cases centred in Mumbai
- **Ethiopian Airlines direct to Delhi** — nonstop, around 6.5 hours; ideal for cardiac, cancer and orthopedics cases
- **Ethiopian Airlines direct to Bangalore** — nonstop, around 6.5 hours; the preferred route for transplants, cardiac and orthopedics

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Ethiopian patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Ethiopian families and dietary accommodation
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Ethiopian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics).
