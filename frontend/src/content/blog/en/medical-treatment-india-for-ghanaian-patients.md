Ghanaian patients travel to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment — procedures that are either unavailable locally or priced well beyond reach in private regional hospitals. With connecting flights from Accra through Addis Ababa or Dubai, and an e-medical visa processing in under a week, the route is practical for patients from Kumasi, Tamale, Takoradi and across Ghana. This guide covers the costs in Ghanaian Cedis, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Accra.

## Why Ghanaian patients choose India

- **50–70% cost savings** compared with South Africa, the UK or Turkey
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments
- **English speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **Connecting flights from Accra** — one stop via Ethiopian Airlines or Emirates, around 9–10 hours
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with a ₵ reference for planning

## Medical visa from Ghana: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Ghana requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

Plan for the visa timeline when booking flights — applying a month before your intended travel date avoids re-booking fees. Carry physical copies of all medical reports; both the airline and the hospital international desk may ask for them.

## How to travel from Accra to India for treatment

There are no direct flights from Ghana to India, but the one-stop routes are reliable:

- **Ethiopian Airlines via Addis Ababa** — daily departures from Accra; total journey around 9–10 hours to Mumbai, Delhi or Bangalore
- **Emirates via Dubai** — comfortable connection into Bangalore, Mumbai or Delhi
- **Qatar Airways via Doha** — strong schedule into Mumbai and Delhi

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Ghanaian patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Ghanaian families and their documentation
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Ghanaian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics).
