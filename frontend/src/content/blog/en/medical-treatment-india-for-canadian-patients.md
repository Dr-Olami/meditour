Patients from Canada increasingly travel to India for medical treatment, seeking timely care that sidesteps long provincial healthcare wait times. With major cities like Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary and Ottawa offering one-to-two-stop flight connections of 14–18 hours, and a straightforward Indian e-visa process, Canadian families can access cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment at 60–80% below private costs in Canada and the USA.

## Why Canadian patients choose India

- **60–80% cost savings** compared with Canada, USA and Thailand
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments
- **English and French speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **14–18 hours, 1–2 stops** — connecting flights via Dubai, Frankfurt or direct Air India routes
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with a CAD reference for planning

## Medical visa from Canada: requirements and process

The medical visa india from Canada requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **3–5 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Toronto to India for treatment

- **Emirates** — one-stop via Dubai, around 16–18 hours; premium economy available for comfort
- **Lufthansa** — one-stop via Frankfurt, around 15–17 hours; strong medical assistance services
- **Air India** — one-stop or direct routes, around 15 hours; convenient for families with baggage

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Canadian patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Canadian families
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Canadian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (Transplant/Cardiac/Orthopedics/Cancer), **Mumbai** (Cancer/Cardiac/Cosmetic), **Delhi** (Cardiac/Cancer/Orthopedics).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does heart surgery cost in India vs Canada?**
Cardiac surgery packages in India run $4,500–12,000 (C$6,120–16,320) — typically 60–80% less than comparable private care abroad, at JCI-accredited hospitals.

**Is there a direct flight from Toronto to India?**
No direct flights from most Canadian cities — one-to-two-stop routes via Dubai, Frankfurt, or connecting Air India services.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from Canada?**
The e-visa typically processes in 3–5 business days once the hospital invitation letter is issued.

**Can my relative stay with me in the hospital?**
Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Canada?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Canadian physician on request.
