Iranian families increasingly travel to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment, drawn by JCI-accredited hospitals and costs well below private options in the region. Tehran, Mashhad, Isfahan and Shiraz connect to India through one-stop routes via Dubai and Sharjah, and the embassy medical visa processes in 7–10 business days. This guide covers costs in Iranian Rials, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Tehran and other major cities.

## Why Iranian patients choose India

- **30–50% cost savings** compared with Turkey, UAE and Germany
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments
- **Persian, Farsi and English speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **One-stop flights via Dubai and Sharjah** — connecting routes from Tehran reach Delhi in about 4 hours, Mumbai in 5 and Bangalore in 6
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with an IRR reference for planning

## Medical visa from Iran: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Iran requirements follow the embassy visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply at the Indian embassy** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **7–10 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Tehran to India for treatment

- **Emirates via Dubai** — one-stop into Delhi in about 4 hours, Mumbai in 5 and Bangalore in 6; the most common route for Iranian families
- **Air Arabia via Sharjah** — one-stop into Bangalore and Mumbai; competitive fares from Tehran, Mashhad and Isfahan
- **Qatar Airways via Doha** — comfortable connections into Delhi and Mumbai

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Iranian patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Iranian families
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Iranian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics).

No — there are no direct flights from Tehran to India, but one-stop routes via Dubai and Sharjah reach Delhi in about 4 hours, Mumbai in 5 and Bangalore in 6.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from Iran?**
The embassy medical visa typically processes in 7–10 business days once the hospital invitation letter is issued.

Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Iran?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Iranian physician on request.
