Patients from Australia increasingly travel to India for medical treatment, seeking high-quality care at a fraction of private hospital costs back home. With major cities like Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth and Adelaide offering one-stop flight connections of 12–15 hours, and a straightforward Indian e-visa process, Australian families can access cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment without the long waits many face under Medicare.

## Why Australian patients choose India

- **50–70% cost savings** compared with Australia, Thailand and Singapore
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments
- **English speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **12–15 hours, one stop** — connecting flights via Singapore, Kuala Lumpur or Doha
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with an AUD reference for planning

## Medical visa from Australia: requirements and process

The medical visa india from Australia requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **3–5 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Sydney to India for treatment

- **Singapore Airlines** — one-stop via Singapore, around 12–13 hours; excellent medical assistance services
- **Qatar Airways** — one-stop via Doha, around 14–15 hours; comfortable layover lounges
- **Malaysia Airlines** — one-stop via Kuala Lumpur, around 13 hours; competitive fares for families

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Australian patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Australian families
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Australian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (Transplant/Cardiac/Orthopedics/Cancer), **Mumbai** (Cancer/Cardiac/Cosmetic), **Chennai** (Cardiac/Orthopedics/Cancer).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does heart surgery cost in India vs Australia?**
Cardiac surgery packages in India run $4,500–12,000 (A$6,840–18,240) — typically 50–70% less than comparable private care abroad, at JCI-accredited hospitals.

**Is there a direct flight from Sydney to India?**
No direct flights — one-stop routes via Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, or Doha.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from Australia?**
The e-visa typically processes in 3–5 business days once the hospital invitation letter is issued.

**Can my relative stay with me in the hospital?**
Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Australia?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Australian physician on request.
