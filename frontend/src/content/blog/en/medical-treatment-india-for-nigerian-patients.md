Nigerian patients have travelled to India for serious medical care for decades — for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer treatment and kidney transplants that are either priced far beyond reach at home or have long waiting lists. The route from Lagos and Abuja is well established, the medical visa process is documented, and package pricing is transparent. This guide covers the practical details Nigerian families ask about most.

## Why Nigerian patients choose India

- **50–70% cost savings** compared with private options in Nigeria or medical travel to South Africa, the UK or the USA
- **Internationally accredited hospitals** with dedicated international patient departments that have served West African patients for years
- **English-speaking doctors and coordinators** — no language barrier from consultation through discharge
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with an approximate Naira reference for planning
- **Established travel routes** from Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt and Kano via Ethiopian Airlines, Emirates (via Dubai) or Qatar Airways (via Doha)

## Medical visa from Nigeria: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Nigeria requirements follow the standard e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-medical visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

Plan for the visa timeline when booking flights — applying a month before your intended travel date avoids re-booking fees. Carry physical copies of all medical reports; both the airline and the hospital international desk may ask for them.

## How to travel from Lagos to India for treatment

There are no direct flights from Nigeria to India, but the one-stop routes are reliable:

- **Ethiopian Airlines via Addis Ababa** — daily departures from Lagos and Abuja; total journey around 8–10 hours to Mumbai, Delhi or Bangalore
- **Emirates via Dubai** — comfortable connection into Bangalore, Mumbai or Delhi
- **Qatar Airways via Doha** — strong schedule into Bangalore and Delhi

Budget 9–10 hours door to door from Lagos, plus airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Families usually fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the best hospital in India for Nigerians

The best hospital in India for Nigerians is the one accredited for your specific procedure with a proven international patient flow:

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for any major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with West African patients and their documentation
3. **Procedure-specific expertise** — transplant volumes for transplant cases, cath-lab volumes for cardiac
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before you pay anything
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Nigerian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics).

**Can my relative stay with me in the hospital?**
Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Nigeria?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up consults, and reports are shared with your Nigerian physician on request.
