Sri Lankan families have travelled to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and transplant treatment for years. With Colombo connected to Chennai, Bangalore and Kochi by direct flights in roughly 1.5 hours and the Indian e-medical visa processing in just a few days, the route is one of the most efficient for patients from Sri Lanka. This guide covers the costs in Sri Lankan Rupees, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Colombo, Kandy, Galle or Jaffna.

## Why Sri Lankan patients choose India

- **40–60% cost savings** compared with private care in Singapore, Thailand or Malaysia
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments experienced with Sri Lankan families
- **Sinhala, Tamil and English-speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **A direct flight option** — SriLankan Airlines and IndiGo fly Colombo–Chennai and Colombo–Bangalore nonstop in about 1.5 hours
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with a Sri Lankan Rupee reference for planning

## Medical visa from Sri Lanka: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Sri Lanka requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-medical visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **2–3 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Colombo to India for treatment

- **SriLankan Airlines direct to Chennai** — nonstop, around 1.5 hours; the simplest option for cardiac, cancer, orthopedic and transplant cases centred in Chennai
- **IndiGo direct to Bangalore** — nonstop, around 1.5 hours; ideal for transplant, cardiac and orthopedic treatment in Bangalore
- **Air India direct to Kochi** — nonstop, around 1 hour; convenient for cardiac, general surgery and orthopedic cases

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Sri Lankan patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Sri Lankan families and their documentation
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Sri Lankan physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Chennai** (1.5h from Colombo, direct — cardiac, cancer, orthopedics, transplant), **Bangalore** (1.5h, direct — transplant, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Kochi** (1h, direct — cardiac, general surgery, orthopedics).

## Top treatments Sri Lankan patients travel for

2. **Orthopedic treatment** — knee and hip replacements, spine surgery
3. **Cancer care** — full protocols including radiation and surgical oncology

## How to get started

1. Gather recent medical reports, prescriptions and imaging
2. Send them for a **free opinion and written cost estimate** — you receive a hospital shortlist matched to your case
3. Confirm the hospital and receive the invitation letter
4. Apply for the e-medical visa (2–3 business days); file attendant visas for family
5. Book the direct Colombo–Chennai, Colombo–Bangalore or Colombo–Kochi flight
6. Fly, get admitted, and recover while the coordinator manages logistics

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does heart surgery cost in India vs Sri Lanka?**
Cardiac surgery packages in India run $4,500–12,000 (रू1,341,000–3,576,000) — typically 40–60% less than comparable private care abroad, at JCI-accredited hospitals.

**Is there a direct flight from Colombo to India?**
Yes — SriLankan Airlines and IndiGo fly Colombo to Chennai and Bangalore nonstop in about 1.5 hours, and Air India flies direct to Kochi in around 1 hour.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from Sri Lanka?**
The e-medical visa typically processes in 2–3 business days once the hospital invitation letter is issued.

**Can my relative stay with me in the hospital?**
Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Sri Lanka?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Sri Lankan physician on request.
