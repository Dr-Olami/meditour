Cameroonian families increasingly travel to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment — procedures that are either unavailable locally or priced well beyond reach in private regional hospitals. With connecting flights from Douala and Yaoundé through Addis Ababa or Dubai, and an e-medical visa processing in under a week, the route is practical for patients from Bamenda, Bafoussam and across Cameroon. This guide covers the costs in Central African CFA francs, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Douala.

## Why Cameroonian patients choose India

- **50–70% cost savings** compared with South Africa, France or Turkey
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments
- **French and English speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **Connecting flights from Douala** — one to two stops via Ethiopian Airlines or Emirates, around 10–12 hours
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with an FCFA reference for planning

## Medical visa from Cameroon: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Cameroon requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

Carry physical copies of all medical reports in both French and English; the hospital international desk may request them at admission.

## How to travel from Douala to India for treatment

There are no direct flights from Cameroon to India, but the one-stop routes are reliable:

- **Ethiopian Airlines via Addis Ababa** — one-stop into Bangalore, Mumbai or Delhi; total journey around 10–12 hours from Douala
- **Emirates via Dubai** — comfortable connection into Bangalore, Mumbai or Delhi
- **Qatar Airways via Doha** — strong schedule into Mumbai and Delhi

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Cameroonian patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Cameroonian families and French-language coordination
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Cameroonian physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics).
