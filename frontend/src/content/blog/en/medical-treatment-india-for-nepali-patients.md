Nepali families have travelled to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and neurosurgery for years. With Kathmandu connected to Delhi by direct flights in roughly 1.5 hours and visa-free entry for Nepali citizens, the route is one of the most seamless for patients from Nepal. This guide covers the costs in Nepali Rupees, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar or Lalitpur.

## Why Nepali patients choose India

- **50–70% cost savings** compared with private care in Thailand, Singapore or Bangladesh
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments experienced with Nepali families
- **Nepali, Hindi and English-speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **A direct flight option** — Air India and IndiGo fly Kathmandu–Delhi nonstop in about 1.5 hours; direct routes also reach Bangalore
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with a Nepali Rupee reference for planning

## Medical visa from Nepal: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Nepal requirements follow the visa-free pathway for Nepali citizens:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — recommended for smooth admission and treatment planning
3. **Travel to India (visa-free for Nepali citizens)** — Nepali citizens can enter India without a visa; carry your passport or valid identification along with medical documents and the invitation letter
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant travel for family members is straightforward under the same visa-free arrangement

## How to travel from Kathmandu to India for treatment

- **Air India direct to Delhi** — nonstop, around 1.5 hours; the simplest option for cardiac, cancer and neurosurgery cases centred in Delhi
- **IndiGo direct to Bangalore** — nonstop, around 3 hours; ideal for transplant, cardiac and orthopedic treatment in Bangalore
- **Buddha Air or connecting via Delhi to Varanasi** — around 1 hour connecting; convenient for general surgery and orthopedic cases

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant travel is straightforward under the visa-free arrangement.

## Choosing the right hospital for Nepali patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Nepali families and their documentation
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Nepali physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Delhi** (1.5h from Kathmandu, direct — cardiac, cancer, neurosurgery), **Bangalore** (3h, direct — transplant, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Varanasi** (1h, connecting via Delhi — general surgery, orthopedics).

## Top treatments Nepali patients travel for

1. **Cardiac surgery** — bypass and valve procedures at 50–70% below regional private pricing
2. **Orthopedic treatment** — knee and hip replacements, spine surgery
3. **Cancer care** — full protocols including radiation and surgical oncology
4. **Kidney transplants** — dedicated programmes with international coordinators
5. **Fertility treatment** — IVF cycles planned around a 2–3 week stay

**How much does heart surgery cost in India vs Nepal?**

**Is there a direct flight from Kathmandu to India?**
Yes — Air India and IndiGo fly Kathmandu to Delhi nonstop in about 1.5 hours, and IndiGo flies direct to Bangalore in around 3 hours.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from Nepal?**
Nepali citizens can enter India visa-free — no visa application is required, though carrying the hospital invitation letter is recommended for smooth admission.

**Can my relative stay with me in the hospital?**
Yes — attendant travel for family members is straightforward under the visa-free arrangement, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Nepal?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Nepali physician on request.
