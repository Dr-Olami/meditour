Iraqi families increasingly travel to India for cardiac surgery, orthopedics, cancer care and fertility treatment, drawn by JCI-accredited hospitals and costs well below private options in the region. Baghdad, Basra, Erbil and Mosul connect to India through one-stop routes via Dubai, and the e-medical visa processes in under a week. This guide covers costs in Iraqi Dinars, the visa steps, and how to plan treatment from Baghdad and other major cities.

## Why Iraqi patients choose India

- **50–70% cost savings** compared with Turkey, Jordan and Iran
- **JCI-accredited hospitals** with international patient departments
- **Arabic, Kurdish and English speaking doctors and coordinators** throughout the journey
- **One-stop flights via Dubai** — connecting routes from Baghdad reach Delhi in about 5 hours, Mumbai in 5.5 and Bangalore in 6
- **Transparent package pricing** quoted in USD, with an IQD reference for planning

## Medical visa from Iraq: requirements and process

The medical visa India from Iraq requirements follow the e-visa pathway:

1. **Share medical reports** — send scans, prescriptions and hospital summaries via WhatsApp so a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter
2. **Receive the hospital invitation letter** — required for the medical visa application
3. **Apply for the e-medical visa** — submit passport, photographs, medical documents and the invitation letter; processing typically takes **5–7 business days**
4. **Fly and get admitted** — attendant visas can be filed for family members

## How to travel from Baghdad to India for treatment

- **Emirates via Dubai** — one-stop into Delhi in about 5 hours, Bangalore in 6; the most common route for Iraqi families
- **Flydubai via Dubai** — one-stop into Delhi and Mumbai; competitive fares from Baghdad and Basra
- **Qatar Airways via Doha** — comfortable connections into Delhi and Mumbai from Erbil and Baghdad

Budget a full travel day each way, with airport pickup in India arranged in advance. Most families fly one attendant; attendant visas are filed with the patient's medical visa.

## Choosing the right hospital for Iraqi patients

1. **JCI or NABH accreditation** — the baseline for major surgery
2. **International patient department** experienced with Iraqi families
3. **Procedure-specific volumes** — ask how many of your procedure the hospital performs annually
4. **Written package quote** — consultation, procedure, stay and discharge medication itemised before payment
5. **Telemedicine follow-up** — post-return consults so your Iraqi physician stays in the loop

Recommended cities: **Bangalore** (transplants, cardiac, orthopedics, cancer), **Delhi** (cardiac, cancer, orthopedics), **Mumbai** (cancer, cardiac, cosmetic).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does heart surgery cost in India vs Iraq?**
Cardiac surgery packages in India run $4,500–12,000 (ع.د5.9M–15.7M) — typically 50–70% less than comparable private care abroad, at JCI-accredited hospitals.

**Is there a direct flight from Baghdad to India?**
No — there are no direct flights from Baghdad to India, but one-stop routes via Dubai reach Delhi in about 5 hours, Mumbai in 5.5 and Bangalore in 6.

**How long does the Indian medical visa take from Iraq?**
The e-medical visa typically processes in 5–7 business days once the hospital invitation letter is issued.

**Can my relative stay with me in the hospital?**
Yes — attendant visas are filed alongside the medical visa, and family accommodation near the hospital is arranged.

**What about follow-up after I return to Iraq?**
Partner hospitals offer telemedicine follow-up, and reports are shared with your Iraqi physician on request.
