Our partner hospitals are equipped with **da Vinci robotic surgical systems**, **advanced endoscopy suites** (ERCP, EUS, capsule endoscopy), **dedicated GI ICU**, and **multidisciplinary tumour boards** for comprehensive cancer care.

## Conditions Treated

- **GI Cancers** — colorectal, gastric, oesophageal, pancreatic, liver, gallbladder
- **Benign GI disorders** — gallstones, hernias, appendicitis, haemorrhoids, fistulas
- **HPB conditions** — liver tumours (benign and malignant), pancreatic cysts, bile duct strictures
- **Inflammatory bowel disease** — Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis
- **GERD and motility disorders** — acid reflux, achalasia, gastroparesis
- **Liver disease** — cirrhosis, portal hypertension, liver abscess
- **Pancreatic disease** — pancreatitis, pancreatic pseudocyst
- **Colorectal conditions** — polyps, diverticulitis, colorectal cancer

## Treatment Modalities

### Surgical Gastroenterology

- **GI cancer surgery** — colorectal, gastric, oesophageal resections with lymph node dissection
- **Robotic GI surgery** — da Vinci system for enhanced precision and faster recovery
- **Laparoscopic GI surgery** — minimally invasive approach for gallbladder, appendix, hernia
- **Anti-reflux surgery** — laparoscopic fundoplication for GERD
- **Hernia repair** — open and laparoscopic (inguinal, incisional, hiatal)

### HPB Surgery (Hepato-Pancreato-Biliary)

- **Liver resection** — for hepatocellular carcinoma, metastases, benign tumours
- **Pancreatic surgery** — Whipple's procedure (pancreaticoduodenectomy), distal pancreatectomy
- **Biliary surgery** — bile duct exploration, biliary bypass, choledochal cyst excision
- **Gallbladder surgery** — laparoscopic and robotic cholecystectomy
- **Complex liver surgery** — for portal hypertension, liver trauma

### Advanced Endoscopy

- **Therapeutic ERCP** — bile duct stone removal, stent placement
- **Endoscopic ultrasound (EUS)** — diagnostic and therapeutic
- **Capsule endoscopy** — small bowel evaluation
- **Upper GI endoscopy** — diagnostic and therapeutic (variceal banding, polypectomy)
- **Colonoscopy** — screening, polypectomy, stenting

### GI Oncology

- **Multidisciplinary tumour board** — personalised treatment planning
- **Neoadjuvant and adjuvant chemotherapy** coordination
- **Cytoreductive surgery** — for peritoneal surface malignancies
- **HIPEC** — hyperthermic intraperitoneal chemotherapy
