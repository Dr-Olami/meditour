## Overview

> **Summary:** Organ transplant programmes in Bangalore offer liver, kidney, heart and lung transplants at JCI-accredited hospitals with dedicated transplant ICUs, advanced organ preservation systems and experienced surgical teams. Costs range from $20,000 to $40,000 — 60-80% savings compared to Western countries — with comprehensive international patient coordinators and strict adherence to transplant regulations and ethics.

Organ transplant programmes at our partner hospitals in Bangalore are among the most comprehensive in India, offering **liver**, **kidney**, **heart** and **lung** transplants. The transplant teams include surgeons, hepatologists, nephrologists, cardiologists, transplant coordinators and dedicated nursing staff with extensive experience in both living-donor and deceased-donor transplants.

All transplant programmes follow **international protocols** with rigorous donor evaluation, recipient assessment and post-transplant immunosuppression management. The hospitals are equipped with dedicated transplant ICUs, advanced organ preservation systems and comprehensive infection control measures.

## Transplant Programmes

### Liver Transplant

Both **living-donor liver transplant (LDLT)** and **deceased-donor liver transplant (DDLT)** programmes. The team performs right lobe, left lobe and split liver transplants. Indications include end-stage liver disease (cirrhosis), liver cancer (HCC within Milan criteria), acute liver failure and biliary atresia in children.

### Kidney Transplant

**Living-donor kidney transplant** is the most common organ transplant performed. The programme includes ABO-incompatible transplants, paired kidney exchange and pre-emptive transplantation. Laparoscopic donor nephrectomy ensures faster donor recovery. Indications include end-stage renal disease from diabetes, hypertension, glomerulonephritis and polycystic kidney disease.

### Heart Transplant

Heart transplant assessment and surgery for end-stage heart failure not amenable to medical or surgical therapy. Includes bridging therapies such as LVAD (left ventricular assist device) for patients awaiting transplant. Rigorous recipient and donor matching protocols.

### Lung Transplant

Single and bilateral lung transplants for end-stage lung disease including COPD, pulmonary fibrosis, cystic fibrosis and pulmonary hypertension. Includes ECMO bridging for critically ill patients.

## Transplant Process

### Pre-Transplant Evaluation

- Comprehensive **medical evaluation** of recipient
- **Donor work-up** for living donors (blood group compatibility, tissue typing, cross-match)
- **Transplant committee** approval
- **Legal and regulatory** clearance per organ transplant laws
- Financial counselling and package finalisation

### Transplant Surgery

- **State-of-the-art operating theatres** with laminar airflow
- **Advanced organ preservation** techniques including machine perfusion
- **Intra-operative monitoring** with TEE and haemodynamic support
- **Dedicated transplant anaesthesia** team

### Post-Transplant Care

- **Dedicated transplant ICU** with HEPA-filtered rooms
- **Immunosuppression management** with therapeutic drug monitoring
- **Infection prophylaxis** and surveillance
- **Regular graft function monitoring** with biopsy when needed
- **Rehabilitation and nutrition** support
