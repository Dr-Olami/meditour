## Procedures & Interventions

### Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery (FESS)

Minimally invasive endoscopic surgery for chronic sinusitis and nasal polyps. Uses high-definition cameras and microdebriders to open sinus passages and remove diseased tissue. Typically day-care or overnight stay with recovery in 1-2 weeks.

### Cochlear Implant

Surgical implantation of electronic device to restore hearing in patients with severe-to-profound sensorineural hearing loss. Includes internal electrode array insertion and external sound processor fitting. Hospital stay 1-2 days with activation after 3-4 weeks.

### Tonsillectomy & Adenoidectomy

Surgical removal of tonsils and adenoids for recurrent infections or obstructive sleep apnoea. Performed with **coblation** or **electrocautery** techniques. Day-care or overnight stay with recovery in 1-2 weeks.

### Thyroid Surgery

- **Hemithyroidectomy** — removal of one thyroid lobe
- **Total thyroidectomy** — complete gland removal
- **Neck dissection** — for thyroid cancer with lymph node involvement
- Intra-operative nerve monitoring to protect recurrent laryngeal nerve

### Sleep Apnoea Surgery

- **UPPP** (uvulopalatopharyngoplasty) — removal of excess soft palate tissue
- **Septoplasty & turbinate reduction** — for nasal obstruction
- **Maxillomandibular advancement** — for severe obstructive sleep apnoea
- **Hypoglossal nerve stimulation** — implantable device for tongue base obstruction
