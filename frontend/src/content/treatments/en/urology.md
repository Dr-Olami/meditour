## Procedures & Interventions

### Kidney Stone Management

- **RIRS (Retrograde Intrarenal Surgery)** — flexible ureteroscopy with laser lithotripsy for stones up to 2cm. Day-care or overnight stay.
- **PCNL (Percutaneous Nephrolithotomy)** — keyhole removal of large kidney stones through a small back incision. Hospital stay 2-3 days.
- **ESWL (Extracorporeal Shock Wave Lithotripsy)** — non-invasive shock wave stone fragmentation. Outpatient procedure.
- **Mini-PCNL** — smaller tract for reduced bleeding and faster recovery

### Laser Prostate Surgery (HoLEP / TURP)

- **HoLEP (Holmium Laser Enucleation of Prostate)** — laser removal of prostate tissue for BPH. Suitable for large prostates. Catheter for 1-2 days with hospital stay 2-3 days.
- **TURP (Transurethral Resection of Prostate)** — traditional endoscopic prostate removal. Hospital stay 2-3 days.
- **GreenLight laser** — photoselective vaporisation for BPH

### Robotic Radical Prostatectomy

Robot-assisted surgical removal of the prostate for localised prostate cancer. Benefits include **precision nerve-sparing**, **minimal blood loss**, **faster continence recovery** and **shorter hospital stay**. Hospital stay 2-3 days with catheter for 7-10 days.

### Bladder Tumour Surgery

- **TURBT (Transurethral Resection of Bladder Tumour)** — endoscopic removal for non-muscle invasive bladder cancer
- **Radical cystectomy** — surgical removal of bladder for muscle-invasive cancer with urinary diversion (ileal conduit or neobladder)
- **Intravesical BCG/chemotherapy** — for non-muscle invasive disease

### Male Infertility Work-Up

- **Semen analysis** and hormonal evaluation
- **Varicocele repair** — microsurgical or laparoscopic
- **Sperm retrieval** — PESA, TESA, TESE and micro-TESE for IVF/ICSI
- **Vasectomy reversal** — microsurgical vaso-vasostomy
