Costs range from $2,000 to $15,000 — 60-80% savings compared to Western countries — with internationally trained paediatric neurologists, advanced electrophysiology services, and multidisciplinary care teams.

Paediatric neurology services at our partner hospitals in Bangalore provide specialised care for children with neurological disorders from birth through 18 years of age. The paediatric neurology teams include consultants with training from premier institutes such as AIIMS, PGIMER, and JIPMER, with expertise in epilepsy, neurodevelopmental disorders, neurometabolic diseases, neuro-immunological conditions, and neuromuscular disorders.

Our partner hospitals are equipped with **dedicated paediatric EEG facilities**, **advanced neuroimaging** (3 Tesla MRI, CT), **paediatric intensive care units**, and **multidisciplinary teams** including developmental specialists, psychologists, physiotherapists, occupational therapists, and dietitians.

## Conditions Treated

- **Epilepsy** — first seizure evaluation, epilepsy syndromes, refractory epilepsy, Dravet syndrome, infantile spasms
- **Neurodevelopmental disorders** — cerebral palsy, developmental delays, ADHD, autism spectrum, learning disabilities
- **Headache disorders** — migraine, tension-type headache, chronic daily headache
- **Demyelinating/immune-mediated disorders** — ADEM, optic neuritis, transverse myelitis, NMOSD, MS, autoimmune encephalitis, opsoclonus-myoclonus ataxia syndrome
- **Neurometabolic disorders** — inborn errors of metabolism, mitochondrial disorders, leukodystrophies
- **Neurogenetic disorders** — neurocutaneous syndromes, chromosomal disorders with neurological manifestations
- **Neuromuscular disorders** — muscular dystrophy, spinal muscular atrophy, neuropathies, myasthenia
- **Paediatric stroke** — arterial ischaemic stroke, sinovenous thrombosis, moyamoya disease
- **Neonatal neurology** — neonatal seizures, hypoxic-ischaemic encephalopathy, high-risk newborn follow-up
- **Neuroinfections** — meningitis, encephalitis, tuberculoma

## Treatment Modalities

### Epilepsy Management

- **First seizure evaluation** — comprehensive assessment to determine risk of recurrence
- **Epilepsy syndrome classification** — based on EEG, clinical features, and neuroimaging
- **Refractory epilepsy management** — advanced anti-epileptic drugs, combination therapy, surgical evaluation
- **Dietary therapies** — ketogenic diet, modified Atkins diet, low glycaemic index diet for drug-resistant epilepsy
- **Long-term video EEG monitoring** — for event classification and syndrome diagnosis

### Neurodevelopmental Care

- **Multidisciplinary evaluation** — involving developmental specialists, psychologists, therapists, and educators
- **Cerebral palsy management** — motor assessment, therapy planning, botulinum toxin, orthopaedic liaison
- **ADHD and autism spectrum** — diagnostic assessment, behavioural therapy, medication management
- **Learning disability evaluation** — psycho-educational assessment and remedial planning

### Neuro-immunological Disorders

- **Diagnosis** — MRI, electrophysiology, CSF analysis, antibody testing
- **Immunotherapy** — acute treatment (IVIG, plasmapheresis, steroids) and long-term management
- **Rehabilitation and monitoring** — physical therapy, cognitive rehabilitation, patient education

### Neurometabolic Disorders

- **Metabolic screening** — preliminary and confirmatory testing
- **Specific treatment** — enzyme replacement, substrate reduction, cofactor supplementation
- **Dietary management** — special formulas, restricted diets, emergency sick-day protocols
- **Long-term monitoring** — developmental assessment, biochemical monitoring, health education

### Paediatric Electrophysiology

- **Electroencephalography (EEG)** — neonates to 18 years, routine and sleep-deprived
- **Video EEG** — short-term and long-term monitoring for event classification
- **EMG/nerve conduction studies** — for suspected neuromuscular disorders
- **Visual Evoked Potentials (VEP)** — for visual pathway assessment
- **Brainstem Evoked Response Audiometry (BERA)** — for hearing assessment in infants and children
