## Overview

> **Summary:** Nephrology and kidney care in Bangalore offers comprehensive management of acute and chronic kidney disease, dialysis, glomerular disorders, and kidney transplantation at JCI-accredited hospitals. Costs range from $3,000 to $25,000 — 60-80% savings compared to Western countries — with dedicated international patient coordinators, advanced dialysis technology, and experienced transplant teams.

Nephrology services at our partner hospitals in Bangalore provide the complete spectrum of kidney care — from early-stage chronic kidney disease (CKD) management to complex kidney transplantation. The nephrology teams include transplant physicians, dialysis specialists, and glomerular disease experts with international training and certifications.

Our partner hospitals are equipped with **dedicated dialysis units**, **advanced immunology laboratories** for HLA typing and cross-match testing, **renal pathology services** with immunofluorescence and electron microscopy, and **comprehensive transplant programmes** with dedicated transplant coordinators for international patients.

## Conditions Treated

- **Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)** — staging, management, and progression slowing
- **Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)** — diagnosis and management including CRRT
- **Glomerular Diseases** — nephrotic syndrome, nephritic syndrome, IgA nephropathy, membranous nephropathy, FSGS, lupus nephritis
- **Diabetic Kidney Disease** — diabetic nephropathy management and prevention
- **Kidney Stone Disease** — metabolic evaluation and prevention
- **Electrolyte Disorders** — sodium, potassium, calcium, and acid-base disorders
- **Resistant Hypertension** — secondary causes and management
- **Post-Transplant Complications** — rejection, infection, recurrent disease
- **Hereditary Kidney Diseases** — polycystic kidney disease, Alport syndrome, cystinosis

## Treatment Modalities

### Dialysis Services

- **Haemodialysis** — conducted in dedicated units with specialised nursing staff and international-standard water treatment systems
- **Online Haemodiafiltration (HDF)** — advanced dialysis modality offering superior clearance and outcomes
- **Peritoneal Dialysis** — CAPD (Continuous Ambulatory Peritoneal Dialysis) and APD (Automated Peritoneal Dialysis) for home-based therapy
- **Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy (CRRT)** — for critically ill patients in ICU settings with haemodynamic instability

### Kidney Transplantation

- **Living-donor kidney transplant** — the most common transplant, with laparoscopic donor nephrectomy for faster donor recovery
- **ABO-incompatible kidney transplant** — for donors with non-matching blood groups, using desensitisation protocols
- **Paired kidney exchange** — for incompatible donor-recipient pairs
- **Pre-emptive transplantation** — transplant before dialysis initiation for better outcomes
- **Paediatric kidney transplantation** — specialised programme for children with end-stage renal disease

### Glomerular Disease Management

- **Kidney biopsy** with immunofluorescence and electron microscopy
- **Immunosuppressive therapy** protocols tailored to specific glomerular diseases
- **GlomCon-certified specialists** with expertise in rare glomerular disorders
- **Monitoring and follow-up** with proteinuria and renal function tracking

### Vascular Access Procedures

- **Arteriovenous (AV) fistula** creation and maintenance
- **AV graft** placement for patients with unsuitable veins
- **Tunneled dialysis catheters** for long-term dialysis access
- **AV fistula surveillance** and intervention for stenosis or thrombosis

## Transplant Process

### Pre-Transplant Evaluation

- Comprehensive **medical evaluation** of recipient and donor
- **Blood group compatibility**, tissue typing (HLA), and cross-match testing
- **Transplant committee** approval
- **Legal and regulatory** clearance per organ transplant laws
- Financial counselling and package finalisation

### Transplant Surgery

- **State-of-the-art operating theatres** with laminar airflow
- **Laparoscopic donor nephrectomy** for faster donor recovery
- **Dedicated transplant anaesthesia** team
- **Intra-operative monitoring** with haemodynamic support

### Post-Transplant Care

- **Dedicated transplant ICU** with HEPA-filtered rooms
- **Immunosuppression management** with therapeutic drug monitoring
- **Infection prophylaxis** and surveillance
- **Regular graft function monitoring** with biopsy when needed
- **Long-term follow-up** with teleconsultation for international patients
