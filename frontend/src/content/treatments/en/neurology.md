## Conditions Treated

- **Stroke** — ischaemic and haemorrhagic stroke, transient ischaemic attacks (TIA), stroke rehabilitation
- **Epilepsy** — first seizure evaluation, epilepsy syndromes, refractory epilepsy, status epilepticus
- **Movement disorders** — Parkinson's disease, essential tremor, dystonia, Huntington's disease, ataxia
- **Demyelinating diseases** — multiple sclerosis, neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder (NMOSD), ADEM
- **Headache disorders** — migraine, tension-type headache, cluster headache, chronic daily headache
- **Dementia** — Alzheimer's disease, vascular dementia, frontotemporal dementia, Lewy body dementia
- **Nerve and muscle disorders** — peripheral neuropathy, myasthenia gravis, muscular dystrophy, motor neuron disease
- **Sleep disorders** — insomnia, sleep apnoea, restless legs syndrome, narcolepsy
- **Vertigo and balance disorders** — BPPV, vestibular neuritis, Meniere's disease
- **Fibromyalgia and chronic pain** — comprehensive pain management
- **Neuroinfections** — meningitis, encephalitis, neurotuberculosis

## Treatment Modalities

### Stroke Management

- **Acute stroke care** — IV thrombolysis, mechanical thrombectomy, neurocritical care
- **Stroke rehabilitation** — physiotherapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy
- **Secondary prevention** — antiplatelet therapy, anticoagulation, risk factor management
- **Long-term follow-up** — functional assessment, medication adjustment, lifestyle counselling

### Epilepsy Management

- **First seizure evaluation** — comprehensive assessment to determine recurrence risk
- **Anti-epileptic drug therapy** — personalised medication selection and monitoring
- **Refractory epilepsy management** — combination therapy, vagus nerve stimulation (VNS)
- **Epilepsy surgery evaluation** — for drug-resistant cases with localised seizure focus
- **Long-term video EEG monitoring** — for event classification and syndrome diagnosis

### Movement Disorders

- **Parkinson's disease management** — medication optimisation, DBS evaluation
- **Dystonia treatment** — botulinum toxin injections, oral medications
- **Essential tremor** — medication, surgical options
- **Rehabilitation** — physiotherapy, gait training, fall prevention

### Demyelinating Diseases

- **Multiple sclerosis** — disease-modifying therapy, relapse management, symptom control
- **NMOSD** — immunotherapy, antibody monitoring
- **Rehabilitation and support** — physical therapy, cognitive rehabilitation, patient education

### Headache Management

- **Migraine** — acute treatment, preventive therapy, CGRP antagonists, botulinum toxin
- **Chronic daily headache** — medication overuse management, behavioural therapy
- **Cluster headache** — acute and preventive treatment

### Cognitive Disorders

- **Dementia evaluation** — cognitive assessment, neuroimaging, biomarker testing
- **Alzheimer's disease** — cholinesterase inhibitors, memantine, supportive care
- **Vascular dementia** — risk factor management, cognitive rehabilitation
- **Caregiver support** — education, counselling, support groups
