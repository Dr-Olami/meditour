Pulmonology and lung care at our partner hospitals in Bangalore covers the full spectrum of **respiratory medicine** and **thoracic surgery**. - **Lung cancer** — diagnostic workup, staging and surgical resection

- **Tuberculosis and drug-resistant TB** — including medical and surgical management
- **Pulmonary hypertension** — specialised vasodilator therapy and follow-up

## Procedures & Interventions

### Lung Resection Surgery

Surgical removal of diseased lung tissue for lung cancer, benign tumours, severe bronchiectasis or cavitary TB. Includes:

- **Lobectomy** — removal of one lobe (most common for lung cancer)
- **Segmentectomy** — wedge removal for small tumours or localised disease
- **Pneumonectomy** — removal of an entire lung (advanced cases)
- **Sleeve resection** — airway-sparing technique for central tumours

### VATS (Video-Assisted Thoracoscopic Surgery)

Minimally invasive thoracic surgery using small keyhole incisions and a high-definition camera. VATS offers:

- Smaller incisions and less post-operative pain
- Shorter hospital stay (typically 3-5 days)
- Faster return to normal activity
- Used for lobectomy, bullectomy, pleurodesis and lung biopsy

### Bronchoscopy

Endoscopic examination of the airways using a flexible bronchoscope. Two types:

- **Diagnostic bronchoscopy** — visual inspection, biopsy, BAL (bronchoalveolar lavage) and transbronchial lung biopsy
- **Therapeutic bronchoscopy** — foreign-body removal, tumour debulking, airway stent placement, balloon dilation and cryotherapy

### COPD and Asthma Management

Structured long-term management packages including:

- Pulmonary function testing (spirometry, DLCO, lung volumes)
- Medication optimisation (bronchodilators, inhaled corticosteroids, biologics)
- Inhaler-technique coaching and written action plan
- Smoking-cessation support
- Annual influenza and pneumococcal vaccination
- Tele-consultation follow-up after return home

### Sleep Apnea Evaluation and Treatment

- Overnight **polysomnography (sleep study)**
- CPAP / BiPAP titration and mask fitting
- Mandibular advancement device fitting
- Surgical options (UPPP, maxillomandibular advancement) for selected cases
- Remote data monitoring of CPAP usage after travel

### Pulmonary Rehabilitation

Structured 6-8 week programme combining:

- Supervised exercise training
- Breathing-technique education (pursed-lip, diaphragmatic breathing)
- Nutritional counselling
- Psychosocial support
- Self-management education

## Important Considerations

- Lung surgery outcomes depend on **lung function, fitness and tumour stage** — a pre-operative assessment is mandatory
- COPD and asthma are **chronic conditions** requiring long-term follow-up; we arrange remote tele-consultations
- Sleep-apnoea treatment is **ongoing** — CPAP needs regular review and data download
- Smoking cessation **before surgery** significantly improves outcomes and reduces complications
- Patients with drug-resistant TB require **infection-control clearance** before travel — we advise on eligibility
- **Pre-travel records** (recent chest X-ray, PFT, CT scans) help us plan your care before you arrive
