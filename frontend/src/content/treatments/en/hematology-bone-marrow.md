## Conditions Treated

### Blood Cancers (Malignant Hematology)

- **Acute leukemias** — AML (acute myeloid leukemia) and ALL (acute lymphoblastic leukemia)
- **Chronic leukemias** — CML (chronic myeloid leukemia) and CLL (chronic lymphocytic leukemia)
- **Lymphomas** — Hodgkin lymphoma and non-Hodgkin lymphoma (NHL)
- **Multiple myeloma** — including relapsed/refractory disease
- **Myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS)** — pre-leukaemic bone-marrow disorders
- **Myeloproliferative neoplasms (MPN)** — including polycythaemia vera and myelofibrosis

### Inherited Blood Disorders (Benign Hematology)

- **Thalassemia major** — curative BMT for children and young adults
- **Sickle cell disease** — curative BMT and supportive care
- **Aplastic anemia** — immunosuppressive therapy and BMT
- **Haemophilia** — comprehensive haemophilia care centre
- **Immune deficiency disorders** — including severe combined immunodeficiency (SCID)

## Procedures & Interventions

### Autologous Bone Marrow Transplant

Patient's own stem cells are harvested, frozen and re-infused after high-dose chemotherapy. Used for:

- Multiple myeloma
- Relapsed Hodgkin and non-Hodgkin lymphoma
- Selected solid tumours (neuroblastoma, germ-cell tumours)

### Allogeneic Bone Marrow Transplant

Stem cells from a donor (matched sibling, matched unrelated donor, or haploidentical family donor) are infused after conditioning chemotherapy. Used for:

- Acute leukemias (AML, ALL) in remission or relapse
- Myelodysplastic syndromes
- Aplastic anemia
- Thalassemia major and sickle cell disease
- Inherited immune deficiencies

### Haploidentical Transplant

Half-matched (50% HLA) family donor transplant — a breakthrough option for patients without a fully matched donor. Especially valuable for international patients from regions with smaller donor registries. Uses advanced post-transplant cyclophosphamide (PTCy) to reduce graft-versus-host disease (GVHD).

### CAR-T Cell Therapy

Chimeric antigen receptor T-cell therapy — a personalised cellular immunotherapy for relapsed/refractory blood cancers. Available for:

- **B-cell ALL** (paediatric and adult)
- **Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL)**
- **Follicular lymphoma**
- **Multiple myeloma** (BCMA-directed CAR-T)

Includes leukapheresis, cell manufacturing, lymphodepletion chemotherapy and CAR-T infusion with dedicated cellular-therapy unit and structured long-term follow-up for cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and neurotoxicity.

### Thalassemia and Sickle Cell Transplant

Curative BMT for children and young adults with thalassemia major or sickle cell disease. Includes:

- HLA typing of siblings and family
- Matched-sibling donor transplant (best outcomes)
- Haploidentical transplant for patients without matched sibling
- Comprehensive pre-transplant evaluation and post-transplant iron-chelation planning

### Aplastic Anemia Therapy

Two treatment pathways based on age, severity and donor availability:

- **Immunosuppressive therapy (IST)** — anti-thymocyte globulin (ATG) plus cyclosporine for older patients or those without a donor
- **Allogeneic BMT** — first-line for young patients with a matched sibling donor

### Supportive Hematology Care

- Comprehensive **haemophilia care** — factor replacement, prophylaxis and joint-care programme
- **Thalassemia transfusion and chelation** programme for patients not undergoing BMT
- **Sickle cell hydroxyurea** and transfusion programme
- **Thrombosis and haemostasis** clinic for clotting disorders
- **Iron-deficiency anaemia** workup and IV iron therapy

## Important Considerations

- BMT is a **major procedure** requiring 4-6 weeks inpatient stay and 3-12 months recovery
- **Donor availability** is a key factor — we coordinate HLA typing of family members before travel
- **Disease stage matters** — earlier transplant in remission gives better outcomes
- **CAR-T therapy** requires specific eligibility criteria and may need manufacturing lead time
- **Post-transplant follow-up** is essential — we arrange tele-medicine consultations after return
- **Infection prevention** is critical in the first 100 days post-transplant — patients need a clean home environment and restricted contact
- **Fertility preservation** should be discussed before chemotherapy — sperm/egg banking can be arranged
- **Pre-travel records** (recent blood counts, bone marrow biopsy, HLA typing, imaging) help us plan your care before you arrive
