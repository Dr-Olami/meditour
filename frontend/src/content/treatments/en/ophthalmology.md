## Overview

Ophthalmology care at our partner hospitals in Bangalore covers the full range of eye care — from routine vision correction to complex retinal surgery and corneal transplantation. The ophthalmology teams include **cataract surgeons**, **cornea specialists**, **retina specialists**, **glaucoma experts** and **paediatric ophthalmologists**.

All partner hospitals feature **advanced diagnostic suites** with OCT, fundus photography, corneal topography and biometry. The operating theatres are equipped with **femtosecond laser platforms** for bladeless cataract and LASIK surgery.

## Conditions Treated

- **Cataract** — clouding of the natural lens requiring surgical replacement
- **Refractive errors** — myopia, hyperopia and astigmatism correctable with laser
- **Glaucoma** — elevated intraocular pressure causing optic nerve damage
- **Diabetic retinopathy** — retinal damage from diabetes
- **Macular degeneration** — age-related central vision loss
- **Corneal disease** — keratoconus, corneal scarring and dystrophies
- **Retinal detachment** — surgical repair to restore vision
- **Paediatric eye disorders** — squint, amblyopia and congenital cataract

## Procedures & Interventions

### Cataract Surgery with Premium IOL

Phacoemulsification with foldable intraocular lens implantation. Options include:
- **Monofocal IOL** — clear distance or near vision
- **Multifocal IOL** — clear vision at all distances
- **Toric IOL** — astigmatism correction
- **Extended depth of focus (EDOF) IOL** — seamless intermediate vision

Femtosecond laser-assisted cataract surgery (FLACS) available for bladeless precision. Day-care procedure with recovery in 1-2 weeks.

### LASIK / SMILE

Laser vision correction for refractive errors:
- **LASIK** — flap-based laser correction for myopia, hyperopia and astigmatism
- **SMILE** — minimally invasive small incision lenticule extraction
- **PRK / TransPRK** — surface ablation for thin corneas
- **ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens)** — for high refractive errors unsuitable for laser

### Corneal Transplant

- **Penetrating keratoplasty (PK)** — full-thickness corneal transplant
- **DSEK / DSAEK** — partial-thickness transplant for endothelial disease
- **DMEK** — Descemet membrane endothelial keratoplasty
- **Deep anterior lamellar keratoplasty (DALK)** — for stromal disease

### Glaucoma Surgery

- **Trabeculectomy** — creation of new drainage channel
- **Glaucoma drainage devices** — Ahmed valve and Baerveldt implant
- **Laser trabeculoplasty** (SLT) — for open-angle glaucoma
- **Minimally invasive glaucoma surgery (MIGS)** — iStent and Xen gel stent

### Retina Laser Treatment

- **Panretinal photocoagulation** — for diabetic retinopathy
- **Focal laser** — for macular oedema
- **Photodynamic therapy** — for macular degeneration
- **Vitrectomy** — for retinal detachment, vitreous haemorrhage and macular holes
