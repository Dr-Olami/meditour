## Procedures & Interventions

### Brain Tumour Surgery

Microsurgical resection of brain tumours using **neuronavigation** and **intra-operative fluorescence** (5-ALA) for maximal safe resection. Awake craniotomy for tumours in eloquent brain areas. Hospital stay is typically 5-10 days depending on tumour type and location.

### Spinal Fusion

Stabilisation of spinal segments using pedicle screws, rods and interbody cages. Performed with **intra-operative neuromonitoring** to protect neural structures. Options include TLIF, PLIF and ALIF approaches. Recovery time is 4-12 weeks.

### Disc Replacement

Artificial disc implantation for cervical and lumbar degenerative disc disease. Motion-preserving alternative to fusion surgery. Minimally invasive approach with hospital stay of 2-3 days and recovery in 2-4 weeks.

### Stroke Thrombectomy

Mechanical thrombectomy for acute ischaemic stroke — removal of blood clots from brain arteries using stent retrievers and aspiration catheters. Time-critical procedure performed in a **neurointerventional suite**. Must be performed within 6-24 hours of symptom onset.

### Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS)

Implantation of electrodes in specific brain nuclei for movement disorders — Parkinson's disease, essential tremor and dystonia. Programmed externally with adjustable stimulation parameters.
