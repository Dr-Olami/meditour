## Overview

Bariatric surgery at our partner hospitals in Bangalore offers safe, evidence-based weight-loss procedures for patients with severe obesity and related metabolic conditions. The bariatric teams include **laparoscopic surgeons**, **bariatric physicians**, **dietitians** and **psychologists** working together to ensure long-term success.

All procedures are performed **laparoscopically** (keyhole surgery) with minimal scarring, reduced pain and faster recovery. The hospitals feature dedicated **bariatric surgery suites** with advanced stapling and energy devices, and comprehensive post-surgery support programmes.

## Conditions Treated

- **Severe obesity** (BMI ≥ 35) with comorbidities such as diabetes, hypertension or sleep apnoea
- **Obesity with type 2 diabetes** — metabolic surgery for glycaemic control
- **Morbid obesity** (BMI ≥ 40) without comorbidities
- **Failed previous bariatric surgery** — revisional procedures
- **Obesity-related cardiovascular disease** — weight reduction for cardiac health
- **Obesity with joint problems** — weight loss to reduce joint stress

## Procedures & Interventions

### Sleeve Gastrectomy

Removal of approximately 80% of the stomach, leaving a narrow sleeve-shaped stomach. Reduces stomach capacity and decreases ghrelin (hunger hormone) production. Most popular bariatric procedure worldwide. Hospital stay is 2-3 days with recovery in 1-2 weeks.

### Gastric Bypass (Roux-en-Y)

Creation of a small stomach pouch connected directly to the small intestine, bypassing the majority of the stomach and duodenum. Provides both **restriction** and **malabsorption** for maximum weight loss. Particularly effective for patients with type 2 diabetes. Hospital stay is 3-4 days.

### Mini Gastric Bypass (MGB)

A simplified version of gastric bypass with a single anastomosis. Faster procedure with comparable weight loss outcomes. Lower complication rate than traditional Roux-en-Y bypass. Hospital stay is 2-3 days.

### Revisional Bariatric Surgery

Correction or conversion of previous bariatric procedures that have failed or caused complications. Includes band-to-sleeve conversion, sleeve-to-bypass conversion and revision of dilated gastric pouches. Requires specialised expertise and careful evaluation.

### Non-Surgical Options

- **Intragastric balloon** — temporary balloon placement for moderate weight loss
- **Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty** — endoscopic suturing to reduce stomach size

- **Regular weight monitoring** and body composition analysis
