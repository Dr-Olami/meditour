## Procedures & Interventions

### Rhinoplasty (Nose Reshaping)

Surgical reshaping of the nose for aesthetic or functional reasons. Options include open and closed techniques. Can address dorsal hump, tip refinement, nostril width and deviated septum. Recovery in 1-2 weeks with final results visible after 6-12 months.

### Liposuction

Removal of localised fat deposits using tumescent or laser-assisted techniques. Areas include abdomen, flanks, thighs, arms, chin and neck. Performed as day-care or overnight stay. Recovery in 1-2 weeks with compression garment wear for 4-6 weeks.

### Hair Transplant

- **FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)** — individual follicular units extracted and transplanted
- **FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation)** — strip harvesting for larger areas
- **DHI (Direct Hair Implantation)** — implanter pen for precise placement
- Natural-looking results visible after 6-12 months

### Tummy Tuck (Abdominoplasty)

Surgical removal of excess skin and fat from the abdomen with tightening of abdominal muscles. Options include full, mini and extended abdominoplasty. Hospital stay 1-2 days with recovery in 2-4 weeks.

### Breast Procedures

- **Breast augmentation** — implant-based enhancement
- **Breast reduction** — relief of back, neck and shoulder pain
- **Breast lift (mastopexy)** — correction of sagging
- **Gynaecomastia surgery** — male breast reduction

### Non-Surgical Treatments

- **Botox** and **dermal fillers** for wrinkle reduction
- **Chemical peels** for skin rejuvenation
- **Laser resurfacing** for scars and pigmentation
- **Thread lift** for non-surgical facial lifting
- **PRP therapy** for hair and skin
