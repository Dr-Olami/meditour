## Overview

A lung transplant is a life-saving surgical procedure in which one or both diseased lungs are replaced with healthy lungs from a donor. It is typically recommended for patients with end-stage lung disease, including chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, cystic fibrosis, and pulmonary arterial hypertension, where other treatments have been exhausted. In India, living-donor lobar lung transplantation is the primary approach for international patients, where lower lobes from healthy relatives are surgically removed and transplanted into the recipient.

Bangalore has emerged as a premier destination for lung transplant surgery, home to several JCI-accredited hospitals with dedicated lung transplant units, advanced ICU infrastructure with ECMO capability, and multidisciplinary teams comprising pulmonologists, transplant surgeons, anaesthesiologists, and specialised nursing staff. These centres follow international clinical protocols and have reported outcomes on par with leading Western institutions.

For international patients, the combination of clinical excellence and significant cost savings makes Bangalore an attractive choice. A lung transplant that would cost over $1,000,000 in the United States can be performed for between $40,000 and $90,000 in Bangalore, without compromising on quality of care or safety standards.

## Procedure Details

The lung transplant process begins with a comprehensive evaluation of both the recipient and the living donor(s). This includes blood tests, imaging studies (CT and echocardiography), pulmonary function tests, cardiac catheterisation, tissue typing (HLA matching), and psychosocial assessments. Once compatibility is confirmed and the transplant authorisation committee grants approval, the surgery is scheduled. The living-donor lobar lung transplant involves simultaneous surgeries: the donor lobectomies, in which the lower lobes are removed from two healthy donors (for a bilateral transplant), and the recipient surgery, in which the diseased lungs are excised and the donor lobes are implanted.

The surgery itself lasts between 6 and 10 hours under general anaesthesia. The recipient's diseased lung(s) are carefully removed, preserving the vital blood vessels and airways, and the donor lobe(s) are connected to the recipient's vascular and bronchial systems. Cardiopulmonary bypass or ECMO may be required during the procedure. After surgery, the recipient is transferred to the ICU with mechanical ventilation support for close monitoring of lung function, oxygenation, bleeding, and signs of rejection. Immunosuppressive medications are initiated immediately to prevent the body's immune system from attacking the new organ.

Post-operative care is critical and spans several weeks in the hospital followed by extended monitoring in Bangalore. The transplanted lung(s) begin functioning almost immediately, and patients are gradually weaned from the ventilator and transitioned from ICU to a private room. Regular blood tests, pulmonary function tests, bronchoscopy with biopsy (if needed), and imaging are performed to monitor graft function. Patients receive detailed counselling on medication adherence, pulmonary rehabilitation, dietary restrictions, infection precautions, and signs of complications before being cleared for travel.

## Travel & Visa

International patients travelling to Bangalore for a lung transplant will need a medical visa (e-Medical Visa), which can be applied for online through the Indian government's e-Visa portal. The hospital will provide a medical visa invitation letter once the evaluation and scheduling are confirmed. The medical visa is typically valid for 60 days and can be extended if a longer stay is required for recovery.

Patients should plan to arrive in Bangalore at least two weeks before the scheduled surgery date to complete pre-transplant evaluations, donor workup, and committee approvals. It is advisable to travel with two or more attendants (including the donors) who can provide support during the hospital stay and recovery period. The hospital's international patient team will arrange airport pickup, accommodation guidance for attendants, and local transportation for follow-up visits. Ensure that all medical records, donor relationship documents, and pathology reports are carried in original or attested copies for the transplant authorisation committee review.
