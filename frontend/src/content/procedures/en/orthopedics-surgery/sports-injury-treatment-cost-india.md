## Overview

Sports Injury Treatment encompasses the diagnosis, management, and rehabilitation of musculoskeletal injuries sustained during athletic activity. These range from acute injuries such as ligament tears (ACL, MCL, ankle), tendon ruptures (Achilles, rotator cuff), and fractures, to overuse conditions including tendinopathy, stress fractures, and cartilage damage. The goal is not only to repair the injury but to restore full function and safely return the patient to their sport.

In Bangalore, sports injury treatment is delivered at JCI- and NABH-accredited hospitals by fellowship-trained sports medicine surgeons and physiotherapists, many of whom have worked with national and professional sports teams. Treatment options span the full spectrum from conservative management (immobilisation, physiotherapy, injections) to arthroscopic surgical repair and regenerative medicine (PRP, stem cell therapy), ensuring an individualised approach for each athlete.

For international patients, Bangalore offers an excellent medical tourism destination: English-speaking medical teams, dedicated international patient desks, streamlined visa processing, and world-class rehabilitation facilities. The total package, including surgery, hospital stay, and initial physiotherapy, typically costs 75-80% less than in the United States.

## Procedure Details

The treatment pathway begins with a thorough clinical assessment and imaging (X-ray, MRI, ultrasound) to establish an accurate diagnosis. Many sports injuries are managed conservatively with a structured protocol of rest, immobilisation, physiotherapy, and gradual return-to-sport progression. Non-surgical treatments may also include PRP injections, corticosteroid injections, or extracorporeal shockwave therapy.

When surgery is indicated, the majority of sports injury procedures are performed arthroscopically (keyhole surgery). Common surgical interventions include ACL reconstruction using a hamstring or patellar tendon graft, meniscal repair or trimming, rotator cuff repair, labral repair, Achilles tendon repair, and cartilage restoration techniques (microfracture, OATS). These minimally invasive approaches result in smaller scars, less pain, and faster recovery compared to open surgery.

Post-operative rehabilitation is a critical component of sports injury treatment. A phased physiotherapy programme begins immediately after surgery, progressing from pain and swelling control to range-of-motion restoration, strengthening, proprioception training, and finally sport-specific drills. Return-to-sport testing ensures the athlete meets objective criteria before resuming competitive activity. Full recovery timelines range from 4 weeks for minor injuries to 6-9 months for ACL reconstruction.
