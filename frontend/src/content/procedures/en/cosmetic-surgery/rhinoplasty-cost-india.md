## Overview

If you've been thinking about changing the shape of your nose — whether it's something that's bothered you for years or the result of an injury — you probably have lots of questions about what the surgery involves. Rhinoplasty, often called a "nose job," is one of the most common cosmetic procedures in the world, and it can do much more than change how you look. It can also fix breathing problems caused by a deviated septum or nasal trauma.

In simple terms, rhinoplasty reshapes your nose to bring it into better balance with the rest of your face. Your surgeon can make your nose smaller or larger, refine the bridge or tip, narrow your nostrils, or adjust the angle between your nose and upper lip. If you also struggle with breathing, those functional issues can be corrected at the same time.

## Who Is This Procedure For?

Rhinoplasty is suitable for adults over 18 whose nasal structure has fully developed. This age guideline exists because your nose continues to grow during adolescence, and operating on a nose that's still changing wouldn't give you a stable, lasting result.

You'll be an ideal candidate if you're in good overall health with no active infections, chronic nasal conditions, or uncontrolled medical issues. If you have conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure, or a bleeding disorder, they need to be well-managed before surgery.

The most important thing is having realistic expectations. Rhinoplasty can dramatically improve your facial harmony and fix functional problems, but it can't create a "perfect" nose or exactly replicate someone else's features. During your consultation in Bangalore, your surgeon will use 3D imaging to show you what's genuinely achievable for your face.

Whether you're seeking correction because of genetics, an injury, or a previous surgery that didn't give you the result you wanted, you're a potential candidate. If you smoke, you'll need to stop 4-6 weeks before and after surgery, as smoking seriously impairs healing.

## Procedure Details

It's natural to feel anxious about what will happen during surgery. Rhinoplasty is performed using one of two techniques, and your surgeon will recommend the best one for your specific nose.

The **open approach** involves a small incision across the columella — the strip of skin between your nostrils. This gives your surgeon a clear view of the entire nasal structure, which is helpful for complex reshaping or revision surgery. There is a tiny external scar, but it typically fades over time.

The **closed approach** places all incisions inside your nostrils, so there are no visible external scars. This works well for straightforward adjustments and usually means less swelling and a quicker recovery.

The surgery takes 1-3 hours under general or local anaesthesia. Your surgeon reshapes the bone and cartilage to create your new contour. If you need breathing correction, a septoplasty or turbinate reduction can be done at the same time. In some cases, your surgeon may take a small cartilage graft from your septum, ear, or rib to add structural support.

After surgery, a splint is placed on the outside of your nose, and internal splints or soft packing may support your septum. Swelling and bruising will be most noticeable in the first few days, then gradually fade over 1-2 weeks. Most people return to work within 10-14 days, though you'll need to hold off on strenuous activity for 4-6 weeks.

## What Happens During the Procedure

On the day of your surgery, you'll be admitted to the hospital and taken to a pre-operative area. The anaesthesiologist will check your vital signs and give you pre-medication to help you feel calm. Your surgeon will confirm the surgical plan with you, mark the incision sites, and take final reference photographs.

Once you're under anaesthesia, you won't feel anything. Your surgeon will make the planned incisions — either inside your nostrils (closed) or across the columella (open). The skin is gently lifted from the underlying bone and cartilage so your surgeon can see the nasal framework clearly. Then the reshaping begins: removing excess tissue, adding cartilage grafts for support if needed, or performing precise osteotomies to narrow the bridge. If you're having breathing correction, the deviated septum is straightened.

Once reshaping is complete, the skin is redraped and the incisions are closed with fine sutures. A splint is placed on the outside of your nose, and internal splints or soft packing may be positioned to support your septum. The entire procedure takes 1-3 hours, after which you'll be transferred to the recovery room.

## Post-Operative Care & Recovery

**Will it hurt?** You'll have some discomfort, but it's manageable with prescribed pain medication. Most people describe it more as pressure and congestion than sharp pain. You'll stay in the hospital for 1-3 days, where nursing staff will manage your pain and make sure your splint stays secure.

Keep your head elevated at all times to help reduce swelling. For the first few days, breathing through your nose will be difficult because of internal swelling and packing, so you'll need to breathe through your mouth.

The swelling and bruising around your eyes and nose will be most noticeable in the first 48-72 hours, then gradually fade over 1-2 weeks. Cold compresses applied gently around (not directly on) your nose can help.

One important thing: avoid blowing your nose, sneezing through your nose, or doing anything that increases nasal pressure for at least 2 weeks. If you need to sneeze, try to do it through your mouth.

Your surgeon will see you for a follow-up at 7-10 days to remove the external splint and any external sutures. Most people feel ready to return to work and social activities within 10-14 days.

You'll need to avoid strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, and contact sports for 4-6 weeks. Protect your nose from the sun for several months, as UV rays can cause pigmentation changes in healing skin. Some residual swelling — particularly in the tip of your nose — may linger for several months.

## Long-Term Outcomes & Success Rates

You're probably wondering: "Will I be happy with the result?" The odds are in your favour. Over 90% of people who have primary rhinoplasty achieve their desired outcome without needing revision surgery. When breathing corrections are combined with cosmetic reshaping, most people report significant improvements not just in how they look, but in their breathing, sleep quality, and overall confidence.

**When will you see the final result?** Be patient with yourself. Your nose will continue to refine over 6-12 months as residual swelling gradually disappears, with subtle changes possible for up to 18 months — especially in the nasal tip. While your nose will continue to age naturally like the rest of your face, the structural improvements from rhinoplasty are permanent.

Revision rhinoplasty is needed in about 5-10% of cases, usually for minor asymmetries or incomplete correction. Bangalore's leading surgeons have extensive experience with both primary and revision rhinoplasty, with revision rates comparable to top international centres.

## Alternatives to Consider

Before committing to surgical rhinoplasty, it's worth knowing about non-surgical options. **Liquid rhinoplasty** uses injectable dermal fillers (usually hyaluronic acid-based) to smooth bumps, refine the tip, and improve symmetry. If you're looking for minor adjustments and want to avoid surgical downtime, this might suit you. However, results are temporary (lasting 6-12 months), it cannot make your nose smaller or improve breathing, and it carries its own risks.

If your main concern is breathing difficulty rather than appearance, **septoplasty alone** — without external reshaping — may be enough for you. This less invasive procedure straightens your deviated septum to improve airflow, with a shorter recovery and no visible changes to the outside of your nose.

If you have mild nasal imperfections and feel hesitant about surgery, thread lifting or radiofrequency treatments can offer subtle tip refinement, though their effectiveness is limited.

The best way to decide is to have a consultation with a qualified surgeon in Bangalore. They'll assess your anatomy, listen to your goals, and help you choose the approach that's right for you.
