## Overview

Rhinoplasty, commonly known as nose surgery, is one of the most frequently performed cosmetic procedures worldwide. It involves reshaping the nose to improve facial harmony, correct congenital deformities, or address functional breathing issues caused by a deviated septum or nasal trauma. In India, rhinoplasty is performed by highly skilled, board-certified plastic and maxillofacial surgeons using advanced surgical techniques and state-of-the-art equipment.

For international patients, Bangalore has emerged as a leading destination for affordable rhinoplasty. The city is home to several JCI-accredited hospitals and specialised cosmetic surgery centres that offer world-class surgical care at a fraction of the cost found in Western countries. With costs ranging from $1,500 to $5,000 — compared to $8,000 or more in the USA — patients can achieve excellent results while saving up to 70%.

## Procedure Details

Rhinoplasty can be performed using either an open or closed technique. In the open approach, a small incision is made across the columella (the tissue between the nostrils), allowing the surgeon full visibility of the nasal structure. The closed approach involves incisions made entirely inside the nostrils, leaving no visible external scars. The choice of technique depends on the complexity of the case and the surgeon's preference.

During the procedure, which typically lasts 1-3 hours under general or local anaesthesia, the surgeon reshapes the bone and cartilage to achieve the desired contour. If functional correction is needed, septoplasty or turbinate reduction may be performed simultaneously. Cartilage grafts may be taken from the septum, ear, or rib if additional structural support is required.

After surgery, a splint is applied to the nose to maintain the new shape and protect it during initial healing. Patients experience swelling and bruising around the eyes and nose, which gradually subsides over 1-2 weeks. Nasal packing, if used, is removed within 1-2 days. Most patients can return to work within 10-14 days, though strenuous activity should be avoided for 4-6 weeks.

International patients travelling to Bangalore for rhinoplasty require an e-Medical Visa, which can be applied for online through the Indian government's e-Visa portal. The visa is typically issued within 72 hours and is valid for 60 days with triple entry.
