## Overview

Deciding to have breast augmentation is a deeply personal choice. You may have been thinking about it for years — perhaps you have always felt self-conscious about your breast size, or you have noticed changes after pregnancy, breastfeeding or weight loss that have affected your confidence. Whatever your reason, it is important to feel informed and supported throughout the process. This guide will walk you through what breast augmentation in Bangalore involves, so you can make a decision that feels right for you.

Breast augmentation (also known as augmentation mammoplasty) enhances the size, shape and fullness of your breasts using implants. It is sought by women who wish to increase breast volume, correct asymmetry, or restore fullness lost after pregnancy, weight loss or ageing. The procedure uses FDA-approved silicone or saline implants placed either behind your breast tissue or beneath your chest muscle, depending on your anatomy and your surgeon's recommendation.

In India, breast augmentation is performed by highly qualified plastic surgeons in JCI-accredited hospitals and specialised cosmetic surgery centres in Bangalore. With costs ranging from $2,500 to $6,000 — compared to $6,000 to $12,000 in the USA — you can access premium implant brands and expert surgical care at a fraction of the cost. The savings remain substantial even after accounting for international flights, accommodation and travel expenses.

Bangalore has emerged as a preferred hub for medical tourists because of its concentration of board-certified plastic surgeons, modern hospital infrastructure and English-speaking medical staff. Surgeons in the city routinely perform hundreds of augmentation procedures each year and are skilled in the latest techniques, including dual-plane placement and rapid recovery protocols.

## Who Is This Procedure For?

Breast augmentation is suitable if you are a woman over 18 years of age for saline implants and over 22 years for silicone implants, per FDA guidelines. Ideal candidates are in good overall physical health with no active infections, untreated breast conditions, or uncontrolled chronic medical issues. If you have a family history of breast cancer, you should discuss screening and implant imaging implications with your surgeon beforehand.

The procedure is commonly sought by women who wish to enhance breast volume after pregnancy or breastfeeding, correct noticeable asymmetry, restore fullness lost after significant weight loss, or simply increase breast size for improved body confidence. Women who have undergone mastectomy may also be candidates for augmentation as part of breast reconstruction, though this involves a separate surgical pathway.

The best candidates have realistic expectations. While the procedure can dramatically improve breast size, shape and symmetry, it cannot create a "perfect" result or replicate someone else's appearance exactly. Non-smokers or those willing to stop smoking for at least 4 weeks before and after surgery make the best candidates, as smoking significantly impairs healing and increases the risk of complications.

## Pre-Operative Preparation

Once your surgery date is confirmed, your surgeon will schedule a virtual consultation to review your medical history, discuss your aesthetic goals and assess your breast anatomy. You will be asked to provide recent medical records, including any previous breast surgeries, mammograms, biopsies and current medications. The surgeon may request specific photographs taken from multiple angles to aid in pre-operative planning and implant sizing.

Approximately 1–2 weeks before travel, you should complete any pre-operative diagnostic tests recommended by your surgeon. These typically include a complete blood count, coagulation profile, blood chemistry panel and a baseline mammogram or ultrasound for women over 35 or those with a family history of breast cancer.

You should stop taking blood-thinning medications such as aspirin, ibuprofen and certain herbal supplements (including fish oil, vitamin E and ginkgo biloba) at least two weeks before surgery. Smoking must be discontinued 4 weeks before and after the procedure. Arrange for a travel companion, pack loose front-buttoning clothing, and prepare a recovery kit with a surgical bra, cold compresses and prescribed medications.

## Procedure Details

Breast augmentation is performed under general anaesthesia and typically takes 1–2 hours. The surgeon makes an incision in one of several locations — most commonly the inframammary fold (under your breast crease), the periareolar region (around your nipple), or the transaxillary approach (in your armpit). The inframammary approach is most widely used because it provides excellent visibility for precise implant placement and the scar is concealed beneath your breast.

After creating the incision, the surgeon develops a pocket either behind your breast gland (subglandular placement) or beneath your pectoralis major muscle (submuscular placement). Submuscular placement generally provides a more natural upper pole slope and may reduce the risk of capsular contracture, though recovery can be slightly longer. A dual-plane technique, where the upper portion of the implant sits beneath the muscle and the lower portion behind the gland, is increasingly used to combine the benefits of both approaches.

Implant selection is a critical part of the planning process. Surgeons in Bangalore offer FDA-approved silicone and saline implants from leading global brands such as Mentor, Allergan and Sientra. Silicone implants tend to feel more natural and are preferred by most patients, while saline implants allow for smaller incisions and adjustable fill volumes. During your consultation, the surgeon will help you choose the appropriate size, profile and texture based on your body frame, chest width and aesthetic goals. 3D imaging and sizer trials are often used to preview the expected result.

## What Happens During the Procedure

On the day of surgery, you will be admitted and taken to a pre-operative holding area where the anaesthesiologist reviews your vital signs and administers pre-medication. The surgeon confirms the implant size and type, marks the incision sites and takes final reference photographs — this is your last opportunity to ask questions.

Under general anaesthesia, the surgeon makes the planned incisions, creates the surgical pocket, inserts and positions the implant, and checks for symmetry and contour before closing in layers with dissolvable sutures. A surgical support bra is applied to minimise swelling and hold the implants in position. The entire procedure takes 1–2 hours, after which you are transferred to the recovery room and then to your hospital room for an overnight stay.

## Post-Operative Care & Recovery

**Will it hurt?** You will have some discomfort, tightness and soreness in your chest for the first few days, but this is well managed with prescribed pain medication and gradually subsides over the first week. You will be monitored in the hospital for 1–2 days, with nursing staff managing pain, checking for bleeding or infection, and ensuring your surgical bra remains secure.

Swelling, bruising and mild discomfort are most pronounced during the first 48–72 hours and gradually subside over 1–2 weeks. Keep your upper body slightly elevated and avoid raising your arms above shoulder height for the first week. Wear the surgical bra continuously for the first 2–3 weeks, including while sleeping, to support the implants as they settle.

Your surgeon will schedule a follow-up at 7–10 days to assess wound healing, remove any non-dissolvable sutures and check implant position. Most patients return to work and light activities within 7–10 days. Strenuous exercise, heavy lifting and sleeping on the stomach should be avoided for 4–6 weeks. The implants continue to settle and soften over 3–6 months, with final results visible once residual swelling resolves.

Report any concerning symptoms — increasing pain, redness, fever, changes in breast shape, or unusual firmness — to your surgeon immediately. Capsular contracture, the most common long-term complication, can develop months or years after surgery, so ongoing monitoring is important even after you return home.

## Long-Term Outcomes & Success Rates

Breast augmentation has a high success rate, with over 90% of patients reporting satisfaction with both the aesthetic result and the improvement in body confidence when the procedure is performed by an experienced, board-certified surgeon using high-quality implants. Patient satisfaction tends to increase as the implants settle and soften over the first 3–6 months.

Breast implants are not considered lifetime devices. On average, they last 10–15 years, after which they may need to be replaced due to rupture, capsular contracture or changes in aesthetic preference. Regular MRI or ultrasound screenings are recommended to monitor implant integrity, particularly for silicone implants, as a silent rupture may not produce noticeable symptoms.

Revision surgery is needed in approximately 10–15% of cases over the lifetime of the implants, usually due to capsular contracture, implant rupture or size change. Choosing an experienced, board-certified surgeon significantly reduces the likelihood of early complications.

## Alternatives to Consider

Before committing to surgical breast augmentation, consider non-surgical alternatives. Fat transfer breast augmentation uses liposuction to harvest fat from another area (such as your abdomen or thighs) and inject it into your breasts. This suits patients seeking a modest increase in volume (typically one cup size or less) who prefer a natural result without implants — though the volume increase is limited, some fat may be reabsorbed, and multiple sessions may be needed.

If your primary concern is sagging rather than volume, a breast lift (mastopexy) alone may be more appropriate. This reshapes and lifts your breast tissue without adding volume, and may be combined with implants if both lifting and enlargement are desired. A consultation with a qualified surgeon in Bangalore will help determine the most appropriate approach based on your anatomy, goals and risk tolerance.
