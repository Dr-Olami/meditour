## Overview

Breast augmentation, also known as augmentation mammoplasty, is a surgical procedure that enhances the size, shape, and fullness of the breasts using implants. It is one of the most commonly performed cosmetic surgeries worldwide, sought by women who wish to increase breast volume, correct asymmetry, or restore breast fullness lost after pregnancy, weight loss, or ageing. The procedure uses FDA-approved silicone or saline implants placed either behind the breast tissue or beneath the chest muscle.

In India, breast augmentation is performed by highly qualified plastic surgeons in JCI-accredited hospitals and specialised cosmetic surgery centres in Bangalore. With costs ranging from $2,500 to $6,000 — compared to $6,000 to $12,000 in the USA — international patients can access premium implant brands and expert surgical care at a fraction of the cost.

## Procedure Details

Breast augmentation is performed under general anaesthesia and typically takes 1-2 hours. The surgeon makes an incision in one of several locations — most commonly the inframammary fold (under the breast crease), the periareolar region (around the nipple), or the transaxillary approach (in the armpit). The choice of incision depends on the implant type, patient anatomy, and surgeon recommendation.

After creating the incision, the surgeon develops a pocket either behind the breast gland (subglandular placement) or beneath the pectoralis major muscle (submuscular placement). Submuscular placement generally provides a more natural upper pole slope and may reduce the risk of capsular contracture, though recovery can be slightly longer. The implant is carefully positioned and the incisions are closed in layers with dissolvable sutures.

Post-surgery, patients wear a surgical support bra to minimise swelling and support the implants as they settle. A 1-2 day hospital stay is standard for monitoring. Swelling and mild discomfort are expected for the first 1-2 weeks, and most patients can return to non-strenuous work within 7-10 days. Strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, and sleeping on the stomach should be avoided for 4-6 weeks. Final results are typically visible after 3-6 months as the implants settle into their natural position.
