## Overview

A hair transplant is a surgical procedure that restores hair to areas of the scalp affected by male or female pattern baldness, thinning, or scarring. It works by relocating healthy, genetically resistant hair follicles from the donor area (typically the back and sides of the scalp) to the bald or thinning recipient areas. The transplanted hair continues to grow naturally for a lifetime, making it a permanent solution for hair loss.

## Procedure Details

There are two primary hair transplant techniques available in Bangalore: Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) and Follicular Unit Transplantation (FUT). In the FUE technique, the surgeon uses a micro-punch tool (0.6–0.8 mm diameter) to individually extract follicular units from the donor area one at a time. These grafts are then carefully implanted into tiny incisions made in the recipient area. FUE leaves minimal scarring — tiny dot scars that are virtually invisible — and has a faster recovery time, making it the preferred choice for most patients.

In the FUT technique, the surgeon removes a thin strip of scalp from the donor area, typically 1–1.5 cm wide. The strip is then dissected under a microscope into individual follicular units for implantation. FUT leaves a linear scar in the donor area, which is concealed by surrounding hair. FUT can yield a higher number of grafts in a single session and may be recommended for patients requiring extensive coverage or those with tighter scalps where FUE extraction is difficult.

Both procedures are performed under local anaesthesia and take 4–8 hours depending on the number of grafts. A typical session involves 1,500–3,500 grafts. After the procedure, the scalp is bandaged, and patients are given detailed post-operative care instructions. Mild swelling, redness, and scabbing are normal for 1–2 weeks. The transplanted hair typically sheds within 2–4 weeks (shock loss), and new permanent hair growth begins at 3–4 months, with final results visible at 12–18 months.

International patients travelling to Bangalore for a hair transplant require an e-Medical Visa, which can be applied for online through the Indian government's e-Visa portal. The visa is typically issued within 72 hours and is valid for 60 days with triple entry.
