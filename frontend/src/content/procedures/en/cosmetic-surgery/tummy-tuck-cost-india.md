## Overview

A tummy tuck, or abdominoplasty, is a cosmetic surgical procedure that removes excess skin and fat from the abdomen while tightening the underlying abdominal muscles. It is particularly beneficial for individuals who have experienced significant weight loss, pregnancy, or ageing, resulting in loose, sagging abdominal skin and weakened muscles that cannot be corrected through diet and exercise alone.

In India, tummy tuck surgery is performed by experienced, board-certified plastic surgeons in JCI-accredited hospitals in Bangalore. With costs ranging from $2,000 to $5,000 — compared to $6,000 to $12,000 in the USA — international patients can achieve a flatter, firmer abdomen at a substantially lower cost without compromising on surgical quality or safety standards.

## Procedure Details

A full tummy tuck is performed under general anaesthesia and typically takes 2-4 hours. The surgeon makes a horizontal incision across the lower abdomen, usually from hip to hip, positioned just above the pubic area. The skin and fat are then lifted from the abdominal wall, and the underlying rectus abdominis muscles are tightened using sutures to restore a firmer abdominal contour. The excess skin is trimmed, the remaining skin is repositioned, and the belly button is repositioned through a new opening to maintain a natural appearance.

A mini tummy tuck is a less extensive variant suitable for patients with excess skin limited to the lower abdomen. It involves a shorter incision, does not require repositioning of the belly button, and has a shorter recovery time. Your surgeon will determine the most appropriate approach based on the amount of excess skin, muscle laxity, and your aesthetic goals.

After surgery, drains are typically placed to remove excess fluid and are removed after 5-10 days. Patients wear a compression garment for 4-6 weeks to support healing and minimise swelling. A hospital stay of 2-4 days is standard. Walking in a slightly bent position is recommended for the first week to reduce tension on the incision. Most patients return to light activities within 2-3 weeks, with full recovery and clearance for strenuous exercise at 6-8 weeks.

International patients travelling to Bangalore for a tummy tuck require an e-Medical Visa, available through the Indian government's online e-Visa portal. The visa is typically issued within 72 hours and is valid for 60 days with triple entry.
