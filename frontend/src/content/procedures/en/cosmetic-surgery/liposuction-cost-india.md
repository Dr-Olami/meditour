If you have been working hard on your diet and exercise but still have stubborn pockets of fat that just will not shift — on your abdomen, thighs, hips, arms or chin — you are not alone. Liposuction is a popular cosmetic surgical procedure that removes stubborn fat deposits from specific areas of your body to improve contour and proportion. Common treatment areas include the abdomen, thighs, hips, flanks, arms, chin and back. The procedure is ideal if you are close to your ideal body weight but struggle with localised fat pockets that do not respond to diet and exercise.

## Who Is This Procedure For?

Liposuction is best suited if you are an adult within 30% of your ideal body weight and have localised fat deposits that have not responded to diet and exercise. It is a body contouring procedure, not a weight-loss solution, and you should have realistic expectations about what it can achieve.

Good candidates typically have firm, elastic skin that can retract smoothly after fat removal. If you have poor skin elasticity, you may experience loose or sagging skin after the procedure and may be better suited to a combined excisional procedure such as a tummy tuck. The surgeon will assess your skin quality during the consultation and advise accordingly.

If you have chronic conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes, heart disease or bleeding disorders, you may not be a suitable candidate. Smokers are required to quit at least four weeks before and after surgery to reduce the risk of complications and promote healing. Pregnant women and patients with severe obesity are not candidates for liposuction.

## Pre-Operative Preparation

Two to three weeks before surgery, you should complete all recommended blood tests, including a complete blood count, coagulation profile and metabolic panel. A pre-anaesthetic evaluation will be scheduled to confirm your fitness for anaesthesia.

You should stop taking blood-thinning medications such as aspirin, ibuprofen and certain herbal supplements at least two weeks before surgery, as these can increase bleeding risk. Your surgeon will provide a comprehensive list of medications to avoid. Smokers must stop completely at least four weeks prior, as nicotine impairs wound healing and increases the risk of complications.

Arrange for a companion to travel with you, as you will need assistance on the day of discharge and during the first few days of recovery. Prepare loose, comfortable clothing that opens at the front, as you will be wearing compression garments underneath. On the day before surgery, you will be asked to fast for at least eight hours and to shower with an antibacterial soap.

## Procedure Details

Liposuction in Bangalore is performed using several advanced techniques. The most common is tumescent liposuction, where a large volume of saline solution mixed with local anaesthetic and epinephrine is injected into your target area. This solution constricts blood vessels, reduces bleeding and provides pain relief, allowing for smoother fat removal with minimal trauma. Other available techniques include VASER (ultrasound-assisted) liposuction, which uses sound waves to emulsify fat for more precise contouring, and laser-assisted liposuction (SmartLipo), which also promotes skin tightening.

The procedure typically takes 1–3 hours depending on the number and size of areas being treated. Small incisions (3–5 mm) are made near the target sites, and a thin cannula is inserted to loosen and suction out excess fat. The procedure is usually performed under general anaesthesia or local anaesthesia with sedation, depending on the extent of treatment.

After the procedure, compression garments are applied to your treated areas to minimise swelling and help your skin conform to the new contours. Liposuction is generally a day-care procedure, meaning you can be discharged the same day. Mild swelling, bruising and discomfort are normal for the first 1–2 weeks. The compression garment should be worn continuously for 2–4 weeks, and final results become visible after 3–6 months as residual swelling resolves completely.

## What Happens During the Procedure

On the day of surgery, you will be admitted to the hospital or clinic and prepared in the surgical suite. The anaesthetist will administer either general anaesthesia or local anaesthesia with sedation, depending on the number of areas being treated and your surgeon's recommendation. Once you are comfortable and monitored, the surgeon will mark your target areas with a surgical marker while you are standing to ensure accurate contouring.

For tumescent liposuction, the surgeon injects the saline-anaesthetic solution into each target area, waiting approximately 20–30 minutes for it to take full effect. Small incisions are then made, and a thin cannula is inserted to loosen and suction out the excess fat using controlled back-and-forth movements. For VASER liposuction, an ultrasound probe is first used to emulsify the fat before suction, allowing for gentler removal and enhanced precision around delicate areas.

**Will it hurt?** Under general anaesthesia you will not feel anything during the procedure. With local anaesthesia and sedation, you may feel some pressure or movement but should not feel pain. After the procedure, you will have soreness, bruising and swelling in the treated areas, similar to the feeling after an intense workout, but this is well managed with prescribed oral analgesics.

Throughout the procedure, the surgical team monitors your vital signs, fluid balance and the total volume of fat removed. Once the desired contour is achieved, the incisions may be left open to drain or closed with a single suture, and compression garments are applied immediately. The entire procedure typically takes 1–3 hours, after which you are moved to the recovery room for observation before being discharged the same day.

## Post-Operative Care & Recovery

After discharge, you are advised to rest and limit movement for the first 24–48 hours. Compression garments must be worn continuously for 2–4 weeks to minimise swelling, reduce fluid accumulation and help your skin conform to the new contours. Mild swelling, bruising and numbness in the treated areas are normal and typically subside within 2–4 weeks.

You should walk short distances within a day of the procedure to promote circulation and reduce the risk of blood clots, but strenuous activity and heavy lifting must be avoided for at least four weeks. A follow-up appointment is scheduled within 7–10 days to assess healing, remove any sutures and clear you for international travel.

Lymphatic drainage massage may be recommended after the first week to accelerate the reduction of swelling and improve contour smoothness. You should keep the incision sites clean and dry and watch for signs of infection such as increasing redness, warmth or discharge. Staying well-hydrated and maintaining a balanced diet supports the healing process.

## Long-Term Outcomes & Success Rates

Liposuction delivers permanent fat reduction in the treated areas, as the removed fat cells do not regenerate. However, maintaining a stable weight is essential — significant weight gain can cause remaining fat cells in untreated areas to expand, altering your overall contour. When combined with a healthy lifestyle, the results are long-lasting and consistently rated highly by patients.

Studies report patient satisfaction rates of over 85% for liposuction, with the majority of patients reporting improved body confidence and comfort in clothing. Final results become visible 3–6 months after surgery once all residual swelling resolves and your skin fully retracts. Skin tightening effects are more pronounced with VASER and SmartLipo techniques, particularly in patients with good skin elasticity.

Complications are uncommon when the procedure is performed by an experienced surgeon in an accredited facility. The most frequently reported issues are minor contour irregularities, which can often be corrected with a small touch-up procedure if needed.

Schedule a visit with your local physician within two weeks to confirm that healing is on track and to address any concerns. ## Alternatives to Consider

If liposuction is not the right fit, several alternatives may be more appropriate depending on your goals. Non-surgical fat reduction options such as cryolipolysis (CoolSculpting) and radiofrequency treatments can reduce small fat pockets with no downtime, though results are more modest and gradual. If you have significant loose skin, a tummy tuck or body lift may deliver better results than liposuction alone.

If you are seeking overall weight loss rather than contouring, bariatric surgery procedures such as gastric sleeve or gastric bypass are more suitable. During your consultation, the surgeon will discuss all viable options and recommend the approach that best matches your anatomy, expectations and health profile.
