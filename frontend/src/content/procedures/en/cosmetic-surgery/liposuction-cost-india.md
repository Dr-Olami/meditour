## Overview

Liposuction is a popular cosmetic surgical procedure that removes stubborn fat deposits from specific areas of the body to improve contour and proportion. Common treatment areas include the abdomen, thighs, hips, flanks, arms, chin, and back. The procedure is ideal for individuals who are close to their ideal body weight but struggle with localised fat pockets that do not respond to diet and exercise.

In India, liposuction is performed by board-certified plastic surgeons in modern, well-equipped hospitals and cosmetic surgery centres in Bangalore. With costs ranging from $1,200 to $4,000 — compared to $3,000 to $10,000 in the USA — international patients can achieve their body contouring goals at a significantly lower cost without compromising on quality or safety.

## Procedure Details

Liposuction in Bangalore is performed using several advanced techniques. The most common is tumescent liposuction, where a large volume of saline solution mixed with local anaesthetic and epinephrine is injected into the target area. This solution constricts blood vessels, reduces bleeding, and provides pain relief, allowing for smoother fat removal with minimal trauma. Other available techniques include VASER (ultrasound-assisted) liposuction, which uses sound waves to emulsify fat for more precise contouring, and laser-assisted liposuction (SmartLipo), which also promotes skin tightening.

The procedure typically takes 1-3 hours depending on the number and size of areas being treated. Small incisions (3-5 mm) are made near the target sites, and a thin cannula is inserted to loosen and suction out excess fat. The procedure is usually performed under general anaesthesia or local anaesthesia with sedation, depending on the extent of treatment.

After the procedure, compression garments are applied to the treated areas to minimise swelling and help the skin conform to the new contours. Liposuction is generally a day-care procedure, meaning patients can be discharged the same day. Mild swelling, bruising, and discomfort are normal for the first 1-2 weeks. The compression garment should be worn continuously for 2-4 weeks, and final results become visible after 3-6 months as residual swelling resolves completely.
