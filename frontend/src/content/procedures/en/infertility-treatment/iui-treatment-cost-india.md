## Overview

IUI (Intrauterine Insemination) is a minimally invasive fertility treatment that involves placing washed and concentrated sperm directly into the uterus at the time of ovulation. By bypassing the cervix and increasing the number of sperm that reach the fallopian tubes, IUI improves the likelihood of fertilisation for couples with mild fertility issues.

IUI is often recommended as a first-line treatment before progressing to more advanced options like IVF or ICSI. It is particularly suitable for couples with unexplained infertility, mild male-factor infertility, or cervical mucus issues, and for single women or same-sex couples using donor sperm.

In India, IUI treatment costs between $300 and $800 per cycle, making it one of the most accessible and affordable fertility treatments available to international patients. Bangalore's fertility clinics combine clinical expertise, modern laboratory facilities, and patient-centred care to deliver excellent outcomes at a fraction of Western prices.

## Procedure Details

An IUI cycle typically begins on day 2 or 3 of the menstrual cycle, when the patient undergoes a baseline ultrasound and blood tests. Depending on the treatment protocol, the patient may take oral ovulation-inducing medications (such as letrozole or clomiphene) or mild injectable hormones to encourage the development of one or two mature follicles.

Follicular growth is monitored through serial transvaginal ultrasounds over 10-14 days. Once the lead follicle reaches approximately 18-20mm in diameter, a trigger injection of hCG is administered to induce ovulation. The insemination is scheduled 24-36 hours after the trigger, timed to coincide with the release of the egg.

On the day of the procedure, a semen sample is processed in the laboratory to isolate the healthiest, most motile sperm through a technique known as sperm washing. The prepared sample is loaded into a thin catheter, which is gently passed through the cervix into the uterus, where the sperm is deposited. The procedure takes only a few minutes and requires no anaesthesia. Patients rest briefly before returning to normal activities, and a pregnancy test is performed approximately 14 days later.

## Travel & Visa

International patients travelling to Bangalore for IUI treatment can apply for an e-Medical Visa through the Indian government's online portal. The visa is typically valid for 60 days and is sufficient for one or more IUI cycles. Patients should carry their fertility evaluation records, semen analysis reports, and any prior treatment history.

Bangalore's Kempegowda International Airport offers excellent connectivity with direct and connecting flights from major international cities. Since an IUI cycle spans 2-4 weeks, patients can plan a relatively short stay. Fertility clinics assist with appointment scheduling, local accommodation, and coordination of monitoring visits to ensure the treatment timeline aligns with the patient's travel plans.
