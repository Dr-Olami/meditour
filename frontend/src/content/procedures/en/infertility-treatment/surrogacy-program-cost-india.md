## Overview

Surrogacy is a form of assisted reproduction in which a woman (the surrogate) carries a pregnancy on behalf of intended parents who are unable to conceive or carry a child to term. The process typically involves IVF to create embryos using the intended parents' gametes (or donor gametes), which are then transferred into the surrogate's uterus.

In India, surrogacy is governed by the Surrogacy (Regulation) Act 2021, which fundamentally changed the legal landscape. The Act prohibits commercial surrogacy and permits only altruistic surrogacy for Indian citizen couples who meet strict eligibility criteria. Importantly, international surrogacy is no longer permitted—foreign nationals and OCI cardholders cannot pursue surrogacy in India.

The cost of an altruistic surrogacy program in India ranges from $10,000 to $25,000, covering IVF procedures, medical expenses for the surrogate, legal formalities, and pregnancy care. This is significantly lower than costs in countries where commercial surrogacy is legal, such as the USA. However, international patients should be aware of the legal restrictions and seek appropriate counsel before considering India as a surrogacy destination.

## Procedure Details

For eligible Indian couples, the surrogacy process begins with a thorough medical evaluation to establish the need for surrogacy, such as absence of the uterus, severe uterine damage, or repeated failed IVF attempts. The couple must obtain a certificate of indication from a registered medical practitioner and fulfil all legal prerequisites under the Surrogacy (Regulation) Act 2021.

A surrogate is selected from among close relatives of the intended parents, in accordance with the Act. The surrogate must be a married woman aged 25-35 years with at least one living child of her own and must act altruistically, receiving only medical and insurance coverage—not commercial compensation. Both parties must enter into a legally binding surrogacy agreement approved by the appropriate authorities.

The medical process follows standard IVF protocols: ovarian stimulation of the intended mother (or egg donor), egg retrieval, fertilisation with the intended father's sperm, and embryo transfer into the surrogate. Pregnancy is monitored through regular ultrasounds and blood tests. Throughout the pregnancy, the surrogate receives comprehensive medical care, and upon delivery, the child is handed over to the intended parents as per the legal agreement. Parental rights are established through court-ordered parentage orders post-delivery.

## Travel & Visa

Under the Surrogacy (Regulation) Act 2021, foreign nationals and OCI cardholders are not eligible to pursue surrogacy in India. International patients seeking surrogacy should consult legal experts and consider countries where commercial surrogacy is legally permitted for foreigners.

Eligible Indian citizen couples residing abroad may travel to Bangalore for surrogacy treatment under a medical visa. The process requires extensive documentation, including the certificate of medical indication, surrogacy agreement, and regulatory approvals. Bangalore's fertility clinics assist eligible couples with legal coordination, documentation, and medical scheduling. Given the 12-18 month timeline, couples should plan for multiple visits to Bangalore for IVF procedures, embryo transfer, key pregnancy scans, and ultimately, delivery and legal formalities for parentage establishment.
