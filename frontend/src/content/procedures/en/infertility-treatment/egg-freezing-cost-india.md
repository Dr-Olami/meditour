## Overview

Egg freezing, or oocyte cryopreservation, is a fertility preservation procedure that allows women to retrieve, freeze, and store their eggs for future use. By freezing eggs at a younger age, women can preserve their fertility potential, increasing the likelihood of successful conception later in life when natural egg quality and quantity may have declined.

The procedure is increasingly sought by women who wish to delay childbearing for personal, professional, or medical reasons, including those undergoing cancer treatments that may damage ovarian function. Egg freezing empowers women to take control of their reproductive timeline without the pressure of a biological clock.

In India, egg freezing costs between $1,500 and $3,500 per cycle, making it significantly more affordable than in Western countries. Bangalore's fertility clinics utilise advanced vitrification technology and internationally standardised protocols, ensuring high egg survival and fertilisation rates for international patients.

## Procedure Details

The egg freezing process begins with a comprehensive fertility assessment, including anti-Müllerian hormone (AMH) testing, antral follicle count via ultrasound, and a review of medical history. This evaluation helps determine ovarian reserve and guides the stimulation protocol tailored to the patient's individual response profile.

Ovarian stimulation involves daily hormone injections for approximately 10-14 days to encourage the development of multiple mature follicles. Follicular growth is closely monitored through serial transvaginal ultrasounds and blood oestrogen levels. Once follicles reach optimal size, a trigger injection is administered to finalise egg maturation, and egg retrieval is scheduled 34-36 hours later.

Egg retrieval is performed under light sedation as a day-care procedure using ultrasound-guided transvaginal aspiration. Retrieved eggs are examined by an embryologist, and mature eggs are cryopreserved using vitrification—a rapid freezing technique that prevents ice crystal formation and preserves cellular integrity. Frozen eggs are stored in liquid nitrogen at -196°C until the patient is ready to use them, at which point they are thawed and fertilised using ICSI as part of a future IVF cycle.

## Travel & Visa

International patients travelling to Bangalore for egg freezing can apply for an e-Medical Visa through the Indian government's online portal. The visa is typically valid for 60 days, which is sufficient for a single egg freezing cycle. Patients should carry their fertility evaluation records, AMH test results, and any relevant medical history.

Bangalore is accessible via Kempegowda International Airport with excellent global connectivity. An egg freezing cycle spans 2-4 weeks, making it a manageable trip for international patients. Fertility clinics assist with appointment scheduling, accommodation, and coordination of monitoring visits. Patients should plan to stay for 1-2 weeks post-retrieval to ensure ovarian recovery before travelling home. Annual storage fees and future thaw-and-fertilisation costs should be discussed with the clinic during the initial consultation.
