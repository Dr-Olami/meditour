## Overview

ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection) is a specialised form of IVF that addresses male-factor infertility by injecting a single, carefully selected sperm directly into the centre of a mature egg. This micromanipulation technique dramatically improves fertilisation rates when sperm count, motility, or morphology is compromised, or when previous conventional IVF cycles have failed to achieve fertilisation.

For international patients, ICSI treatment in India—particularly in Bangalore—offers world-class fertility care at a fraction of the cost found in Western countries. Leading fertility centres in Bangalore are equipped with state-of-the-art embryology laboratories, highly trained reproductive medicine specialists, and internationally accredited protocols that match global standards.

The cost of ICSI treatment in India ranges from $2,000 to $5,000 per cycle, compared to $15,000 or more in the USA. This affordability, combined with high clinical success rates and comprehensive patient support services, makes Bangalore a preferred destination for medical tourists seeking advanced fertility solutions.

## Procedure Details

An ICSI cycle begins with ovarian stimulation, where the female partner receives hormone injections to encourage the development of multiple mature eggs. Progress is monitored through transvaginal ultrasound and blood tests over approximately 10-14 days. Once follicles reach optimal size, a trigger injection is administered to finalise egg maturation.

Egg retrieval is performed under light sedation as a day-care procedure, using ultrasound-guided transvaginal aspiration. On the same day, a semen sample is collected (or surgically retrieved if necessary), and the embryologist selects the most morphologically normal, motile sperm. Using a high-powered microscope and micromanipulation tools, a single sperm is immobilised and injected directly into each mature egg.

Fertilised eggs are cultured in the laboratory for 3-5 days, developing into embryos. One or two selected embryos are then transferred into the uterus in a quick, painless procedure. Any remaining high-quality embryos can be frozen for future use. After transfer, patients rest briefly before resuming normal activity, with a pregnancy test scheduled approximately 10-14 days later.

## Travel & Visa

International patients travelling to Bangalore for ICSI treatment typically require a medical visa (e-Medical Visa), which can be applied for online through the Indian government's e-Visa portal. The visa is usually granted for up to 60 days and allows entry for medical treatment at recognised healthcare facilities. Patients should carry their medical records, referral letters, and clinic appointment confirmation.

Bangalore is well-connected by Kempegowda International Airport, with direct flights from major global hubs. Fertility clinics typically assist with visa documentation, airport pickup, and accommodation arrangements. Since an ICSI cycle spans 4-6 weeks, patients should plan for an extended stay, with the option for the partner to accompany during key appointments such as egg retrieval and embryo transfer.
