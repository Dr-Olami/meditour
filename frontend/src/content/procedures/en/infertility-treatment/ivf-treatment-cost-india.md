## Overview

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) is an assisted reproductive technology in which eggs are retrieved from the ovaries and fertilised with sperm in a controlled laboratory environment. The resulting embryos are cultured for 3-5 days and one or two are transferred into the uterus, with any remaining good-quality embryos frozen for future use. IVF is the most effective fertility treatment for tubal factor infertility, male factor infertility, endometriosis, and unexplained infertility.

In Bangalore, IVF is performed at JCI- and NABH-accredited fertility centres by reproductive medicine specialists with international training. ## Procedure Details

A standard IVF cycle begins with ovarian stimulation using daily injections of follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) and luteinising hormone (LH) for approximately 10-12 days. During this phase, patients attend the clinic every 2-3 days for transvaginal ultrasound monitoring of follicle growth and blood oestradiol levels. The stimulation protocol—long, short, antagonist, or mild—is selected based on ovarian reserve markers (AMH, AFC), age, and prior cycle history.

Once follicles reach 17-18 mm, a trigger injection of human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) is given and egg retrieval is scheduled 34-36 hours later. Oocyte pick-up (OPU) is performed under short general anaesthesia or sedation using transvaginal ultrasound guidance, taking about 20-30 minutes. Retrieved eggs are inseminated with prepared sperm in the embryology lab, and fertilisation is confirmed the next morning. Embryos are cultured to the cleavage (day 3) or blastocyst (day 5) stage, and one or two are transferred into the uterus through a thin catheter—a painless day-care procedure.

After embryo transfer, patients receive luteal-phase support with progesterone pessaries or injections. A blood pregnancy test (beta-hCG) is performed 10-14 days after transfer. If positive, an ultrasound is scheduled at 6-7 weeks to confirm a gestational sac and fetal heartbeat. Surplus embryos are vitrified for potential frozen embryo transfers, which are less expensive and equally effective in selected patients.
