## About the Doctor

Dr. Sunil Narayan Dutt is a highly experienced ENT Specialist based in Bangalore, Karnataka, with a remarkable 30 years of practice in the field of Ear, Nose, and Throat medicine. He holds an extensive array of qualifications, including MS (ENT), DNB (ORL), DLO, RCS, FRCS ED, FRCS (ORL-HNS), and a PhD, demonstrating his commitment to excellence and continuous learning in otolaryngology.

Dr. Dutt has trained and practiced ENT both in India and abroad. He is a Faculty Mentor for South Asia for Cochlear Implants, Bone Anchored Hearing Aids, and Balloon Sinuplasty.

He was the Founder and Founder-Secretary of the Cochlear Implant Group of India (CIGI) in 2003 and performed the first pediatric cochlear implant in Bangalore in 2003.

He has to his credit of performing over 80 cochlear implants at the Apollo Deafness Center, Bangalore. He is perhaps one of few surgeons in the country to also receive a PhD. He was awarded a PhD for dissertation on Patient Satisfaction and Quality of Life Outcomes with the Bone Anchored Hearing Aids — the Birmingham Experience (University of Nijmegen, Holland, 2002).

Fluent in multiple languages including English, Hindi, and Kannada, Dr. Dutt ensures effective communication with a diverse range of patients, providing personalized care tailored to their needs. His dedication to patient health and well-being is evident in his approach, combining advanced medical techniques with compassionate care.

As a member of the prestigious Apollo network, Dr. Dutt has access to cutting-edge technology and resources, enabling him to offer comprehensive treatments for various ENT conditions. His specialization allows him to address common ailments such as allergies, sinusitis, hearing loss, and more complex disorders requiring surgical intervention.

Respected among his peers and patients, Dr. Dutt is well-regarded in the community for his expertise and knowledgeable insights into ENT issues, making him a trusted choice for individuals seeking the highest standard of care in this specialty.
