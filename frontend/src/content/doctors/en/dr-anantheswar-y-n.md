## About the Doctor

Dr. Anantheswar Y N is the HOD & Consultant of Plastic, Cosmetic & Reconstructive Surgery at Manipal Hospital Old Airport Road, Bangalore. With a long range of credentials and affiliations, he is well-known for his expertise and contributions to the medical community. Holding multiple degrees including MBBS, MS, MCh, and DNB in Plastic Surgery, Dr. Anantheswar demonstrates his dedication to academic excellence and specialisation in his field. He is widely regarded as one of the best cosmetic surgeons in Bangalore.

He is a fellow and member of several distinguished organisations including the Association of Plastic Surgeons of India, the Association of Surgeons of India, and the Association of Brachial Plexus Society of India. As a devoted professional, Dr. Anantheswar has made widespread contributions to the advancement of scientific knowledge. He is a founder member of the Association of Palate, Cleft Lip & Craniofacial Anomalies of India and the Skull Base Society of India. He additionally serves as an editorial member for the International Journal of Craniomaxillofacial Trauma & Reconstruction and is the editor of the International Journal of Plastic Surgery & Transplantation.

Dr. Anantheswar's expertise spans cosmetic surgery, microvascular and hand reconstructive surgery, craniofacial surgery, and plastic surgery. He is a sought-after consultant for patients seeking specialised care and treatment due to his extensive knowledge and skill in those fields. In addition to his professional achievements, he speaks Kannada, Marathi, English, Telugu, and Hindi fluently.

He has authored numerous articles in reputable medical journals and has delivered talks on various topics related to his field. His publications cover a wide range of subjects, from tissue expansion techniques in the closure of myelomeningocele to facial granulomas following the injection of fillers containing acrylic hydrogel particles. His commitment to education and sharing knowledge is evident in his involvement as an international faculty member of Craniomaxillofacial Surgeons, AO, Switzerland, and his role as the president-elect of KAPRAS (Karnataka Association of Plastic & Reconstructive Surgeons) for 2020.

Dr. Anantheswar's expertise and dedication have earned him recognition and respect from colleagues and patients alike. His compassionate approach to patient care, combined with his extensive knowledge and skill set, makes him a trusted professional in the field of plastic, reconstructive, and cosmetic surgery.
