## About the Doctor

Dr. Vidya Bhushan R is an experienced Head & Neck Oncosurgeon at SPARSH Hospital, Hennur Road, Bangalore, specialising in head and neck cancer surgery and advanced reconstruction. Many patients searching for the best oncologist in Bangalore consider Dr. Vidya Bhushan for his focused expertise and careful management of complex cancer cases.

He completed his medical training at St. John's Medical College, Bangalore, followed by a Fellowship in Head and Neck Surgical Oncology from Mazumdar Shaw Cancer Centre, Narayana Health, and advanced training at Christian Medical College, Vellore.

Since joining Mazumdar Shaw Cancer Centre in 2017, he has performed over 1,500 advanced head and neck cancer resections and reconstructions, including minimally invasive, robotic, and endoscopic skull base procedures. He is also actively involved in research and academic activities.
