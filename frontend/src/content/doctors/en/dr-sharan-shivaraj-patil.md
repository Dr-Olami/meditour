## About the Doctor

Dr. Sharan is a renowned Orthopaedic Surgeon based in Bangalore who has gained advanced medical training in the U.K., where he acquired valuable experience at leading institutions specialising in paediatric and complex orthopaedic care. His expertise and commitment have significantly impacted orthopaedics in Bangalore, where he has performed over 5,000 major complex surgical procedures, focusing on Paediatric Orthopaedics, Joint Replacement, and Complex Trauma. Recognised as one of the best joint replacement surgeons in Bangalore, Dr. Sharan's skills have earned him a strong reputation in the field.

Driven by a commitment to enhancing healthcare, Dr. Sharan established SPARSH Hospital in 2006. This facility, equipped with 120 beds and 5 operating rooms, is capable of performing over 30 surgeries daily. Under his leadership, SPARSH Hospital has grown to offer premium-quality care and has become a prominent name among the best orthopaedic doctors in the region. His vision and leadership have broadened the accessibility of top-tier orthopaedic care across Tier 1 and Tier 2 regions in Karnataka.
