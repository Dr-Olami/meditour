## About the Doctor

Dr Naveen Rao is a distinguished Plastic and Cosmetic Surgeon based at Apollo Hospitals Bannerghatta Road, Bangalore. With an impressive 19 years of experience in the medical field, he has dedicated his career to providing exceptional cosmetic and reconstructive surgery services to patients from India and abroad. He holds MBBS, MS, MCh, and a Fellowship in Cosmetic Surgery, showcasing his extensive training and commitment to medical excellence. He is proficient in English, Hindi, and Kannada, ensuring effective communication with a diverse range of patients.

He is known for his patient-centric approach, emphasising the importance of understanding each patient's unique needs and medical history. His expertise encompasses a wide range of conditions related to plastic and cosmetic surgery, enabling him to deliver precise diagnoses and treatments. He ensures that patients receive care tailored to their individual health requirements.

In his practice, Dr Naveen Rao goes beyond treatments by educating patients about their conditions and expected outcomes. He is one of the few cosmetic surgeons to use special software and computer assistance to discuss the likely surgical outcome with patients before the procedure. This empowers individuals to take active roles in managing their care and making informed decisions. By fostering a supportive and understanding environment, he builds trust and ensures long-term health management strategies for patients.

Dedicated to staying updated with advancements in his field, Dr Naveen Rao actively collaborates with other healthcare professionals to provide comprehensive care. His commitment to improving health outcomes continues to leave a lasting impact on the lives of his patients, solidifying his reputation as a trusted name in Plastic and Cosmetic Surgery.
