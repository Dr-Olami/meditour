## About the Doctor

Dr. Manohar T is a distinguished Urologist based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, with an impressive 24 years of extensive experience in the field of urology. His qualifications include an MBBS, MS, DNB in Surgery, DNB in Urology, and a fellowship in Endourology & Laparoscopy, along with being a Robotic Surgery Fellow. Dr. Manohar is known for his expert skills in various advanced surgical techniques, including laser, laparoscopic, and robotic surgeries. As the Chief Urologist and Director of the Institute of Renal Sciences (AIRS), he has made significant contributions to urology, performing over 17,000 surgeries for kidney stones, 30,004 laser prostate treatments, 500 kidney transplants, and 89,004 cancer-related surgeries. His dedication to innovative patient care is reflected in his ability to address complex cases, including pediatric and female health issues. With a remarkable portfolio, Dr. Manohar has published over 250 articles and presented his findings at numerous national and international conferences. He has received various prestigious awards, including the Aryabhata International Award in 2011, Best Urologist in Bengaluru accolade from Times media in 2016, and the Karnataka Media Research Award in 2017. As a notable figure in the urological community, he has served as an invited faculty, panelist, and expert reviewer for eminent journals and has demonstrated advanced laparoscopic techniques at international meetings. Dr. Manohar T's expertise in laser applications has earned him recognition as a key opinion leader globally in urology. He is fluent in English, Hindi, and Kannada, ensuring that he can communicate effectively with a diverse patient population. Patients seeking cutting-edge care from a highly skilled and compassionate urologist will find Dr. Manohar T to be an exceptional choice.
