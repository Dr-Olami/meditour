## About the Doctor

As a renowned Senior Consultant in Adult Cardiac Surgery, Dr. Ravindra Setty B R brings a plethora of experience and knowledge to the table. His broad skill set is evident in many aspects of Adult Heart Surgeries, with an emphasis on intricate techniques. All Adult Cardiac Surgeries, including valve replacements and coronary artery bypass surgery, are within Dr. Setty's area of competence. He is skilled in doing complex redo valve procedures, mitral valve repairs, and total anomalous pulmonary venous connection (TAPVC) repairs. Notably, he has a high degree of expertise and precision and specializes in difficult coronary artery surgery.

Dr. Setty is passionate about upgrading this field and has a particular interest in difficult valve replacements and sophisticated coronary artery surgeries. His dedication to expanding the frontiers of cardiac surgery and enhancing patient outcomes using state-of-the-art techniques is demonstrated by these areas of focus.

Dr. Ravindra Setty is distinguished by his uncommon and special abilities, especially his love of academics in the field of Cardiothoracic Surgery. His devotion to lifelong learning and knowledge sharing is demonstrated by his dedication to academics, which has greatly advanced the field.
