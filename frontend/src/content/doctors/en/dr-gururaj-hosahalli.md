## About the Doctor

Dr. Gururaj Hosahalli is a skilled and experienced Plastic, Reconstructive and Aesthetic Surgeon at Sparsh Hospital, Bangalore, with expertise spanning reconstructive microsurgery, trauma reconstruction, diabetic foot care, hand surgery, limb salvage, and aesthetic procedures. With over 25 years of clinical experience, he is known for delivering meticulous surgical care with a strong focus on functional and aesthetic outcomes.

He completed his MBBS from JSS Medical College, followed by postgraduate qualifications in ENT (DLO), General Surgery (DNB), and advanced training in Plastic Surgery (DNB) from the prestigious Ganga Hospital, Coimbatore, one of India's leading centers for reconstructive and microsurgery.

Dr. Gururaj has previously served at PES Medical College (PESIMSR), Apollo Hospitals, Valluvanad Hospital, and The Bangalore Hospital, gaining extensive experience in managing complex trauma, reconstructive challenges, and general surgical emergencies.
