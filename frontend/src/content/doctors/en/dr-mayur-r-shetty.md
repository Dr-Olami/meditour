## About the Doctor

Dr. Mayur R. Shetty is a well-known plastic and cosmetic surgeon in Bangalore with extensive training and experience spanning over 10 years in performing aesthetic and reconstructive procedures. He is practising at Manipal Hospital Old Airport Road, Bangalore as a Consultant – Plastic, Cosmetic & Reconstructive Surgery. Dr. Shetty has honed his expertise in various areas of plastic surgery, ranging from Body Contouring and Facial Rejuvenation to Microvascular Reconstruction and Hand Surgery. He is recognised as one of the best plastic surgeons in Bangalore.

On the academic side, Dr. Mayur R. Shetty has completed an MBBS from Kasturba Medical College, Mangalore, followed by an M.S. in General Surgery from KIMS, Bangalore. The doctor then went on to pursue an M.Ch., specializing in Plastic Surgery from St. John's Medical College & Hospital, Bangalore. He has also completed Fellowship training in Cosmetic Surgery from Cocoona, Centre for Aesthetic Excellence – Dubai and RG Aesthetics – Delhi.

Dr. Mayur R. Shetty's skill set includes a wide range of cosmetic and aesthetic procedures, such as Rhinoplasty, Liposuction, Breast Augmentation, Facelifts, and Abdominoplasty, among others. He is proficient in Microvascular Surgery, with expertise in Extremity Replantation, Free Tissue Reconstruction, and Post-Onco-Resection Defect Reconstruction. Dr. Shetty's proficiency extends to Maxillofacial Trauma, Burns, and Post-Burn Deformities, showcasing his approach to holistic patient care.

Committed to delivering optimal results, Dr. Mayur R. Shetty prioritizes patient care and realism, ensuring that each patient receives personalized treatment tailored to their unique needs and desires. He emphasizes collaboration and teamwork, recognizing the importance of interdisciplinary cooperation in achieving the best outcomes for his patients.

Dr. Mayur Shetty is actively involved in research and publications. He is affiliated with esteemed professional organizations such as the Indian Association of Aesthetic Plastic Surgeons (IAAPS), the International Society of Aesthetic Plastic Surgeons (ISAPS), and the Karnataka Association of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgeons (KAPRAS). This shows his commitment to upholding the highest standards of ethical and professional excellence, establishing him as one of the best plastic and cosmetic surgeons in Bangalore.
