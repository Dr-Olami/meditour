Dr. Nithin Kumar is a highly experienced specialist and trusted neurologist in Yeswanthpur, with over two decades of experience managing complex neurological disorders. He completed his MBBS from Mysore Medical College, MD in General Medicine from KIMS, Hubli, and DM in Neurology from Bombay Hospital and Institute of Medical Sciences.

Patients searching for a reliable neurologist in Yeswanthpur value his patient-focused approach and expertise in epilepsy, stroke, movement disorders, headaches, and neuropathies.
