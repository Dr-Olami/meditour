## About the Doctor

Dr. Mahesh Rajashekaraiah is a senior Haematologist in Bangalore and Bone Marrow Transplant physician in Yeswanthpur and Hennur Road, known for ethical, patient-focused care in complex blood disorders.

He holds an MD in Internal Medicine and DM in Clinical Haematology from KEM Hospital, Mumbai, with advanced fellowship training in Leukaemia and Bone Marrow Transplantation from Vancouver General Hospital and BC Cancer Agency, Canada. He has been involved in over 500 bone marrow transplants and treats conditions such as thalassemia, aplastic anaemia, leukaemia, lymphoma, and multiple myeloma, with strong expertise in stem cell transplantation and bleeding disorders.
