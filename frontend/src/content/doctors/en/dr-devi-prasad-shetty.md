## About the Doctor

Dr. Devi Prasad Shetty stands as a visionary force who has not only reshaped healthcare in India but has also transformed the landscape of cardiac surgery. As the Founder, Chairman, and Senior Consultant Cardiac Surgeon at Narayana Health, his strong commitment has propelled the institution to become a symbol of affordable, high-quality healthcare. With a career marked by remarkable dedication, Dr. Shetty has not merely practiced medicine; he has established a legacy of compassion, innovation, and accessibility in the field of heart care. This legacy is now seamlessly integrated, offering comprehensive healthcare solutions through hospitals, clinics, labs, diagnostics, pharmacies and insurance — a testament to his holistic approach to medical services. Dr. Shetty's pioneering efforts have not only elevated Narayana Health but have also set new standards for integrated care in the healthcare industry.

## Education & Training

- MBBS — Kasturba Medical College, Mangalore (1979)
- MS (General Surgery) — Kasturba Medical College, Mangalore (1982)
- FRCS — Royal College of Surgeons, England
- Trained as a cardiothoracic surgeon under the National Health Service, UK
- Professor of International Health at the University of Minnesota Medical School, USA
- Professor at Rajeev Gandhi University of Health Sciences, Bangalore, India

## Positions Held

- Senior Consultant Cardiac Surgeon at B M Birla Heart Research Centre, Kolkata (1989–1996)
- Senior Consultant Cardiac Surgeon at Manipal Heart Foundation, Bangalore (1996–2001)
- Founded Narayana Health (formerly Narayana Hrudayalaya) in Bangalore in 2000
