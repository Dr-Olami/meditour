## About the Doctor

Dr. Prashant Puranik is a highly experienced plastic and reconstructive surgeon in Old Airport Road and Kanakapura Road, Bengaluru, with over 18 years of specialised experience in reconstructive, microsurgical, and aesthetic procedures. He is currently practising as Lead Consultant – Reconstructive Oncology & Microsurgery in the Department of Plastic, Cosmetic & Reconstructive Surgery at Manipal Hospital, Old Airport Road and Manipal Hospital, Kanakapura Road. With expertise in complex reconstructive surgery and aesthetic procedures, he is recognised as one of the best plastic surgeons in Old Airport Road for advanced reconstructive and cosmetic surgical care.

Dr. Puranik completed his MBBS, followed by an MS in General Surgery, and later pursued training in plastic surgery with M.Ch and DNB in Plastic Surgery. To further advance and refine his subspecialty expertise, he completed several prestigious fellowships, including a Fellowship in Reconstructive Oncology from Tata Memorial Hospital, Mumbai. He has also undertaken international fellowships and training in breast surgery in Chelmsford, UK, supermicrosurgery in Tokyo, Japan, and advanced microsurgical reconstruction training in Taiwan. These advanced global training experiences have helped him develop strong expertise in complex reconstructive techniques and microsurgery.

Over the course of his career, Dr. Puranik has successfully performed more than 5,000 surgical procedures. His clinical expertise includes reconstructive oncology, microsurgical reconstruction, aesthetic and reconstructive breast surgery, facial plastic surgery, and body contouring procedures. He is particularly skilled in complex reconstructive surgeries for cancer patients, including head and neck reconstruction, breast reconstruction, and limb salvage procedures using advanced microsurgical techniques.

Dr. Puranik also specialises in procedures such as lymphoedema surgery, facial palsy surgery, aesthetic surgery, and complex reconstructive surgeries following trauma. His approach focuses on restoring both function and aesthetics, helping patients regain confidence and improve their quality of life. By combining meticulous surgical precision with an artistic understanding of form and symmetry, he ensures natural and functional outcomes in both reconstructive and cosmetic procedures.

In addition to his clinical practice, Dr. Puranik is actively involved in academic research and surgical education. He has authored chapters in leading medical textbooks related to reconstructive surgery and has published research in reputed medical journals. He frequently participates as faculty and speaker at national and international conferences on plastic surgery and reconstructive microsurgery.

Dr. Puranik is a member of several professional organisations, including the Association of Plastic Surgeons of India, Indian Association of Aesthetic Plastic Surgeons, Breast Reconstruction and Aesthetic Surgeons Association, Indian Society of Reconstructive Microsurgeons, and the Karnataka Association of Plastic, Reconstructive and Aesthetic Surgeons. His academic contributions have also been recognised through awards such as the Kilner Essay Award at APSICON.

Fluent in Kannada, English, Hindi, and Marathi, Dr. Prashant Puranik focuses on clear communication and patient-centred care throughout the surgical journey. With his extensive experience, advanced training in microsurgery, and commitment to achieving functional and aesthetic excellence, he continues to be regarded as one of the best plastic surgeons in Manipal Old Airport Road and Kanakapura Road for complex reconstructive and aesthetic surgical procedures.
