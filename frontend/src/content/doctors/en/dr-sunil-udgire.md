## About the Doctor

Dr. Sunil Udgire is a highly experienced Haematologist, Paediatric Oncologist, and Bone Marrow Transplant (BMT) Physician with over 15 years of expertise in diagnosing and treating complex blood disorders, blood cancers, paediatric cancers, and immune disorders. He currently serves as a Senior Consultant in Haematology, Paediatric Oncology, Bone Marrow Transplantation, and Cellular Therapy at SPARSH Hospital, Hennur Road, Bangalore.

With extensive experience in advanced stem cell transplantation and cellular therapies, including CAR-T Cell Therapy, Dr. Udgire has played a pivotal role in developing successful Bone Marrow Transplant programs at leading healthcare institutions. He has been involved in more than 500 Bone Marrow Transplants, including matched sibling donor (MSD), matched unrelated donor (MUD), haploidentical, and autologous transplants.

Known for his evidence-based and patient-centric approach, Dr. Udgire focuses on delivering personalised treatment plans that combine the latest medical advances with compassionate care.
