## About the Doctor

Dr. Vishwanath S is the best nephrologist in Bangalore, Old Airport Road, currently practising at Manipal Hospital Old Airport Road as a HOD & Consultant - Nephrology and Transplant Physician. He holds more than 29 years of experience and has worked for 17 years as a specialist in Nephrology and is proficient in managing kidney problems in both adults and children, establishing him as the best nephrologist and transplant physician in Bangalore.

With a special interest in Clinical Nephrology, Dr. Vishwanath's extensive expertise in kidney care includes a large subset of renal diseases like Acute and Chronic Kidney Disease, Acute Renal Failure, Polycystic Kidney Disease, and more. He has vast experience in areas related to renal procedures, such as Renal Transplant, Swap Transplant, Paired Exchange Transplant, Dual Transplants (Kidney-Pancreas and Kidney-Liver), Hemodialysis, Peritoneal Dialysis (CAPD - Continuous ambulatory peritoneal dialysis and CCPD - Continuous cyclic peritoneal dialysis), Critical Care, Central Line Placement, and Catheter Placement.

From the academic front, Dr. Vishwanath S entered the field of medicine with an MBBS degree in 1995, followed by a DNB - Paediatrics and a DNB - Nephrology. He was awarded the prestigious Dr. HL Trivedi Gold Medal in DNB - Nephrology from the National Board of Examinations for securing the 1st rank at the all-India DNB Nephrology Examination. Dr. Vishwanath also has a Dr. PS Aniker, D.CH Gold Medal of Mysore University to his name for his MBBS Degree examination. He holds memberships at various organizations such as Member of National Academy of Medical Sciences (MNAMS), a Life Member of the Indian Academy of Paediatrics, Member of Indian Society of Nephrology, and Member of Indian Society of Transplantation.

Besides his clinical duties, Dr. Vishwanath is very active in medical literature and has authored multiple publications and distributions of journals at the national and international levels. His literature addressed topics such as "Post Cov-19 kidney complications", "The psychological aspect of organ donation in the current scenario", "Busting myths and misconceptions over organ donation: A doctor speaks", and more. With his role as the HOD of the Nephrology Department at Manipal Hospital Old Airport Road, Dr. Vishwanath aims to offer patients with renal problems the best of medical care that is at par with international standards.
