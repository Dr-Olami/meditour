## About the Doctor

Dr. Mahadev Swamy B is a highly accomplished and distinguished Senior Cardiologist with over 13 years of rich experience in the field of Interventional Cardiology. Trained in premier institutions across the country, he is a Gold medalist in DM Cardiology from the prestigious Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education and Research (JIPMER, Puducherry). He is also a Fellow of the American College of Cardiology (ACC) and the Society for Cardiovascular Angiography and Interventions (SCAI), USA.

Dr. Mahadev is highly skilled in performing primary angioplasty, complex coronary interventions, implantation of pacemakers and implantable cardioverter-defibrillators (ICD), and advanced procedures like Trans-catheter Aortic Valve Implantation/Replacement (TAVI/TAVR). With his vast experience and commitment, he has performed over 10,000 cardiac catheterisation procedures including more than 2,000 major cardiac interventions with the highest success rate.

As a top cardiologist in Bangalore, Dr. Mahadev believes in delivering patient-centric care, combining the latest cutting-edge technology with compassion. His expertise in cardiac imaging techniques, including IVUS (Intravascular Ultrasound) and Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT), allows for precise treatment to deliver exceptional patient outcomes and minimally invasive treatment strategies.

He has held significant roles, including Assistant Professor of Cardiology at Sri Jayadeva Institute of Cardiovascular Sciences & Research, Bangalore, and also rendered his service at the renowned Fortis Hospital, Cunningham Road, Bangalore. Dr. Mahadev's commitment to excellence in cardiac care, combined with his extensive experience and brilliant academics, makes him a leading choice for patients seeking top quality cardiac care in Bangalore.
