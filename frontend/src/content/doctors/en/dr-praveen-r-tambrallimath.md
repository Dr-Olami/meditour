## About the Doctor

Dr. Praveen R Tambrallimath is a highly skilled and experienced Senior Consultant Cardiac Surgeon (Adult & Paediatric) at Narayana Institute of Cardiac Sciences, Bangalore. He has made significant contributions to the field of cardiovascular medicine. With a focus on innovation and excellence, Dr. Tambrallimath has played pivotal roles in renowned medical institutions. He has established a presence in the digital world with educational cardiac surgery videos on his YouTube channel, contributing to the dissemination of knowledge and advancements in the field.

In collaboration with CTSNET, Dr. Tambrallimath has furthered the reach of his expertise through published videos, solidifying his commitment to advancing the understanding and practice of cardiac surgery.
