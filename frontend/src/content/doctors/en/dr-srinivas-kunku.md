## About the Doctor

With over 15 years of clinical excellence, Dr. Srinivas Kunku is a highly experienced and compassionate ENT Surgeon based at SPARSH Hospital, Hennur Road, Bangalore. A graduate of Devaraj Urs Medical College (2004), Dr. Kunku completed his post-graduate training at Jawaharlal Nehru Medical College, Belgaum, where he was recognised as the Best Outgoing Post-graduate.

His commitment to providing high-quality, patient-centric care has driven him to train and work with renowned ENT specialists across the globe, including Dr. Ashish Bhumkar, Dr. Satish Jain, and Dr. Anil D Cruz. Dr. Kunku's expertise spans the full spectrum of ENT care, and he is known for his precise surgical skills, thorough diagnostic approach, and compassionate patient interaction.
