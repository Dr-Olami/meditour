## About the Doctor

Dr. S. Vidyadhara is a globally acclaimed pioneer in advanced spinal reconstructions, minimally invasive keyhole operations, and computer-navigated robotic therapies. He currently serves as the Chairman & HOD – Spine Surgery, Consultant – Robotic Spine Surgeon, and Co-Chairman – Medical Advisory Board at Manipal Hospital Old Airport Road, Bengaluru. Widely respected for his expertise in complex spine surgery and robotic-assisted spinal procedures, he has dedicated his career to treating a broad spectrum of spinal disorders using both advanced surgical and non-surgical techniques. With internationally acclaimed training, pioneering research, and numerous global leadership positions, Dr. Vidyadhara is widely regarded as the best spine surgeon in Bangalore.

Dr. Vidyadhara completed his MBBS, MS, DNB, FNB in Spine Surgery, and Fellowship in Spine Surgery (FHKU) from the University of Hong Kong.

At Manipal Hospitals, Dr. Vidyadhara specialises in robotic spine surgery, minimally invasive spine surgery, microscopic and endoscopic spinal procedures, artificial disc replacement, complex cervical spine surgery, scoliosis correction, spinal deformity correction, vertebroplasty and kyphoplasty, spinal tumour surgery, spinal reconstruction, and advanced lumbar fusion procedures including PLIF and TLIF. His expertise also extends to comprehensive non-operative management of neck and back pain, helping patients achieve optimal outcomes through personalised treatment plans that combine advanced technology with evidence-based care.

Beyond his clinical excellence, Dr. Vidyadhara has made exceptional contributions to spine research and innovation. His work has been published extensively in internationally renowned journals, including Spine, Journal of Spinal Disorders and Techniques, and Journal of Orthopaedic Surgery, with several of his studies receiving international recognition and citations. Widely regarded as one of the best spine surgeons in Bangalore, he is the recipient of numerous prestigious honours, including the International Society for the Study of Lumbar Spine (ISSLS) Best Research Award, Hong Kong Orthopaedic Association Most Promising Spine Research Award, ISCA Young Scientist Award presented by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, the Lester Lowe SICOT Award, and the Scoliosis Research Society Travelling Fellowship, further establishing his leadership in the global spine community.

Fluent in English, Kannada, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Tulu, and Konkani, Dr. Vidyadhara is known for his compassionate patient care, meticulous surgical planning, and commitment to restoring spinal function and quality of life. His combination of robotic surgical expertise, international recognition, groundbreaking research, and patient-centred care has earned him a reputation as the best spine surgeon in Bangalore, trusted for managing everything from routine spinal disorders to the most complex spinal deformities and reconstructive procedures.
