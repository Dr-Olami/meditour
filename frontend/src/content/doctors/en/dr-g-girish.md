## About the Doctor

Dr. G. Girish is one of Bengaluru's leading Surgical Oncologists, dedicated to cancer care since 2010. After completing his super-specialisation in Surgical Oncology from the prestigious Tata Memorial Centre, Homi Bhabha National Institute, Mumbai — India's premier cancer institute, he has consistently delivered high quality, evidence-based care with a deep sense of compassion and commitment.

He is known for his patient-centric approach, combining his up-to-date oncological knowledge with skilled surgical expertise. His passion for surgery and continuous pursuit of excellence ensure that patients receive the most appropriate and effective cancer treatment. He believes in a holistic approach to cancer care, focusing not just on treating the disease but also on supporting the patient through their journey.
