## About the Doctor

Dr. Sharon D'Souza is a highly skilled Ophthalmologist with over 15 years of expertise, specializing in Cornea, Cataract and Refractive surgery. She holds an MBBS and MS in Ophthalmology, along with a Fellowship in Cornea & Refractive Surgery and a PhD in Ocular Immunology, reflecting her deep commitment to clinical excellence and academic advancement in eye care.

Based at Apollo Hospitals, Bannerghatta Road, Bangalore, Dr. D'Souza offers comprehensive ophthalmic services from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Her extensive training and experience equip her to manage a wide range of eye conditions, from common vision problems to complex corneal diseases, with precision and compassion.

Fluent in English, Hindi, Kannada, Bengali, Konkani, she effectively connects with patients from diverse linguistic and cultural backgrounds, ensuring that every individual receives clear communication and personalized care.

Her clinical expertise spans advanced corneal transplants, refractive procedures such as LASIK, PRK, and phakic IOLs, as well as the diagnosis and treatment of ocular surface disorders and infections. Dr. D'Souza's patient-centered approach emphasizes not only restoring vision but also addressing underlying ocular health concerns through evidence-based, holistic strategies.

Passionate about advancing ophthalmic care, she remains actively involved in research, postgraduate training, and surgical mentorship. Her dedication to both clinical excellence and patient education makes her a trusted and respected figure in the field of ophthalmology.
