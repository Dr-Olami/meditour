## About the Doctor

Dr. Jyoti Shetty is an ophthalmologist who practises in Bangalore, India and has years of expertise and training. She has advanced competence and years of training in various areas of ophthalmology, making her one of the most renowned and in-demand eye doctors in the area. Dr Shetty is a prestigious member of some of the prominent institutions, including the All India Ophthalmological Society, the Bangalore and Karnataka Ophthalmological Societies, the American Academy of Ophthalmology, the European Society of Cataract and Refractive Surgery, and several other distinguished associations. In addition to refractive surgery, LASIK laser systems, Phakic ICL, and paediatric glaucoma, her fellowship training also covers the anterior segment, cornea, and glaucoma.

Dr. Jyoti Shetty can conduct a variety of ophthalmic treatments that cover refractive surgery, cataract surgery, pediatric cataract surgery, advanced glaucoma surgery, and squint surgery, thanks to her broad spectrum of skills. Dr Jyoti Shetty's knowledge and contributions to ophthalmology extend beyond her work as a clinician. She has also published several papers in regional and international journals to further the field and give her patients the most cutting-edge care. Additionally, she has spoken and presented at various popular conferences and seminars to share her knowledge and experience with her fellow doctors and other medical specialists seeking knowledge. Dr. Jyoti Shetty is one of the best ophthalmologists in Bangalore.

Dr Jyoti Shetty is renowned for her compassionate and patient-focused style of medicine. She gets to know her patients' wants and needs, ensuring they are informed and at ease during their therapy. She can provide care to various patients with her fluency in English, Hindi, Kannada, and Tulu. Dr Jyoti Shetty has earned several honours and recognitions throughout her career and her memberships and fellowships in professional organisations. She has won the Best Outgoing Fellow Award and became a National Talent Search Scholar. Her inclusion in the Ophthalmology Times as an "Accomplished Ophthalmologist from Southern India" is evidence of her passion and commitment to her field — domestically and internationally publications in journals.

Dr Jyoti Shetty is a highly qualified and sympathetic ophthalmologist with in-depth knowledge in several fields of ophthalmology. She is among the most renowned and in-demand eye surgeons in Bangalore and the surrounding areas thanks to her fellowship, memberships, accolades and accomplishments, publications and lectures, and patient-centred approach.

## Field of Expertise

- Cataract Surgery with all types of Foldable Intra Ocular Lens Implantations under topical Anaesthesia (Aspheric, Toric, Multifocal, Multifocal Toric)
- Complicated Cataract surgeries needing Scleral Fixation of IOL, usage of rings
- Refractive surgeries — Advanced surface ablation (PRK), Microkeratome LASIK, Femto LASIK, Collagen cross-linking, Phakic ICLs
- Advanced Glaucoma surgeries — Trabeculotomy, Trabeculectomy with releasable sutures, Ologen, Glaucoma drainage devices (AVG)
- Complicated Traumatic Corneal, Corneo-Scleral tear repairs, reconstruction following Irido dialysis
- Paediatric Cataracts
- Squint Surgeries
