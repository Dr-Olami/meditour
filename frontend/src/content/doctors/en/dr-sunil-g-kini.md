## About the Doctor

Dr. Sunil G Kini is a highly accomplished orthopaedic surgeon and robotic joint replacement specialist currently serving as HOD & Consultant – Orthopaedic & Robotic Joint Replacement Surgery at Manipal Hospital Old Airport Road, Bengaluru. With more than 22 years of overall experience, including over 15 years as a specialist, he has performed over 12,000 orthopaedic surgeries, including more than 5,000 joint replacement procedures and 3,000 arthroscopic (keyhole) surgeries. Renowned for his expertise in robotic hip and knee replacement, sports injuries, and complex joint reconstruction, Dr. Sunil G Kini is widely regarded as one of the best orthopaedic surgeons in Bangalore.

Dr. Sunil G Kini completed his MBBS from B.M. Patil Medical College, followed by an MS in Orthopaedics from Bangalore Medical College and Research Institute. He subsequently earned his DNB from the National Board of Examinations and dual MRCS qualifications from the Royal Colleges of Surgeons of Glasgow and Edinburgh. He further advanced his expertise with an M.Ch, MNAMS, and a Diploma from SICOT (Belgium). To refine his skills in joint replacement and adult reconstruction, he completed prestigious international fellowships in London, Sydney, Germany, and Singapore, gaining extensive exposure to advanced orthopaedic techniques and robotic surgery.

At Manipal Hospital, Dr. Sunil G Kini specialises in robotic and conventional hip and knee replacement, revision joint replacement surgery, shoulder and elbow replacement, hip and knee arthroscopy, ligament reconstruction, cartilage restoration procedures, sports injury management, and complex trauma surgery. He is among the best orthopaedic surgeons in Bangalore and top orthopaedic surgeons in Karnataka with expertise in hip arthroscopy and robotic hip replacement, offering advanced minimally invasive solutions that enhance surgical precision, reduce recovery time, and improve long-term functional outcomes.

In addition to his clinical practice, Dr. Sunil G Kini has made significant contributions to orthopaedic education and research. He serves as a mentor and core faculty member for training orthopaedic surgeons in arthroscopy and joint replacement techniques. He has authored more than 30 publications in internationally indexed journals, presented over 50 scientific papers at international conferences, and serves as a reviewer for leading journals including the Bone and Joint Journal and the International Orthopaedics Journal. His contributions have earned him several prestigious recognitions, including the LESTER-LOWE Young Surgeon SICOT Award, the SICOT German Travelling Fellowship, and the IOA-Thailand Ambassadorship Award.

Fluent in English, Hindi, Kannada, Konkani, Bengali, Telugu, and Tamil, Dr. Sunil G Kini combines advanced surgical expertise with compassionate patient care. His commitment to innovation, research, surgeon training, and personalised treatment has established him as one of the best orthopaedic surgeons in Bangalore, trusted for comprehensive joint replacement, sports medicine, and complex orthopaedic care.
