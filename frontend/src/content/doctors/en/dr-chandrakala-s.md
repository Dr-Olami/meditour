## About the Doctor

Dr. Chandrakala S is a senior haematologist in Hennur Road, Bangalore, with over 20 years of experience in diagnosing and managing complex blood disorders. She is widely recognised as one of the best haematologists in Bangalore for ethical, evidence-based care.

She treats a wide range of benign and malignant haematological conditions, including anaemia, inherited blood disorders, blood cancers, and bone marrow failure syndromes, and is actively involved in bone marrow transplantation and long-term follow-up care. Known for her clear communication and patient-focused approach, Dr. Chandrakala ensures structured treatment planning and informed decision-making.
