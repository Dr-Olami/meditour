## About the Doctor

Dr. Deepak Dubey is a distinguished urologist and robotic surgeon currently serving as HOD & Consultant – Uro-Oncology, Robotic Surgery and Director of Renal Transplantation Services (South Region) at Manipal Hospital Old Airport Road, Bengaluru. Internationally recognised for his expertise in advanced urological surgery and kidney transplantation, Dr. Dubey has built an outstanding reputation for delivering complex surgical care using minimally invasive and robotic techniques. His extensive clinical expertise and leadership in renal transplantation have established him as one of the best urologists in Bangalore.

Dr. Deepak Dubey completed his MBBS and MS in General Surgery from the prestigious JIPMER, Puducherry, before earning the FRCS from the Royal College of Surgeons of Glasgow, UK. He further specialised by obtaining his DNB and MCh in Urology from Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences (SGPGIMS), Lucknow, followed by the prestigious FRCS (Urology) from the Intercollegiate Speciality Board of the Royal College of Surgeons, UK. His comprehensive national and international training has equipped him with advanced expertise in robotic urological surgery, reconstructive urology, uro-oncology, and renal transplantation.

At Manipal Hospital Old Airport Road, Dr. Deepak Dubey specialises in robotic urological surgery, uro-oncology, kidney transplantation, laparoscopic urology, reconstructive urology, and female urology. He has extensive experience in performing complex robotic procedures for kidney, bladder, and prostate cancers, advanced laparoscopic surgeries, reconstructive urinary tract procedures, and renal transplant surgeries. His patient-centred approach combines precision surgical techniques with evidence-based treatment protocols to deliver optimal outcomes while minimising recovery time and improving quality of life. His expertise in advanced urological procedures and minimally invasive techniques has established him as one of the best urologists in Bangalore.

Beyond his clinical practice, Dr. Deepak Dubey has made significant contributions to urological research, education, and innovation. He serves as a reviewer for leading international journals, including the Journal of Urology, Urology (Gold Journal), Indian Journal of Urology, and the International Brazilian Journal of Urology. He has also contributed extensively to academic discussions on robotic surgery, kidney transplantation, urinary tract infections, and advances in urological care through numerous publications, conference presentations, and expert media interactions. His active memberships in the European Association of Urology, Urological Society of India, and American Urological Association reflect his commitment to advancing global standards in urology.

Recognising the immense emotional and physiological stress connected to chronic kidney failure and multi-organ malignancies, Dr. Dubey emphasises a deeply compassionate, patient-centred care environment. Fluent in English, Hindi, Kannada, and Tamil, Dr. Deepak Dubey believes in providing compassionate, transparent, and personalised care to every patient. His international qualifications, leadership in robotic urology and renal transplantation, and dedication to surgical excellence continue to make him one of the best urologists in Bangalore.
