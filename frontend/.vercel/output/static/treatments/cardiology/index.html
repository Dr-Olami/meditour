  • Hypertension — advanced management of resistant high blood pressure

Procedures & Interventions

Coronary Angiogram

A diagnostic procedure using X-ray imaging and contrast dye to visualise coronary arteries. Performed via radial or femoral access, it identifies blockages and guides treatment decisions. Typically takes 30-45 minutes with same-day discharge.

Angioplasty with Stent (PCI)

Minimally invasive procedure to open blocked arteries using a balloon catheter, followed by placement of a drug-eluting stent. Performed in advanced cath labs with intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) and fractional flow reserve (FFR) guidance. Hospital stay is typically 1-2 days.

Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)

Open-heart surgery that creates new routes for blood flow around blocked arteries using grafts from the patient’s own blood vessels. Performed with cardiopulmonary bypass or off-pump (beating heart) technique. Hospital stay is 5-7 days with full recovery in 4-6 weeks.

Valve Repair & Replacement

Surgical or minimally invasive procedures to repair or replace damaged heart valves (mitral, aortic, tricuspid). Options include mechanical valves, bioprosthetic valves and transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR) for high-risk patients.

Pacemaker & ICD Implantation

Device implantation for arrhythmia management — pacemakers for slow heart rates and implantable cardioverter-defibrillators (ICDs) for life-threatening arrhythmias. Cardiac resynchronisation therapy (CRT) for heart failure patients.

Advanced Technology

  • Advanced catheterisation laboratories with biplane imaging and rotational atherectomy capability
  • Intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) and optical coherence tomography (OCT) for precise stent placement
  • Modular cardiac operating theatres with laminar airflow and intra-operative monitoring
  • 3 Tesla MRI and 128-slice CT for non-invasive cardiac imaging
  • Electrophysiology lab with 3D mapping for complex ablation procedures
  • Dedicated CCU with continuous haemodynamic monitoring
  • ECMO (extracorporeal membrane oxygenation) for critical cardiac support

Is minimally invasive heart surgery available?
Yes, minimally invasive cardiac surgery is available including minimally invasive CABG, minimally invasive valve surgery, and transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR). Can I get a same-day angiogram and angioplasty?
Yes, in many cases a diagnostic coronary angiogram and angioplasty (PCI) can be performed in the same session if a treatable blockage is identified. This is determined by the cardiologist based on angiogram findings, patient condition, and clinical guidelines. The procedure is performed in advanced cath labs with IVUS and FFR guidance.
