About this treatment

Overview

Summary: Cancer treatment in Bangalore follows a multidisciplinary tumour-board approach offering chemotherapy, radiation therapy (IMRT/IGRT/SBRT), surgical oncology, immunotherapy, targeted therapy and bone marrow transplant at JCI-accredited facilities. Costs range from $3,000 to $15,000 — 60-80% savings compared to Western countries — with access to the latest targeted therapies and personalised treatment plans based on molecular profiling.

Cancer treatment at our partner hospitals in Bangalore follows a multidisciplinary approach, bringing together medical oncologists, surgical oncologists, radiation oncologists and pathologists in dedicated tumour board meetings. Each patient receives a personalised treatment plan based on their cancer type, stage, molecular profile and overall health.

Our partner hospitals are equipped with linear accelerators (LINAC) for precision radiation therapy, PET-CT scanners for accurate staging, and dedicated medical oncology day-care units for chemotherapy. The cancer care teams are experienced in treating both common and rare cancers with excellent outcomes.

Conditions Treated

  • Breast cancer — lumpectomy, mastectomy, reconstruction and adjuvant therapy
  • Lung cancer — VATS lobectomy, SBRT and targeted therapy
  • Colorectal cancer — minimally invasive surgery and chemotherapy protocols
  • Prostate cancer — robotic prostatectomy and radiation therapy
  • Head & neck cancer — organ preservation surgery and IMRT
  • Cervical & ovarian cancer — surgical and chemoradiation approaches
  • Leukaemia & lymphoma — chemotherapy and bone marrow transplant
  • Gastrointestinal cancers — oesophageal, gastric, pancreatic and liver
  • Skin cancer — melanoma and non-melanoma management

Treatment Modalities

Chemotherapy

Administered in dedicated day-care oncology units with specialised nursing staff. Treatment protocols follow international guidelines (NCCN) with personalised dosing based on body surface area and molecular markers. Supportive care includes antiemetics, growth factors and cold cap therapy for hair preservation.

Radiation Therapy

  • IMRT (Intensity-Modulated Radiation Therapy) — precise dose shaping to spare healthy tissue
  • IGRT (Image-Guided Radiation Therapy) — daily imaging for accurate targeting
  • SBRT (Stereotactic Body Radiation Therapy) — high-dose focused treatment for small tumours
  • Brachytherapy — internal radiation for cervical, prostate and breast cancers

Surgical Oncology

Minimally invasive and robotic surgical options for faster recovery:

  • Robotic surgery for prostate, gynaecological and head & neck cancers
  • VATS (Video-Assisted Thoracoscopic Surgery) for lung cancer
  • Laparoscopic surgery for colorectal and GI cancers
  • Onco-plastic surgery for breast conservation and reconstruction

Immunotherapy & Targeted Therapy

  • Checkpoint inhibitors (PD-1/PD-L1) for melanoma, lung and bladder cancers
  • Targeted therapy with tyrosine kinase inhibitors and monoclonal antibodies
  • CAR-T cell therapy for certain haematological cancers
  • Hormone therapy for hormone receptor-positive breast and prostate cancers

Bone Marrow Transplant

Both autologous and allogeneic transplant programmes for leukaemia, lymphoma and multiple myeloma. Dedicated HEPA-filtered transplant units with specialised nursing and infection control protocols.

Advanced Technology

  • Linear accelerator (LINAC) with IMRT, IGRT and SBRT capabilities
  • PET-CT for whole-body cancer staging and treatment response assessment
  • 3 Tesla MRI for detailed soft tissue imaging
  • Robotic surgical systems for precision cancer surgery
  • NABL-accredited pathology with molecular testing and genetic profiling
  • Dedicated oncology day-care units for chemotherapy administration
  • HEPA-filtered bone marrow transplant units

What is a tumour board and how does it benefit me?
A tumour board is a multidisciplinary meeting where medical oncologists, surgical oncologists, radiation oncologists, pathologists, and radiologists review your case together. This ensures you receive a consensus-based, personalised treatment plan that considers all treatment modalities and the latest evidence. Every cancer patient at our partner hospitals is discussed in a tumour board.
