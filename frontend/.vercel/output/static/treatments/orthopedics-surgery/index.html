Procedures & Interventions

Total Knee Replacement (TKR)

Replacement of damaged knee joint surfaces with metal and polyethylene implants. Performed using computer-assisted navigation for precise alignment and implant positioning. Minimally invasive techniques reduce recovery time. Hospital stay is typically 3-5 days with full recovery in 6-12 weeks.

Total Hip Replacement (THR)

Replacement of the hip joint with ceramic-on-ceramic or metal-on-polyethylene implants. Options include anterior approach (muscle-sparing, faster recovery) and posterior approach. Hospital stay is 2-4 days with full recovery in 4-8 weeks.

ACL Reconstruction

Arthroscopic reconstruction of the anterior cruciate ligament using hamstring or patellar tendon grafts. Day-care or overnight stay with return to sports in 6-9 months. Includes comprehensive physiotherapy protocol.

Spine Surgery

  • Microdiscectomy — minimally invasive removal of herniated disc material
  • Spinal fusion — stabilisation of vertebral segments for degenerative or traumatic conditions
  • Disc replacement — artificial disc implantation for cervical and lumbar spine
  • Spinal decompression — relief of nerve compression in spinal stenosis

Arthroscopy

Minimally invasive keyhole surgery for joint problems — shoulder rotator cuff repair, meniscus repair, cartilage restoration and loose body removal. Typically day-care procedures with rapid recovery.

How soon can I walk after hip replacement surgery?
Most patients are encouraged to stand and walk with assistance within 24 hours of hip replacement surgery. With the anterior (muscle-sparing) approach, full weight-bearing is often permitted immediately. Independent walking typically resumes within 1-2 weeks, with full recovery in 4-8 weeks depending on the surgical approach and individual rehabilitation progress.

Total knee replacement at our partner hospitals has a success rate of over 95% with significant pain relief and functional improvement. Implant longevity is typically 15-20 years with proper care. Computer-navigated and robotic-assisted techniques improve alignment accuracy, which contributes to better long-term outcomes.
