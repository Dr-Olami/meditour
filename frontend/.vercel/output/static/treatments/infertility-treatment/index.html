Procedures & Interventions

IVF (In Vitro Fertilisation)

Eggs are retrieved from the ovaries and fertilised with sperm in the laboratory. Resulting embryos are cultured for 3-5 days and transferred to the uterus. Includes ovarian stimulation, egg retrieval, fertilisation, embryo culture and transfer. Success rates of 50-65% per cycle for women under 35.

ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)

Single sperm injection directly into the egg for cases of male factor infertility. Used when sperm count, motility or morphology is suboptimal. Combined with IVF protocol for maximum fertilisation success.

Egg Freezing (Oocyte Cryopreservation)

Preservation of eggs for future fertility — ideal for women undergoing cancer treatment, delaying childbearing for personal reasons, or with diminishing ovarian reserve. Vitrification technique ensures high survival rates upon thawing.

Embryo Freezing

Cryopreservation of excess embryos for future use. Allows multiple transfer attempts from a single stimulation cycle. Reduces cost and increases cumulative success rates.

Donor Programmes

  • Egg donation — for women with premature ovarian failure or poor egg quality
  • Sperm donation — for severe male factor infertility
  • Embryo donation — for couples needing both donor eggs and sperm
  • Surrogacy — gestational carrier programme (subject to legal regulations)

PGT (Preimplantation Genetic Testing)

  • PGT-A — aneuploidy screening for chromosomal abnormalities
  • PGT-M — testing for specific single gene disorders (e.g., thalassaemia, cystic fibrosis)
  • PGT-SR — screening for structural rearrangements

What is the success rate of IVF per cycle?
IVF success rates at our partner hospitals are 50-65% per cycle for women under 35, declining with age. Success depends on factors including female age, ovarian reserve, cause of infertility, and embryo quality. The programmes use time-lapse embryo monitoring and endometrial receptivity analysis (ERA) to optimise transfer timing and improve outcomes.
Is egg freezing available for cancer patients?
Yes, egg freezing (oocyte cryopreservation) is available for cancer patients facing fertility-threatening treatment. The fertility team coordinates with oncologists for expedited ovarian stimulation before chemotherapy or radiation. Vitrification technique ensures high survival rates upon thawing. Embryo freezing and ovarian tissue cryopreservation are also available.
Is preimplantation genetic testing (PGT) available?
Yes, preimplantation genetic testing is available including PGT-A for chromosomal aneuploidy screening, PGT-M for specific single gene disorders such as thalassaemia and cystic fibrosis, and PGT-SR for structural rearrangements. Genetic counselling is provided as part of the PGT programme to help patients understand testing options and results.
