Procedures & Interventions

Bone Marrow-Derived Stem Cell Therapy

Harvesting of stem cells from the patient’s own bone marrow (autologous), typically from the iliac crest. The cells are processed and concentrated in the laboratory before being administered to the target area. Used for orthopaedic, neurological and cardiac conditions.

Adipose-Derived Stem Cell Therapy

Extraction of stem cells from the patient’s own adipose (fat) tissue, typically from the abdominal area. Adipose tissue is rich in mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) and can be harvested in larger quantities than bone marrow. Used for cosmetic, orthopaedic and autoimmune applications.

Orthobiologic Injections

  • PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) — concentrated platelets for joint and tendon healing
  • BMAC (Bone Marrow Aspirate Concentrate) — for cartilage and bone regeneration
  • Fat grafting — for soft tissue augmentation and wound healing

Neurological Rehabilitation

Combined stem cell therapy with intensive neurological rehabilitation:

  • Physiotherapy for motor recovery
  • Occupational therapy for functional independence
  • Speech therapy for communication disorders
  • Cognitive rehabilitation for memory and executive function

Immune Modulation Therapy

Use of mesenchymal stem cells for their immunomodulatory properties in autoimmune conditions. MSCs can suppress abnormal immune responses and promote tissue repair. Used for multiple sclerosis, rheumatoid arthritis and other autoimmune disorders.

Advanced Technology

  • GMP-certified cell processing laboratory for stem cell isolation and expansion
  • Flow cytometry for cell characterisation and quality control
  • Closed-system cell harvesting devices for safe and sterile collection
  • Image-guided delivery for precise stem cell administration
  • Cryopreservation facilities for cell storage
  • Comprehensive safety monitoring with real-time cell viability assessment
