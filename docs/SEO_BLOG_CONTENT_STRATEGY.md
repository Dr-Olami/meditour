## How This Helps SEO / AEO / GEO / AIO

### SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
Long-tail keywords have lower competition and higher conversion intent than generic terms. A patient searching "cost of heart bypass surgery in India for Bangladeshi patients" is far closer to booking than someone searching "medical tourism." Each article below targets a specific long-tail phrase with:
- The exact phrase in the H1, `<title>`, meta description, and URL slug
- Natural variations in H2/H3 subheadings
- Internal links to relevant treatment, country, hospital, and doctor pages

### AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
AI engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot) extract answers from well-structured content. Each article should include:
- A **TL;DR / Summary block** at the top (2-3 sentences) — this is what AI engines cite
- **Direct Q&A pairs** formatted as "Question: ... Answer: ..." or as FAQ schema
- **Tables** for cost comparisons — AI engines love structured data
- **Step-by-step lists** for process content (visa, travel, recovery)

### GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
Generative engines prioritize content that demonstrates expertise, cites sources, and covers a topic comprehensively. Each article should:
- Reference real hospital names, doctor names, and accreditation status
- Include specific numbers (costs, success rates, processing times, flight durations)
- Cover the full patient journey (decision → research → visa → travel → treatment → recovery → follow-up)
- Link to primary sources (hospital websites, government visa portals)

### AIO (AI-Optimized Content)
AI search assistants recommend content that is:
- Structured with clear headings (H1 → H2 → H3 hierarchy)
- Written in natural conversational language (not keyword-stuffed)
- Updated regularly with current information
- Cross-linked internally so AI crawlers can trace related content

## Article Production Guidelines

### For every article:
1. **URL slug:** Use the primary keyword phrase, e.g. `/blog/cost-of-heart-bypass-surgery-in-india`
2. **H1:** Match the search query exactly (or very close)
3. **Meta description:** 150-160 chars, includes the primary keyword + a benefit
4. **TL;DR block:** 2-3 sentence summary immediately after the H1
5. **Cost tables:** Always include a cost comparison table (India vs. home country)
6. **FAQ section:** 5-8 questions with FAQPage JSON-LD schema
7. **Internal links:** Link to 2-3 relevant treatment pages, 1-2 country pages, 1 hospital page
8. **CTA:** WhatsApp + lead form at the bottom
9. **Bilingual:** Every article should be published in both EN and BN
10. **Word count:** 1,500-2,500 words (long enough to rank, short enough to read)

### Content guardrails:
- **Never invent medical claims.** Use only verified stats from hospital websites, government sources, or published research.
- **Never invent doctor names.** Reference only doctors in our content collection.
- **Never invent prices.** Use ranges from our treatment content frontmatter or cite hospital quotes.
- **Always disclose:** "Prices are indicative and vary by hospital, surgeon, and case complexity."
